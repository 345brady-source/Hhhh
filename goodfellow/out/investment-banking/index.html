Investment Banking

M&A, capital raising and advisory services

Goodfellow Capital Markets is an independent capital markets and investment banking firm. Our investment banking group advises middle-market companies, business owners and institutional investors on mergers and acquisitions, capital raising and restructuring.

Whether a client is selling a business, completing an acquisition, financing growth or reshaping its balance sheet, we provide senior-level advice and execution at each stage of a company’s development.

Services

Our capabilities

Mergers & Acquisitions

Advises business owners, companies and investors on the sale, acquisition and combination of businesses, from initial strategy and valuation through negotiation and closing.

  • Sell-side advisory
  • Buy-side advisory
  • Mergers and divestitures
  • Recapitalizations

Capital Raising

Arranges private placements of debt and equity securities for middle-market companies.

Debt Financing

Structures and places senior, unitranche, mezzanine and asset-based credit facilities.

Real Estate Capital

Helps property owners, developers and sponsors find capital for real estate projects, arranging debt and equity financing from banks, credit funds and private investors.

Loan Facilitation

Provides liquidity solutions for portfolios of loans, leases and receivables, connecting sellers with banks, credit funds and institutional buyers.

Restructuring and Advisory

Advises companies and their stakeholders on refinancings, recapitalizations and capital structure matters.
