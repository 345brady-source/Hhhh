Overview

Goodfellow Specialty Income Fund seeks to provide consistent, high monthly income through a fixed 10% preferred return paid monthly.

The Fund makes senior secured credit facilities to private credit managers, merchant cash advance providers and other specialty finance companies. Each facility is secured by a first-priority lien on short-duration commercial receivables, generally held in a bankruptcy-remote special purpose vehicle, and earns a fixed rate of at least 16% a year. Each facility also carries warrant coverage of at least 5% on the borrower’s equity, with 100% of warrant proceeds paid to investors.

The Fund is sponsored and managed by Goodfellow Asset Management, an affiliate of Goodfellow Capital Markets. The Manager charges no management or incentive fee; it is paid only from income above the investors’ preferred return, and only after any losses and selling costs have been restored.

Investment highlights

Fixed monthly income

A fixed 10% preferred return on unreturned capital, cumulative and paid monthly from net investment income.

Senior secured lending

Revolving and term lines secured by first-priority liens on receivables, with borrowing bases, fund-controlled collection accounts and monthly borrowing base certificates.

Manager takes the first loss

Income is retained to restore any loss of principal, and selling costs paid from capital, before Goodfellow is paid anything.

No management fee

No management, incentive or origination fees. Fund expenses come out of Goodfellow’s share, not investors’.

Quarterly liquidity

No lockup, with quarterly redemptions and no gate or percentage limit.

Investor governance

A majority of Units can remove the Manager, and key changes require majority or individual investor consent.

Warrant upside

Every facility carries warrant coverage of at least 5% on the borrower’s equity, and 100% of warrant proceeds go to investors, on top of the preferred return.

Evergreen structure

No fixed term: lines are renewed or replaced at maturity and principal is reinvested, keeping capital working.

Credit facility terms (minimums)

Interest rateFixed, at least 16% per year, paid monthly
Origination and other feesNone; borrowers reimburse only the legal, audit and lien costs of their own lines
WarrantsAt least 5% coverage on the borrower’s (or its manager’s) equity, at fair market value, with a 10-year term and net exercise; increased proportionally for line increases; 100% of proceeds to investors
Line term1 to 5 years

Lender protections

Collections control

Collections flow into fund-controlled accounts, with monthly borrowing base certificates, field exams and performance triggers.

Guaranties

Validity and “bad acts” guaranties from borrower principals, plus owner performance guaranties at the merchant level.

Backup servicing

A backup servicer and step-in rights if a borrower cannot service its portfolio.

Covenants

Minimum equity and liquidity covenants, with merchant and industry concentration limits.

Clean collateral

Defaulted and modified receivables are excluded from the borrowing base.

Underwriting

KYC, background, credit and time-in-business underwriting, with daily or weekly ACH remittances from merchants.
