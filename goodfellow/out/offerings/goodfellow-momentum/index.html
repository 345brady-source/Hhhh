Overview

Goodfellow Momentum runs a composite momentum engine across long-only global ETFs, with no leverage or derivatives, in a separately managed account.

The strategy is managed by Goodfellow Asset Management (GFAM), with Lilliano Capital Management serving as sub-adviser, and has more than $173 million in assets.

Target volatility, a capped cash valve and dynamic equity caps adjust exposure as market regimes shift. The strategy seeks to capture a meaningful share of market gains while limiting the depth of declines, aiming to give investors a steadier experience and provide consistent, attractive risk-adjusted returns over a full market cycle.

Because it is held in a separately managed account, investors own the ETFs directly at their custodian, with full transparency into holdings and daily liquidity.

How it works

A systematic engine with risk controls built in.

  1. 01

    Composite momentum ranking

    The engine scores global ETFs on multiple momentum signals and rotates toward the strongest, most persistent trends.

  2. 02

    Concentrated, long-only portfolio

    Up to 15 long-only ETFs with per-name caps, no leverage and no derivatives, held in the investor’s own account.

  3. 03

    Adaptive risk controls

    Target volatility, a capped cash valve and dynamic equity caps adjust exposure as market regimes shift.

  4. 04

    Disciplined rebalancing

    Weekly to quarterly rebalancing, with a turnover-aware optimizer to manage taxes.

Six layers of active risk control

Target volatility

Scales exposure to hold portfolio risk near its target.

Capped cash valve

Moves part of the portfolio to cash when volatility spikes.

Dynamic equity caps

Trims equity exposure in weak regimes, restoring it as trends recover.

Monthly risk recalibration

Keeps volatility and correlation estimates current every month.

Rank hysteresis and cadence smoothing

Waits for trends to persist before trading, avoiding whipsaw.

Exposure gates and group caps

Blocks duplicate exposures and caps each asset class at rebalance.
