Overview

Mav Equity Income Fund seeks to generate consistent, attractive current income for investors, with a disciplined focus on capital preservation.

The Fund invests across the alternative business finance market, including merchant cash advances, private credit and lines of credit to other credit managers. These short-duration, income-producing assets are priced at a premium to traditional bank lending and repay frequently, which supports steady monthly distributions.

Demand for this capital is persistent. In the Federal Reserve’s most recent Small Business Credit Survey, 60% of small employer firms applied for financing, yet only about four in ten received all of the financing they sought, and the share of applicants turning to online lenders rose from 17% in 2020 to 29% in 2025.1

The Fund’s capital is deployed through Block Equity Group, an established small business finance company active since 2021.

Investment highlights

Monthly income

Distributions are paid monthly, with preferred returns of 10%, 12% or 15% depending on the class of Units.

Short-duration assets

Much of the portfolio is repaid within months through daily or weekly remittances, so capital cycles back quickly and can be redeployed.

Diversified credit exposure

Capital is spread across merchant cash advances, private credit and lines of credit to other credit managers, and across a broad base of small businesses and industries.

Independent of public markets

Income is driven by small business repayments and credit performance rather than by stock or bond prices.

Investors paid first

Preferred Units are paid ahead of the Manager’s common units, and the Manager charges no management fee.

Liquidity

Redemptions can be requested at any time and will be satisfied within 45 days.

Management

MAV Equity manages the Fund and directs the allocation of its capital. The Manager is led by Michael Valdini, who also serves as Chief Executive Officer of the Fund’s origination partner.

Redemptions

When

Members can request the redemption of all or part of their Units at any time.

Timing

Redemption requests will be satisfied within 45 days.

How

By written notice to the Manager specifying the Class and number of Units to be redeemed.

Redemption price

The Member’s unreturned capital contributions for the redeemed Units plus any Preferred Return Balance on those Units.
