Sponsors

Experienced sponsors, rigorously reviewed

The sponsor behind a Delaware Statutory Trust shapes nearly every part of the investment, from the property it selects to the way the trust is financed, managed and eventually sold. We focus on sponsors whose priorities align with our investors’ objectives and who have the experience to deliver on them.

Every offering we present first passes a rigorous review of the sponsor’s track record, financial strength and full-cycle results, alongside the property, financing and offering terms.

Before reviewing sponsor information, learn how we qualify an offering and review the risks of DST investments. These resources explain factors to consider; they are not individualized investment, tax or legal advice.

The role of a sponsor

What is the role of a sponsor?

A DST sponsor carries an offering from the first property search through the eventual sale.

  1. 01

    Select

    Sources and underwrites institutional-quality properties in markets with strong long-term fundamentals.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Forms the trust, arranges financing and builds an offering that meets the IRS requirements for DSTs.

  3. 03

    Offer

    Prepares the offering documents and distributes the offering through broker-dealers and financial advisors.

  4. 04

    Manage

    Oversees the property and asset management, distributions and investor reporting throughout the hold.

  5. 05

    Exit

    Plans and executes the sale or other exit at the end of the hold period.
