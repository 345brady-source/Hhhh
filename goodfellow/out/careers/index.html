Vice President, Capital Markets
  • Remote (United States)
  • Independent contractor (1099)
  • Commission-based
Place the firm’s private placement offerings with high-net-worth investors in a fully remote, commission-based role.

The role

We are seeking a Vice President of Capital Markets to place private offerings with high-net-worth investors. This is a fully remote, commission-based role for an experienced, self-directed professional with an established investor network who wants direct access to institutional-quality private placements and the support of a senior team.

Responsibilities

  • Distribute the firm’s private placement offerings to accredited high-net-worth investors and family offices.
  • Source, develop and maintain relationships with qualified investors, building a base of repeat participants across offerings.
  • Present offerings clearly and accurately using approved materials, and answer investor questions about structure, risks and terms.
  • Confirm investor accreditation and suitability, and guide investors through the subscription process with our operations team.
  • Maintain an organized pipeline and consistent follow-up throughout each offering period.
  • Conduct all activity through the firm’s broker-dealer in accordance with FINRA and SEC rules and firm supervisory procedures.

Qualifications

  • Active FINRA Series 7 and 63 licenses required. In special circumstances, we will consider sponsoring licensing for exceptional candidates.
  • A proven track record placing private placements or alternative investments with high-net-worth investors
  • An established network of accredited investors, family offices or referral sources
  • Strong communication and relationship-building skills
  • The discipline to manage a pipeline independently in a fully remote role

Compensation

This is a 1099 independent contractor position and is not salaried. Compensation includes:

  • Commission: 5–7% on each transaction you place
  • Success fee share: a percentage of the overall success fee on each placement
  • Draw: a small draw available for qualified individuals

Candidates sponsored for licensing begin earning commissions once registered.

Vice President, Investment Banking
  • New York, NY
  • Independent contractor (1099)
  • Commission-based
Originate investment banking transactions and distribute securities offerings across retail and wholesale channels.

The role

We are seeking a Vice President of Investment Banking to originate investment banking transactions and distribute securities offerings across retail and wholesale channels. This role suits an entrepreneurial professional with an established network who wants to build a book of business with the support of an experienced platform and senior-level guidance.

Responsibilities

Deal origination

  • Source and qualify middle-market companies, business owners and real estate sponsors seeking capital, M&A advisory, debt financing or restructuring.
  • Build relationships with owners, CFOs, sponsors and referral sources such as attorneys, accountants and intermediaries.
  • Lead initial client conversations, gather the information needed to evaluate each opportunity, and work with senior bankers on structuring and execution.
  • Maintain an organized pipeline and report regularly on deal activity.

Securities distribution

  • Distribute the firm’s securities offerings, including private placements and Delaware Statutory Trust offerings, through both retail and wholesale channels.
  • Develop and manage relationships with broker-dealers, registered investment advisers, financial advisors, family offices and institutional investors.
  • Present offerings, support selling-agreement and due diligence processes, and serve as a point of contact for distribution partners.
  • Coordinate with the firm on approved marketing materials and investor communications.

Compliance

  • Conduct all securities activity through the firm’s broker-dealer in accordance with FINRA and SEC rules and firm supervisory procedures.

Qualifications

  • Experience in investment banking, capital markets, wholesaling, private placements or alternative investment distribution
  • An established network of business owners, capital providers, broker-dealers, advisors or investors
  • Strong presentation, relationship-building and communication skills
  • A self-directed, entrepreneurial approach to building a pipeline and book of business
  • Highly preferred, but not required: active FINRA Series 7 and/or Series 79, plus Series 63
  • Not yet licensed? We will sponsor qualified candidates for the required FINRA registrations through our broker-dealer.

Compensation

This is a 1099 independent contractor position with commission-based compensation. You are compensated on both originations and closings: you earn compensation on the investment banking transactions you originate, commissions when those transactions close, and commissions on the securities you distribute.
