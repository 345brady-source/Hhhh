Capital Markets

From offering documents to distribution

Raising capital through a private placement takes more than a strong strategy. Investors and the broker-dealers who serve them expect institutional-quality offering documents, clear terms and a disciplined distribution plan.

Goodfellow helps sponsors and issuers build both. Our team brings experience in securities structuring and wholesale distribution, and works with issuers from the first term sheet through the final closing.

PPM Creation

Offering documents built for investors and broker-dealers

We structure the offering and prepare the private placement memorandum and supporting documents with the issuer’s securities counsel, so the offering is ready for broker-dealer due diligence and investor review.

Offering structure

Vehicle, security type, share classes, return waterfall, fees, liquidity and investor eligibility, designed around the issuer’s objectives and the market it is raising from.

Private placement memorandum

The full memorandum: summary of terms, business and strategy, management, conflicts of interest, risk factors, use of proceeds and tax considerations.

Subscription documents

Subscription agreements, investor questionnaires and accredited investor verification procedures for Rule 506(b) and 506(c) offerings.

Investor materials

Fact sheets, investor presentations and key terms summaries that present the offering clearly and consistently with the memorandum.

Regulatory filings

Coordination of Form D and state notice filings, and preparation of the materials a broker-dealer needs for its review.

Counsel coordination

Every document is prepared alongside the issuer’s securities counsel, who reviews and approves the final offering documents.

Distribution Consulting

A distribution plan that reaches the right investors

We design and help execute the plan that takes an offering to market, from channel selection and selling agreements to wholesaling, investor onboarding and reporting.

Channel strategy

Which investors to reach and how: independent broker-dealers, registered investment advisers, family offices and verified accredited investors.

Selling group development

Selling agreements, due diligence packages and third-party due diligence reports that broker-dealers and advisers expect before approving an offering.

Wholesaling programs

Internal and external wholesaling structure, territories, targets and compensation, within FINRA limits.

Marketing and compliance review

Marketing materials and communications prepared for review and approval by the participating broker-dealer under FINRA rules.

Investor onboarding

Subscription processing and accredited investor verification workflows that move investors from interest to funding efficiently.

Reporting and investor relations

Distribution statements, investor reporting and ongoing communication that support repeat investment and future raises.

How we work

Our engagement process

  1. 01

    Assess

    Review the issuer, the strategy and the capital need to define the offering and its target investors.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Set the security, terms, economics and exemption, and align them with the distribution plan.

  3. 03

    Document

  4. 04

    Launch

    Complete broker-dealer due diligence, selling agreements and regulatory filings.

  5. 05

    Distribute

    Activate channels and wholesaling, onboard investors and report through the life of the offering.
