# Quick fix: a field read from a picture covers its printed box, ink included

## Why

The owner asked for fields that look finished ("the entry fields look like shit, round the corners and make them more finished and professional"), and the 01.1 UI-SPEC's field-box rule says a field covers the printed box, its ink included, never inside it. Phase 01.2 plan 07's proof found that on the owner's picture form the fields of boxed blanks, initials, signatures and dates sit about 0.3 to 0.5 pt inside the printed box's outer edge, so the box's dark edges show beside the field (clearest at the ends of the initials boxes on pages 4, 5, 7 and 8). Measured mean insets (left/top/right/bottom): blanks 0.43/0.33/0.45/0.34; initials 0.48/0.36/0.36/0.28; signatures and dates about 0.5 on every side; tick boxes about 0. Pictures: `SCRATCH/exec-012/score-abcde-20261114T070224-7516/printed`.

Plan 01.2-01's finder keeps the box's outer edge in `found` while frame strokes are written along the middle of the strokes; the field is evidently being built from an inner or middle edge.

## Rules

- Find where a picture field's box comes from and build it from the printed box's OUTER ink edge (the same rule text pages follow: the field covers the ink of its place; for a drawn box, the box's outer edge). A class rule for every boxed picture place, not a page.
- Add a measure to `picture-fields.js`: on the owner's form, for every boxed field (not tick boxes, which already cover), the field's edges are at or outside the printed box's outer ink edge on all four sides (inset <= 0 pt, and never more than 1.5 pt outside), measured from the finder's own outer edges. It must fail on the code as it is now and pass after.
- The owner's form stays at 241 of 241, 0 strays, 0 overlaps, 0 kind and 0 owner differences (`checks/score.js --only a,e --gate full,pictures`, run at least twice; the page 3 initials box is the fragile one); no two fields may come to overlap because they grew (touching boxes stay two fields with no overlap above 0.5 pt).
