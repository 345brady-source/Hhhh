# Quick fix: no field on a place nobody completes, on the two public scans

## Why

The owner's rule: a field only on a place someone completes; never a field where nothing is meant to be filled in ("don't start adding signature boxes places randomly like before, it overlays the agreement"). Phase 01.2 plans 05 and 06 measured the two public scans (the 72-page binder and the 43-page booklet, found through `SCRATCH/public-agreements/scanset.json`) and left 11 fields on places nobody completes, plus two wrong-owner / wrong-kind classes. The record, by page and kind only, is `SCRATCH/public-agreements/scanset-nobody.json`; the pictures are in `SCRATCH/exec-01206/scanset-final`. `scanset.js --doc all --nobody` FAILS on them today.

## Classes to repair (each as a rule for any picture page, never for one page or document)

1. Top or bottom edge of a ruled table read as a fill-in line (binder p7, p15, p70). A table edge has vertical strokes meeting its ends or crossing it; a fill-in line does not. Plan 06 noted only the pixel reader sees those strokes: use the picture's pixels (`picturepage.js` / `textread.js`) to mark such a rule as a table edge before placement.
2. The short rule that separates footnotes from the body (binder p9, p33, p35). Must NOT remove a real signature line whose caption was not read (booklet p41 has two). Find what tells them apart on the page (e.g. a footnote rule sits at the left margin with small print beneath it that starts with a footnote mark, and nothing above it on its line; a signature line has a caption band or a heading block near it, or sits in a block of By / Name / Title lines). If no rule separates them cleanly on every page of the scan set and the owner's form, stop and report this class.
3. A table cell taken for one box where no neighbouring cell is found (binder p15).
4. The edge of a ticked box inside a table cell read as a small field (binder p7).
5. A date line already answered: "Date" with a handwritten date on the rule (booklet p41, p43). A rule with ink standing on it is answered (the text reader's rule for typed text); on a picture page, handwriting on the rule is ink on it.
6. Not "nobody" faults, same plan: the booklet's individual signature lines come out as kind Text (should be signature); the binder's "Partnership Use Only" blanks go to the investor (they are the firm's own use: nobody's required field, through the shared owner `asOther` or a firm-use rule, never the investor's).

## Rules

- Every repair is a class rule, proved on a made-up page with a control case, in `picture-fields.js` (a new part) or `scanset.js`.
- The owner's form must stay at 241 of 241, 0 strays, 0 overlaps, 0 kind and 0 owner differences after every change (run `checks/score.js --only a,e --gate full,pictures` at least twice; the page 3 initials box is the fragile one). Page confirmation (`picture-fields.js` part confirm) must still pass.
- Text pages untouched: every change in `anchors.js` is guarded by `raw.pictured`. Proof steps 1 and 5, the nine check files (`checks/earlier.js --proof 1,5 --nine`) and the text scorers (`SCRATCH/owner-set/measure.js --root "$PROJECT_ROOT" --places A,B,C`) must stay green.
- Do not loosen `scanset.js`; its `--nobody` measure is the target: `scanset.js --doc all --nobody` ends in PASS, with every page of the record at 0 fields on places nobody completes, and no page losing a field on a place someone does complete (compare with the record's per-page counts of real places).
- A class that cannot be repaired without breaking the owner's form or removing a real place: stop and report it, finish the others.

## Done when
