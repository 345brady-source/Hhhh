# Quick fix: closes the two gaps the Phase 01.2 verifier found

Read `.planning/phases/01.2-agreements-that-are-pictures-the-reader-reads-the-owner-s-fo/01.2-VERIFICATION.md` first.

## Task 1 (first, the owner's form): no field carries another place's caption

Gap 1: of the owner's form's 241 field names, 13 carry a NEIGHBOURING place's caption (page 1: the custodian's large panel; page 2: two co-investor blanks, both e-mail blanks, three blanks in the interested-party block's first row; page 5: five adviser blanks). Cause found by the verifier: when a box's caption over it is not read, `boxField` falls to the under band, which is the caption over the next row's box. These fields are not marked individually, so one "I have checked this page" would send the wrong name to the signer. No check measures names. The verifier's comparison script: `SCRATCH/v-names.js`.

- Rule (a class rule for every picture page): a caption band belongs to the one box it is nearest to and attached to; a band that is the over band of another found box is never used as a lower box's under caption. A box left with no caption of its own is named by its kind's plain word or by its shape, and keeps its OWN individual mark (`checkBy` 'field', reason: caption not read), so page confirmation never waves it through.
- Measure, added to `checks/score.js` as a named gate `names` (and to the `full` gate's checks): on the owner's form, for every field, its name is either its own caption (letters compared case-insensitively, a close reading allowed within the band reader's tolerance), or its kind's plain word / shape name with an individual mark. A field named with ANOTHER place's caption fails (compare against the answer sheet's labels of all places on the page). It must fail on the code as it is now (13) and pass after (0).
- The owner's form stays 241/241, 0 strays, 0 overlaps, 0 kind and 0 owner differences (run `score.js --only a,e --gate full,pictures,names` at least twice; the page 3 initials box is the fragile one). Report the new count of marks left after 8 page confirmations (it may rise above 33: that is correct if those boxes truly have no caption of their own).

## Task 2: binder page 66, the firm's own-use blanks

Gap 2: on the binder (public scan), page 66's two "For Partnership Use Only" blanks go to the investor and the second is required. The heading that decides sits about 100 pt above the first place, beyond every band the picture reader reads today. The text reader has no firm-use rule either.

- Rule: a block under a heading that reserves it for the firm or the partnership ("for ... use only", "do not write below", "office use", "to be completed by the general partner/manager/fund") belongs to nobody who signs in the portal: optional, never the investor's, never filled from onboarding — through the shared owner `asOther` (party named for the firm's use) — on picture pages AND on text pages (the same phrase list in one place in `placement.js`).
- On picture pages this needs a heading zone that reaches up to the nearest heading above a block of places (bounded, e.g. up to 120 pt, stopping at the next found box or rule above), read only once per block, not per box.
- Speed: pages 1 and 2 of the owner's form read in 5.5 to 5.8 s against a 6 s target (D-18). If the zone pushes any page of the owner's form to 6 s or over on two runs, stop this task, revert it, and report; Task 1 stands on its own.
