# Phase 01.1 — Acceptance criteria

**Phase:** 01.1 Placement reads what the agreement has: fields only on its own blanks, boxes and signature lines
**Written:** 2026-11-13, from the owner's own words and the debug session `.planning/debug/placement-real-agreement.md`
**Status of every line below:** locked. Nothing here is optional and nothing may be traded for speed.

## 1. Why this phase exists

The owner uploaded a real subscription agreement to the published preview and judged the automatic placement a failure. Phase 1 was checked only on agreements generated in code, only locally, and its checks could not see a field placed where the document has no blank. This phase replaces the way the portal reads an agreement so that the result is right on real documents, and proves it on real documents and in the preview's own conditions.

## 2. The owner's words (verbatim, binding)

- "every box needs to be tickable, every field needs to be fillable, every signature needs to be signable, learn how to read this document and place fields correctly"
- "you need to notice things about where the signers signature is, and where the counter signature is, it may not have either you need to be able to recognize wether it does or not, only add fields to corresponding fields and boxes on the agreement, don't start adding signature boxes places randomly like before, it overlays the agreement"
- "This is just an example of a subscription agreement for a ppm"
- On seeing a page with its fields drawn (2026-11-13): "why didn't you make all of the boxes fillable on this? Also the entry fields look like shit, round the corners and make them more finished and professional"
- Earlier, still binding: "you have to be able to distinguish what get signed by the client, and what gets filled in by the client, and what requires initials"; "pre fill as much as possible from the onboarding, make the rest fillable"; signing "needs to become a seemless part of the onboarding workflow".

The third line means the uploaded agreement is one example. The reading must work on subscription agreements in general. Tuning rules to this one document is a failure of this phase.

## 3. What is wrong today (established by the debug session)

Measured on the owner's example (13 pages, 47 places a field belongs), current code, local run:

1. A signature, name and date block is invented at fixed positions on the last page for any signer whose signature line was not found. This is what drops boxes over the agreement's text. (`assets/js/placement.js` `analyze()`, and the callers in `assets/js/esign.js` `autoFields`, `agreementTodo`.)
2. Any run of three or more underscores becomes a required investor field, including the sponsor's own bracketed placeholders in a wiring table: 4 stray fields.
3. A blank on the line under its label gets a field only when the label is in a fixed vocabulary: 3 blanks get no field.
4. A label that wraps over more than two lines is cut to its tail, so the field is mislabelled and the known answer (the commitment amount) is not filled in.
5. On the signature page each signature box overlaps the field above it, and date fields cover half their line.
6. `agreementTodo` demands a signature field for each signer, so there is no state for "this document has no such block".
7. The checks pass anyway: they require the invented block, count a stray only at high confidence, never use a real document, and never run in the preview's conditions.
8. Not yet explained: the owner's preview looked much worse than the local run. Agreements stored in the browser from an earlier version keep their old fields, and nothing on screen says what the portal read or which version is running.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

### A. A field only where the agreement has a place for it

- **AC-01** Every field the portal places by itself sits on something printed on the page for that purpose: a blank line (drawn rule, run of underscores, underlined gap, table cell left empty for an answer), a tick box (drawn square, box glyph, empty bracket pair used as a box), a signature line, or a form field the PDF already carries. No automatic field anywhere else.
- **AC-02** No automatic field covers printed words of the agreement. The only printing a field may cover is its own blank line or box.
- **AC-03** The invented block is removed completely. No code path places a field at a fixed position, a fixed fraction of a page, or "the last page" because nothing was found.
- **AC-04** Text the sponsor completes before sending (bracketed placeholders such as a wiring table) takes no field.
- **AC-05** No two automatic fields overlap each other.

### B. Every place gets its field

- **AC-06** Every printed tick box becomes a box the client can tick, sitting on the printed box and of its size.
- **AC-07** Every printed blank becomes a field the client can fill in, on its line and of its width, in each of these arrangements: beside its label; on the line under its label; at the end of a label that runs over two or more lines; inside a sentence; in a table cell.
- **AC-08** Every printed signature line becomes a signature field on that line. A line for initials becomes an initials field.
- **AC-09** Each field takes its name from the agreement's own complete label (the whole wrapped label, not its last line), and its kind from that label: signature, initials, date, amount, percentage, email, phone, tax number, address, free text, tick box.
- **AC-10** A field's box fits its place: it spans the printed blank, rests on the line, and does not reach into the line above or below. A signature box may be taller than a text box only as far as the empty space above its line allows.
- **AC-11** A tick box is never forced: a client can finish signing with boxes left unticked, unless the sponsor marks a box as required in the editor.

### C. Who signs and who fills in

- **AC-12** The portal recognises separately whether the agreement has (a) a signature block for the investor and (b) a countersignature or acceptance block for the firm: both, one, or neither.
- **AC-13** Where a block exists, each of its lines gets its field for the right person: the investor's lines for the investor, the firm's lines (signature, name, title, dates, accepted amount) for the firm's signer.
- **AC-14** Where an agreement has separate blocks or sections for individuals and for entities, each investor gets only the block for their kind of account. Fields in the other block are not shown to them and never block them.
- **AC-15** Where the agreement has no countersignature block, nothing is placed for the firm and signing is set up with no firm step, unless the sponsor adds a firm field by hand.
- **AC-16** Where the agreement has no investor signature line, nothing is placed for the investor's signature, the sponsor is told in plain words, and the agreement cannot be used for onboarding until the sponsor adds an investor signature field by hand.
- **AC-17** A second investor signature line (joint subscriber, spouse, second authorised signatory), a witness line or a notary line gets its field on its line, optional, marked for the sponsor's review with the reason. It is never dropped silently and never made a required field of the wrong person.

### D. Filled in from onboarding

- **AC-18** Fields whose answer the portal already holds (investor name, entity name, signatory name and title, address, email, phone, commitment amount, today's date at signing, the firm signer's name and title, the accepted amount) arrive filled in. Everything else is left for the client to fill in. This must hold for labels that wrap and for blanks under their label.

### E. Honest about what it read

- **AC-19** After reading, the sponsor sees a short plain summary: how many pages were read; how many tick boxes, blanks and signature lines were found; whether an investor signature block was found and on which page; whether a countersignature block was found and on which page.
- **AC-20** When the agreement's text cannot be read (a scan, a damaged file, a reader failure), the portal says exactly that, places nothing by guesswork, and leaves the sponsor to place fields by hand in the editor. A failure is never silent and never looks like a normal result.
- **AC-21** Fields the portal is unsure about (who fills it, what kind it is) are still placed only on their printed blank, are marked for review with the reason, and must be confirmed or moved before the agreement can be used (unchanged rule from Phase 1, PLACE-02 and PLACE-03).
- **AC-22** For an agreement that was uploaded under an earlier version, the sponsor can have the portal read the document again from the editor. Fields the portal placed and the sponsor never touched are replaced by the new reading; fields the sponsor placed, moved or changed are kept.

### F. The whole path works, not just the placement

- **AC-23** On the example agreement, an individual investor can tick every box, fill every blank of theirs and sign, inside the onboarding signing step, and finish. An entity investor can do the same with the entity's fields. The firm can countersign and complete its own fields. The finished PDF shows every value on its own line.
- **AC-24** Reading stays quick and the page stays responsive: a 13-page agreement is read in a few seconds on an ordinary laptop, a 60-page one shows progress while it is read.

### G. Works in the preview exactly as it does locally

- **AC-25** Every check of this phase passes when the portal runs under the published preview's rules, as documented by the artifact host:
  - the page runs in a sandboxed frame on its own origin;
  - scripts load only inline, from the page's own files, or from `cdnjs.cloudflare.com`, `cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/`, `unpkg.com`;
  - stylesheets only inline or from `fonts.googleapis.com`; fonts only from `fonts.gstatic.com` or `data:`;
  - images only `data:` and `blob:`; no remote images;
  - `fetch`, `XMLHttpRequest` and `WebSocket` to any other host are blocked;
  - web workers only from the page's own files or `blob:` URLs;
  - `alert`, `confirm`, `prompt`, downloads started by the page, `window.open` and clipboard reads are refused;
  - `localStorage`, `sessionStorage` and `IndexedDB` may be missing or may throw.
- **AC-26** Nothing in reading, placing, signing or finishing depends on a network request other than the script loads above. (Today the unicode font for unusual names is fetched from a CDN; in the preview that fetch is blocked. Signing must still finish.)
- **AC-27** When storage is missing or throws, the portal tells the sponsor what cannot be saved. It does not fail without a word.

### H. The fields look finished

- **AC-28** Every field box shown on the agreement — in the sponsor's editor, in the investor's signing step and in the firm's countersigning — has rounded corners and a finished, professional look taken from the design system: an even edge, a quiet tint, a clear name, and clear states for empty, active, filled, required and marked for review. No rough dashed boxes. Entry fields the client types into look like the portal's own well inputs.
- **AC-29** A field's name tag or marker never covers the agreement's words. A tick box field shows as a clean box on the printed box, and its tag sits clear of the sentence beside it.
- **AC-30** The look holds at desktop and phone widths, in light and dark, and for each sample firm's brand colour.

## 5. Proof required before this phase can be called done

1. **The owner's example, held out.** The example is used to judge the result, never to write rules from. It stays outside the repository at the path in the environment variable `REAL_AGREEMENT_DIR` (file `agreement.pdf`, expected fields in `expected.json`, both outside the repository). Expected: 47 fields and no others — 15 tick boxes (pages 4, 5, 6, 7), 10 investor blanks (pages 2, 5, 7, 9; the five on page 9 for entities only), 16 lines on the investor signature page (page 11: 7 for an individual, 9 for an entity), 6 for the firm on its acceptance page (page 12), none on pages 1, 3, 8, 10 or 13. Each on its printed place, right person, right kind, nothing overlapping. When the variable is not set the check reports "not run", never "passed".
2. **Agreements the rules were not written from.** A set of further agreements made by a real layout engine (not the PDF library the portal itself uses), covering at least: drawn rules and underscore blanks; blanks beside, under and inside text and in table cells; labels wrapping over one, two and three lines; tick boxes as drawn squares, box glyphs and bracket pairs; signature blocks stacked and side by side; individual and entity blocks; a document with both blocks, with only the investor's, with only the firm's, and with none; a second-signer line; a fillable PDF with its own form fields; a scanned page with no text; a long document. Where public real subscription agreements can be fetched, they are added to this set and kept outside the repository.
3. **Strays count.** In every check, any automatic field that is not on a printed blank, box or signature line fails the check, at any confidence. So does any overlap with printed words or with another field.
4. **Preview conditions.** The same checks run again with the portal inside a sandboxed frame under the rules of AC-25, and give the same results.
5. **End to end.** AC-23 is walked in a browser on the example: individual, entity, firm, finished PDF inspected.
6. **Pictures.** For the example, one image per page taken from the portal's own screen (the sponsor's editor, and the investor's signing step), so the owner judges the real look and the real placement. Not a diagnostic drawing.
7. **Everything else still passes.** The existing checks of phases 1 to 5 are re-run. A check that only passed because of the invented block is rewritten, not deleted and not loosened.
8. **The owner's own check.** After the verifier passes, the preview is republished and the owner checks their own document in it. The phase is not complete before that. This step is never marked deferred.

## 6. Constraints

- The example agreement is confidential. It is never copied into the repository, never committed, and no text from it appears in any committed file, including checks, plans and summaries. Committed files refer to its places by page and kind only.
- White label: investors and email recipients see only the sponsor firm's brand. No product or vendor names in the interface or in code comments.
- No new dependencies. The PDF reader and writer already in use stay (loaded from the allowed script hosts). No optical character recognition in this phase.
- No secrets in the browser.
- Design system: tokens only, no colour or font literals, pill buttons, well inputs, nothing heavier than weight 500; `sloplint` stays at 0 FAIL on the stylesheets it covers.
- Words on screen: plain sentences a sponsor understands. No internal terms.
- Static app, no build step, vanilla JS, namespace `window.CVPortal`.
- Nothing the owner did not ask for. No new features beyond these criteria.

## 7. Out of scope

- Reading scanned agreements (no text layer): the portal says it cannot read them (AC-20). Recognising their text is a later phase.
- Joint investors signing as two people with two logins (v2, SGNR-01). In this phase a second signer's line is placed and flagged (AC-17).
- Changes to the field editor's own behaviour, the signature styles, or the signed record beyond what AC-22, AC-23, AC-26 and AC-28 to AC-30 need.
- The mobile app.
