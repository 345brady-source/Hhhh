# Phase 01.1 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

The owner, on seeing a page with its fields drawn: "the entry fields look like shit, round the corners and make them more finished and professional". Earlier, about the whole feature: "it looks cheap and poorly made". The owner judges the result by looking at the portal's own screens.

This contract covers what is on screen in this phase and nothing more (D-40):

1. The field box on the agreement's page, in three places: the sponsor's editor, the signing step (the investor in onboarding and the firm countersigning in the console use the same page), and the read-only preview of an agreement in the console.
2. The tags, pills and marks that go with a box, and where each sits so it never covers the agreement's words.
3. What the sponsor is told after an agreement is read: the read summary, could not read, no investor signature line, no place for the firm, read by an earlier version, read again, and storage refused.
4. The words for all of it.

It does not change what a field is, where it is placed, or how the editor or the signing step behave. Placement is D-01 to D-17; this contract only says how a placed box looks.

Sources: `01.1-CONTEXT.md` (D-15, D-16, D-19 to D-22, D-27, D-37, D-39 to D-45, D-48, D-49, D-53), `01.1-RESEARCH.md` ("The look of a field box", "Where things go on screen", Pitfalls 18 and 19), `design.md`, `assets/css/tokens.css`, `assets/css/esign.css`, and a prototype of the recipe in this contract, drawn with the portal's own stylesheets and measured (see "Evidence").

## Spacing Scale

The project's own scale in `assets/css/tokens.css`. Every length in this phase is one of these tokens.

| Token | Value | Usage in this phase |
|-------|-------|---------------------|
| `--space-2xs` | 4px | Gap between a box's type mark and its name; a mark's distance from the box's right end; the cap of a tick box's corner |
| `--space-xs` | 8px | Gap between stacked notices; gap between buttons in a row |
| `--space-sm` | 12px | Notice padding (block); icon-to-text gap in a notice |
| `--space-md` | 16px | Notice padding (inline); space under the notices before the editor; the room kept at the right end of an entry field for its mark |
| `--space-lg` | 24px | Editor rail section gap (existing) |
| `--space-xl` | 40px | Not used by new elements |

Exceptions (each already in the project, none added):

- `--space-3xs` 2px, a token of the project's scale: the floor of a box's corner, the cap of the paper strip under a well.
- `--rule-hair` 1px: the edge of every box.
- The 3px text inset of the three field boxes and the 6px edge of the phone signing tray stay exactly as they are, under the waiver at `esign.css:4`. Changing the inset would move what the signer sees against what is printed.
- Sizes of things drawn on the page (box, corner, text in a box, marks) follow the page's own size, as set out under "The field box". They are not spacing.
- Hit areas: 24px on a pointer, 44px on a phone, as a separate transparent layer (see "Size, and where a tap lands").
- Focus rings: `outline: 2px solid`, `outline-offset: 1px` on the page; 2px offset on console controls (existing).

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height |
|------|------|--------|-------------|
| Body (read summary, notice text, dialog text, buttons) | 14px (`--text-sm`) | 400; buttons and emphasis 500 | 1.5 (notices 1.45, existing) |

| Display (the editor's page title, existing `h1` in `.cs-head`) | 23px (`--text-xl`, 1.44rem) | 400, `--font-display`, `--tracking-display` | 1.15 |

Two weights only: 400 and 500. Sentence case everywhere; no capitals, no tracking on body text (`design.md`).

Text drawn on the page is not on this scale: it follows the box, as the printed text follows the page.

- In the editor, a box's name: `font-size: clamp(7px, 58cqh, 13px)`, weight 500, `line-height: 1` (unchanged).
- In the signing step and the preview, a value, a placeholder and the "Sign" cue: `font-size: min(76cqh, 15px)`, `line-height: 1`; the date also `min(76cqh, 17cqw, 15px)`. This is the size the value prints at (`fitText` in `esign.js:543` prints at 72% of the box's height; 76% of the height inside a 1px edge is the same size). The present floor of 6px is removed: a floor makes text taller than a small box.
- The "Review" tag: `font-size: clamp(7px, 54cqh, 12px)`, weight 500, `line-height: 1.25`.

## The field box

### One recipe

Every box on the page, of every kind, in every state:

| Part | Rule |
|------|------|
| Edge | `border: var(--rule-hair) solid <colour>`. One pixel, solid, in every state and at every size. States differ by colour and wash, never by a dashed or dotted edge and never by a thicker edge. After this phase `dashed` and `dotted` do not occur in `assets/css/esign.css` |
| Corner | `border-radius: var(--es-r-box)`; a tick box `var(--es-r-tick)` |
| Wash | `color-mix()` of the box's colour into the paper or into transparent, at the strengths in the tables below. Never a flat colour, never a gradient, never a shadow on a box at rest |
| Name | In the editor: the type mark and the agreement's own label inside the box at its left. In the signing step: the label is the entry field's placeholder; the guide bar names the field the signer is on |
| Ring | `outline: 2px solid <colour>; outline-offset: 1px` for keyboard focus and for the field the signer is on. The only thing allowed outside a box; it is a line, lasts only while the field is active, and is needed by keyboard users (WCAG 2.4.7) |

### How the corner and the edge scale with the box

The corner follows the page, not the single box, so every box on a page has the same corner and a page of fields looks made by one hand. `.es-page` becomes the size container:

```css
:root {
  --es-r-box: clamp(var(--space-3xs), 0.55cqw, var(--r-sm));
  --es-r-tick: clamp(var(--rule-hair), 0.3cqw, var(--space-2xs));
  --es-paper-edge: color-mix(in srgb, var(--es-paper-ink-2) 60%, var(--es-paper));
}
.es-page { container-type: inline-size; }
```

Measured in the prototype:

| Page shown at | Where | A text box (13.5pt tall) | Its corner | A tick box (9.6pt) | Its corner | Edge |
|---------------|-------|--------------------------|------------|--------------------|------------|------|
| 860px wide | Signing step, desktop, 100% | 19.0px tall | 4.7px | 13.5px | 2.6px | 1px |
| 541px wide | Editor at "Fit" on a 1440px screen | 11.9px tall | 3.0px | 8.5px | 1.6px | 1px |
| 366px wide | Signing step on a 390px phone | 8.1px tall | 2.0px | 5.7px | 1.1px | 1px |
| 330px wide | Editor at "Fit" on a 390px phone | 7.3px tall | 2px (floor) | 5.2px | 1px (floor) | 1px |
| 1455px or wider | Zoomed in | grows | 8px (cap, `--r-sm`) | grows | 4px (cap) | 1px |

- `--r-inner` (12px, the radius of the portal's wells) is not used on the page: it would turn every box into a pill. `--r-sm` is the upper bound.
- The edge never scales: 1px at every size. A thinner edge is not drawn the same by every screen.
- A box so low that its corner is more than half its height (a field the sponsor drew very small by hand) takes pill ends, which is a shape of the system. Nothing special is done.
- A box nested inside a box cannot use these two tokens (inside a field the container is the field). Only the box itself, `.fe-origin` and nothing else uses them.
- The dragged copy lives outside any page (`.fe-carry`), where there is no page to measure: `fieldeditor.js` sets `--es-carry-r` on the element to the corner it had on its page when it lifts it (for a new field dragged from the list, the corner a box has on the first page), and the stylesheet reads `border-radius: var(--es-carry-r, var(--r-sm))`. The corner must not jump when a drag starts or ends.

### What the look needs from the box's place

The look cannot move or enlarge a box: the box is the printed blank (D-01, D-06, D-07, D-10). These three properties of placement are what make the look clean. They follow from those decisions and are stated here to the size the look can absorb, measured in the prototype by moving every box 0.7pt off its place.

| Kind | The box's rectangle | What shows when it is off |
|------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| A blank on a line | Left and right edges on the ends of the printed line. Bottom edge within 0.75pt of the lower edge of the line's ink | A second line under the box, or the line sticking out past the box's end |
| A tick box | Covers the printed box, its ink included: on every side no more than 0.5pt outside the ink and never inside it | The printed box's black edges showing beside the field's box (seen at 0.7pt off) |
| A signature line | As a blank; taller only into the empty paper above the line (D-10) | The box reaching into the line above |

The planner decides how the reading gives these (ink edges of a drawn rule, of an underscore run, of a box character). A check must fail when a printed line or a printed box shows outside its own field's box.

In the read-only preview in the console (`.es-doc-ro .es-f-other`): edge `color-mix(in srgb, var(--sc) 42%, var(--es-paper))`, wash `var(--sc)` at 6% in transparent, corner `--es-r-box`, in the signer's own colour so the sponsor sees whose it is.

### Size, and where a tap lands

Research Pitfall 19, and seen in the present pictures: today a signer's box is grown to 24 or 27px on a desktop and to 12 to 16px on a phone. A grown tick box covers the first letters of its sentence; grown boxes on a signature page reach into the lines above and below.

- **The visible box is always the field's own rectangle**: `left: var(--x); top: var(--y); width: var(--w); height: var(--h)`, at every width, for every kind. No `max()`, no floor. The rules at `esign.css:84`, `:103` and `:425-427` that grow the box are removed.
- **The place a tap lands is a separate, see-through layer**: the existing `.sf-hit` element, laid by `signflow.js` under every field of the page, is used at every width (today only on a phone). Its size is `max(24px, the box)` on a pointer (WCAG 2.5.8) and `max(44px, the box)` at 640px wide or less. Because the areas lie under all the fields, a tap on any visible box always reaches that box, and where two areas overlap the later field's area wins only on empty paper between them.
- On a phone a blank is still typed in the tray above the keyboard (existing): the box on the page is a small picture of the field at its true size.
- In the editor nothing changes: the selected field's 44px drag area on touch screens is already a layer of its own (`esign.css:758`).

## Choices made here, for the owner to see and change

The owner was not asked further questions. Where `design.md` or a locked decision answered a point, it was followed. Where neither did, this is what was chosen and why.

| # | Choice | Why | The other way |
|---|--------|-----|---------------|
| 1 | One corner for a whole page, following the page's width (`0.55cqw`, 2px to 8px), not one per box | Every box on a page matches, at every zoom, and it needs no script | A corner worked out from each box's height: boxes of different heights on one page would have different corners |
| 2 | 1px solid edge everywhere; required and optional told apart by strength and the asterisk, not by dashes | The owner asked for finished boxes; D-41 says no rough dashed boxes | Thicker edges for required: uneven, and heavy on small boxes |
| 3 | In the signing step, a field still to do is a well and a done field is ink on the page | "Entry fields the client types into look like the portal's own well inputs" (D-41); what is left stands out and what is done recedes | Done fields stay wells: a page of grey boxes, and the signer cannot see at a glance what is left |
| 4 | A well paints a strip of paper one or two pixels deep under itself | Hides the box's own printed line, so there is one edge and not two. It only ever covers the box's own blank | No strip: a dark line shows under most wells |
| 5 | Tags and marks go inside their own box, or are not drawn | It cannot cover the agreement's words, by construction (D-42), with no measuring of free space | Marks in the margin or beside the box where there is room: needs the reading for every page and can still be wrong on agreements placed by hand |
| 6 | A tick box has no tag and no corner mark at all | Nothing fits inside it, and beside it is the sentence (D-42) | none that keeps D-42 |
| 7 | Boxes are never grown; the tap area is a separate layer | Pitfall 19; a grown tick box covered the first letters of its sentence in today's pictures | Keep a small floor: still covers words on dense pages |
| 8 | On a phone the box shows the value very small (as it will print) | The box is the blank at its true size; typing is in the tray | Hide values on a phone: the signer could not see that a blank is filled |
| 9 | New token `--paper-warn` in `tokens.css` | Without it the "Review" tag is unreadable on the page in dark mode (measured) | Leave it: fails D-43 in dark |
| 10 | The read summary is quiet text; only problems are boxes | A normal result should not shout; a failure must not look like one (D-20) | Summary in a notice box: every agreement would open with a box, and failures would look the same |
| 11 | "Read the PDF again" always asks first | It replaces fields; one clear sentence says which, and which are kept | No question for agreements of this version: quicker, but the sponsor is not told what will happen |
| 12 | "Remove your firm" asks first; "Add your firm" does not | Removing takes the firm's fields away; adding takes nothing away | |
| 13 | After upload the editor opens whenever the reading had any problem | A warning on a page nobody opens is a silent failure (D-20) | Save straight away as today: the sponsor may never see that pages were not read |
| 14 | The editor's handles become round with a 1px edge | They are part of the selected box's look; square handles on round boxes read as unfinished | Leave them square until Phase 2 |

One limit to know: on a tablet with a touch screen wider than 640px, typing in a small box may make the browser zoom toward it, because the text in the box is now its true size and smaller than 16px. On a phone this does not happen (the tray is used). Using the tray on every touch screen is a change to the signing step's behaviour and belongs to Phase 4.
