# Phase 01.1: Placement reads what the agreement has - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-13

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

The portal reads an uploaded subscription agreement and places a field only where the agreement itself has a place for one: a printed blank, a printed tick box, a printed signature line, or a form field the PDF already carries. Every such place gets its field, for the right person and of the right kind. The portal recognises whether the agreement has an investor signature block and a countersignature block (both, one or neither) and sets up signing to match. It never invents a field position. It says what it read and says so when it could not read. The result is proven on real documents, on agreements the rules were not written from, under the published preview's own rules, and end to end through signing. The phase ends with the owner's own check on their document in the preview.

The owner's uploaded agreement is one example ("This is just an example of a subscription agreement for a ppm"). It is held out to judge the result. Writing rules to fit that one document is a failure of this phase.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### A field only where the agreement has a place for it
- **D-01:** Every field the portal places by itself sits on something printed on the page for that purpose: a blank line (drawn rule, run of underscores, underlined gap, table cell left empty for an answer), a tick box (drawn square, box glyph, empty bracket pair used as a box), a signature line, or a form field the PDF already carries. No automatic field anywhere else. (AC-01)
- **D-02:** No automatic field covers printed words of the agreement. The only printing a field may cover is its own blank line or box. (AC-02)
- **D-03:** The invented signature, name and date block is removed completely. No code path places a field at a fixed position, a fixed fraction of a page, or on "the last page" because nothing was found. (AC-03)
- **D-04:** Text the sponsor completes before sending (bracketed placeholders such as a wiring table) takes no field. (AC-04)
- **D-05:** No two automatic fields overlap each other. (AC-05)

### Every place gets its field
- **D-06:** Every printed tick box becomes a box the client can tick, sitting on the printed box and of its size. (AC-06)
- **D-07:** Every printed blank becomes a field the client can fill in, on its line and of its width, in each arrangement: beside its label; on the line under its label; at the end of a label that runs over two or more lines; inside a sentence; in a table cell. (AC-07)
- **D-08:** Every printed signature line becomes a signature field on that line. A line for initials becomes an initials field. (AC-08)
- **D-09:** Each field takes its name from the agreement's own complete label (the whole wrapped label, not its last line) and its kind from that label: signature, initials, date, amount, percentage, email, phone, tax number, address, free text, tick box. (AC-09)
- **D-10:** A field's box fits its place: it spans the printed blank, rests on the line, and does not reach into the line above or below. A signature box may be taller than a text box only as far as the empty space above its line allows. (AC-10)
- **D-11:** A tick box is never forced: a client can finish signing with boxes left unticked, unless the sponsor marks a box as required in the editor. (AC-11)

### Who signs and who fills in
- **D-12:** The portal recognises separately whether the agreement has (a) a signature block for the investor and (b) a countersignature or acceptance block for the firm: both, one, or neither. (AC-12)
- **D-13:** Where a block exists, each of its lines gets its field for the right person: the investor's lines for the investor, the firm's lines (signature, name, title, dates, accepted amount) for the firm's signer. (AC-13)
- **D-14:** Where an agreement has separate blocks or sections for individuals and for entities, each investor gets only the block for their kind of account. Fields in the other block are not shown to them and never block them. (AC-14)
- **D-15:** Where the agreement has no countersignature block, nothing is placed for the firm and signing is set up with no firm step, unless the sponsor adds a firm field by hand. (AC-15)
- **D-16:** Where the agreement has no investor signature line, nothing is placed for the investor's signature, the sponsor is told in plain words, and the agreement cannot be used for onboarding until the sponsor adds an investor signature field by hand. (AC-16)
- **D-17:** A second investor signature line (joint subscriber, spouse, second authorised signatory), a witness line or a notary line gets its field on its line, optional, marked for the sponsor's review with the reason. Never dropped silently, never a required field of the wrong person. (AC-17)

### Filled in from onboarding
- **D-18:** Fields whose answer the portal already holds (investor name, entity name, signatory name and title, address, email, phone, commitment amount, today's date at signing, the firm signer's name and title, the accepted amount) arrive filled in; everything else is left for the client. This holds for labels that wrap and for blanks under their label. (AC-18)

### Honest about what it read
- **D-19:** After reading, the sponsor sees a short plain summary: pages read; tick boxes, blanks and signature lines found; whether an investor signature block was found and on which page; whether a countersignature block was found and on which page. (AC-19)
- **D-20:** When the agreement's text cannot be read (a scan, a damaged file, a reader failure), the portal says exactly that, places nothing by guesswork, and leaves the sponsor to place fields by hand. A failure is never silent and never looks like a normal result. (AC-20)
- **D-21:** Fields the portal is unsure about (who fills it, what kind it is) are still placed only on their printed blank, are marked for review with the reason, and must be confirmed or moved before the agreement can be used. (AC-21; keeps PLACE-02 and PLACE-03)
- **D-22:** For an agreement uploaded under an earlier version, the sponsor can have the portal read the document again from the editor. Fields the portal placed and the sponsor never touched are replaced by the new reading; fields the sponsor placed, moved or changed are kept. (AC-22)

### The whole path works
- **D-23:** On the example agreement, an individual investor can tick every box, fill every blank of theirs and sign, inside the onboarding signing step, and finish; an entity investor can do the same with the entity's fields; the firm can countersign and complete its own fields; the finished PDF shows every value on its own line. (AC-23)
- **D-24:** Reading stays quick and the page stays responsive: a 13-page agreement is read in a few seconds on an ordinary laptop; a 60-page one shows progress while it is read. (AC-24)

### Works in the preview exactly as locally
- **D-25:** Every check of this phase passes with the portal running under the published preview's rules: sandboxed frame on its own origin; scripts only inline, the page's own files, `cdnjs.cloudflare.com`, `cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/`, `unpkg.com`; stylesheets only inline or `fonts.googleapis.com`; fonts only `fonts.gstatic.com` or `data:`; images only `data:` and `blob:`; `fetch`, `XMLHttpRequest` and `WebSocket` to any other host blocked; web workers only from own files or `blob:` URLs; `alert`, `confirm`, `prompt`, page-started downloads, `window.open` and clipboard reads refused; `localStorage`, `sessionStorage` and `IndexedDB` may be missing or throw. (AC-25)
- **D-26:** Nothing in reading, placing, signing or finishing depends on a network request other than those script loads. The unicode font for unusual names is fetched from a CDN today (`assets/js/esign.js` `fetchFont`); in the preview that fetch is blocked, and signing must still finish. (AC-26)
- **D-27:** When storage is missing or throws, the portal tells the sponsor what cannot be saved. It does not fail without a word. (AC-27)

### Proof required (each is a deliverable of this phase)
- **D-28:** The owner's example is held out: used to judge, never to write rules from. It stays outside the repository at `REAL_AGREEMENT_DIR` (`agreement.pdf` and `expected.json`, both outside the repository). Expected: 47 fields and no others — 15 tick boxes (pages 4, 5, 6, 7), 10 investor blanks (pages 2, 5, 7, 9; the five on page 9 for entities only), 16 lines on the investor signature page (page 11: 7 individual, 9 entity), 6 for the firm on its acceptance page (page 12), none on pages 1, 3, 8, 10, 13. Each on its printed place, right person, right kind, nothing overlapping. When the variable is not set the check reports "not run", never "passed".
- **D-29:** A further set of agreements made by a real layout engine (not the PDF library the portal itself uses) covers at least: drawn rules and underscore blanks; blanks beside, under and inside text and in table cells; labels wrapping over one, two and three lines; tick boxes as drawn squares, box glyphs and bracket pairs; signature blocks stacked and side by side; individual and entity blocks; a document with both blocks, only the investor's, only the firm's, and none; a second-signer line; a fillable PDF with its own form fields; a scanned page with no text; a long document. Public real subscription agreements, where they can be fetched, are added to the set and kept outside the repository.
- **D-30:** In every check, any automatic field that is not on a printed blank, box or signature line fails the check, at any confidence. So does any overlap with printed words or with another field.
- **D-31:** The same checks run again with the portal inside a sandboxed frame under the rules of D-25 and give the same results.
- **D-32:** D-23 is walked end to end in a browser on the example: individual, entity, firm, finished PDF inspected.
- **D-33:** For the example, one image per page with the placed fields drawn on it is produced for the owner to look at (kept outside the repository).
- **D-34:** The existing checks of phases 1 to 5 are re-run and pass. A check that only passed because of the invented block is rewritten, not deleted and not loosened.
- **D-35:** After the verifier passes, the preview is republished and the owner checks their own document in it. The phase is not complete before that. This step is never marked deferred.

### Constraints
- **D-36:** The example agreement is confidential: never copied into the repository, never committed, and no text from it appears in any committed file (checks, plans, summaries included). Committed files refer to its places by page and kind only.
- **D-37:** White label: investors and email recipients see only the sponsor firm's brand; no product or vendor names in the interface or in code comments.
- **D-38:** No new dependencies. The PDF reader and writer already in use stay, loaded from the allowed script hosts. No optical character recognition in this phase.
- **D-39:** Design system: tokens only, no colour or font literals, pill buttons, well inputs, nothing heavier than weight 500; `sloplint` stays at 0 FAIL on the stylesheets it covers. Words on screen are plain sentences a sponsor understands, with no internal terms.
- **D-40:** Static app, no build step, vanilla JS, namespace `window.CVPortal`. Nothing the owner did not ask for: no new features beyond these decisions.

### Claude's Discretion
- How the page is read (text layer, drawing operators, rendered pixels, form-field dictionary, or a combination) and how evidence from each is combined.
- The internal data model for what was found on a page, and how confidence is computed.
- How wrapped labels are joined and how a blank is tied to its label.
- How individual and entity blocks and sections are told apart.
- The exact wording and placement of the read summary, within D-19 and D-39.
- Which layout engine generates the further test agreements, and how the preview's rules are reproduced in the checks.
- The shape of the check files and their fixtures.

</decisions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- The debug session's measured result on the example, to beat: 44 of 47 places get a field, 3 get none (two blanks under their labels on page 5, one on page 9), 4 stray fields on page 13, two wrapped labels cut to their tail (pages 2 and 7), signature boxes overlapping the field above on page 11, date fields covering half their line.
- The preview host's documented rules (D-25) say `IndexedDB` works per artifact origin and images may be `blob:`; so the unexplained difference between the owner's preview and the local run is most likely an agreement stored under an earlier version (hence D-22) or a failure with no message (hence D-19, D-20, D-27).
- The reader already loads its worker script on the main thread (`loadOnce(PDFJS_WORKER_URL, …)`), so reading does not need a web worker; keep it that way.
- A fillable PDF's own form fields are the most reliable evidence of where a field belongs and what kind it is; use them when present.

</specifics>
