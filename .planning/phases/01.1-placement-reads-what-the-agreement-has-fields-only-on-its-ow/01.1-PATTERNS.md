### `assets/js/pageops.js` (NEW; reader adapter) or the same code inside `esign.js` `pageLayout`

**Analog:** `assets/js/esign.js` `pageLayout` (lines 2376-2420) for structure; `assets/js/intake.js` `readPdf` (lines 71-79) for the safe open.
