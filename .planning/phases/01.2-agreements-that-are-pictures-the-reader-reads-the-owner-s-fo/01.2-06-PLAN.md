<objective>
Make what the sponsor does with a picture agreement workable and truthful: seconds a page with the page named while
it is read, a time limit that says exactly what was left, and one action that confirms a page the sponsor has checked.

Purpose: D-18 asks for a few seconds a page, progress, and a truthful message at a time limit; today the limit is 150
seconds with a sentence that says nothing was placed, which is no longer how a picture page is read. And every field
read from a picture is marked (D-17): on the owner's form that is 241 marks, each of which had to be confirmed by
itself before the agreement could be saved. The owner's decision (recorded here): the sponsor confirms a page they have
checked with one action; a mark stays a field's own only where the caption could not be read or the owner is in
doubt. D-17 and the rule of Phase 01.1 still hold: every picture field is marked when placed and is confirmed before
use.

Output: the limit, its sentence and the progress in `assets/js/esign.js`; `checkBy` in `assets/js/placement.js`; the
action `es-page-ok` with `setMany` in `assets/js/fieldeditor.js`; the parts `limit` and `confirm` of
`checks/picture-fields.js`.
</objective>
