# Phase 01.2: Agreements that are pictures - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-13
**Status:** Ready for planning
**Source:** PRD Express Path (the owner's instructions of 2026-11-13, recorded below; no separate PRD file)

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Some subscription agreements arrive as PDFs with no text: every page is a picture (a browser or printer "print to PDF" of a page made of images, or a scan). The owner uploaded such a form (8 pages, each page one low-resolution picture, about 107 dpi) and requires that the reader reads it. This phase makes the portal read a picture agreement: find every printed box and line on the picture, put a field on each, name it and assign it from the words printed around it, and never place a field anywhere else. The owner's form is the form this is developed against; it must also hold on the other picture documents listed under the proof.

A first attempt was built today without planning and stopped by the owner ("start over and use the gsd planning"). Its code is in the repository (`assets/js/textread.js`, `assets/vendor/textread/`, picture-page branches in `anchors.js`, `placement.js` and the reading adapter of `esign.js`), off by default. It is a prototype: this phase's research judges what is sound and what is replaced.

</domain>

- **D-05:** "start over and use the gsd planning": research, plan, plan check, build, verification, owner check, none skipped.

### What reading the owner's form means
- **D-06:** Every printed place on the form that a person completes gets a field sitting on it: all 241 on the independent answer sheet (`/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-Hhhh/718a3a12-5721-5a9e-a5ca-f390e1aa2396/scratchpad/build/ocr-truth/truth-printed.json`: 61 tick boxes, 65 initials boxes, 107 blanks, 4 signatures, 4 dates; per page 61, 76, 19, 14, 19, 4, 32, 16).
- **D-07:** No field anywhere else: not on the logo, section rules, the page bar, underlined words, pre-printed details, or running text. No two fields overlap. A field comes only from a box or line found on the picture, never from a recognised word.
- **D-08:** Each field is the right kind: tick box, initials, signature, date beside a signature, or a typed blank. A wide entry box is never a tick box.
- **D-09:** Each field is for the right person, decided from the headings printed over its section or column: the investor's are the investor's; a second investor's are the co-investor's (the merged co-investor role, so the co-investor signs them); a third party's (custodian, broker-dealer, registered representative, branch manager and the like) are nobody's required field: optional, marked, named for that party, never the investor's.
- **D-10:** A signature captioned for a third party is not the investor's signature line. The read summary says truthfully whose signature lines were found and on which pages.
- **D-11:** Each field takes its name from the caption printed before, over, inside or under its box, as well as the small print can be read. Where a caption cannot be read, the field still exists, kind by shape, plainly named, and marked for review.

### The same rules as before
- **D-12:** A field only where the agreement prints a place for it; every printed place gets its field; an unclear one is marked for review with a plain reason, never dropped silently; the summary counts places against fields and tells the truth.
- **D-13:** Honest about what was read: pages read from their picture are named as such; a failure to read is said exactly and nothing is placed by guesswork.
- **D-14:** Never fitted to one document: every rule is stated as a convention class (for example "a box with its caption under it", "a column of boxes under a heading naming who completes it"). The owner's form is a development document; the other picture documents below are the test that the rules generalise.
- **D-15:** Normal PDFs are untouched: the owner's first agreement stays at exactly 47 fields with none marked and no recognition file loaded; prose stays at zero; every existing check and the proof run stay green.
- **D-16:** White label, plain words, design tokens only, no new dependency beyond the one text-recognition library the owner approved; the library ships with the portal's own files and works inside the hosted preview (other hosts blocked; only served file types).
- **D-17:** Real scans must never get wrong fields without the sponsor asking: research decides, on evidence, whether picture reading runs by itself on upload or is offered with one click; if it cannot be shown to give zero stray fields on the scan set, it is offered, and every field it places is marked for checking.
- **D-18:** Speed: a page is read from its picture in a few seconds (target under 6 seconds a page on an ordinary laptop), with progress shown and a truthful message at a time limit.
