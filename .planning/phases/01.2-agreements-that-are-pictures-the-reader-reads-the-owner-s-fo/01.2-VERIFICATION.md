## The two facts the orchestrator asked to be judged

**Page 3, initials 1 (bands.js browser red).** The product is right on this place: the scorer finds 0 kind differences, the frame reading gives 65 initials, and the decision comes from rule K3 ("the verb in a sentence of its caption"), which is proven with its trap ("initial" before a noun is no initials) in picture-fields `shapes`. bands.js accepts only the noun, so it measures less than the product's own rule. That makes it a gap in the check, not in the product. Plan 03's must-have as written ("every initials box has the word for initials read, or a column carries it") is still not met (64 of 65), and `all.js` stays red at 15 of 16. The place is fragile: it is a lone 23 x 10 pt box, and if its verb is misread it falls to the same-size rule. Today's code then makes it a marked "Box", which is a safe failure. Two ways to close it: teach bands.js the K3 verb rule with its trap, or record this override:

```yaml
overrides:
  - must_have: "on the owner's form every one of the 65 initials boxes of the answer sheet either has the caption word for initials read within the tolerance, or stands in a column of two or more boxes of which at least half have it"
    reason: "The 65th box (page 3) is decided initials by rule K3's verb form, proven with its trap; the scorer and the frame reading give 0 kind differences. The band measure accepts the noun only."
    accepted_by: "{owner or orchestrator}"
    accepted_at: "{ISO timestamp}"
```

**Binder page 66, "For Partnership Use Only".** This is a wrong field for the wrong person on a test document the roadmap names (SC5), and it breaks READ-03. It counts as an unclean field. The page mark is the only thing that keeps it from an investor, and one page confirmation takes that mark away. No later phase covers it: Phase 01.3 plan 05 handles the firm's block on text documents B and C, not a firm-use section on a picture. So it stays a gap. The owner may choose to accept it as a known limit of the generalisation test, but that is their decision. I did not defer it.

**33 marks after 8 page confirmations** (caption not read 31, owner in doubt 2, on pages 1, 2, 3 and 5). The code and the check agree. This is the honest result of the rules, and the owner will meet these 33 one by one.
