## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] A band ended inside the next line of print** (Task 3). A row band (the box's row, 1 pt above and below) took the tops of the label's second line; read as one line, such strips came back with no word at all (the cause of most of page 1's misses and of the first strays). Row bands now take the whole lines crossing the middle of their box's row; over, under and rule bands leave out a line their far edge cuts. Pure case added (fails on the previous code). `0e63c0c`.

**2. [Rule 2 - D-02] A free rule's over band: 0 to 20 pt instead of 5 to 15 pt.** The line rules of Phase 01.1 look up to 20 pt above a rule for print (a table's cells) and for the nearest print (clear paper). With the plan's reach the form got 4, then 17 strays (blank fields over section rules). All lines within 20 pt are read where two or more box sides meet the rule; elsewhere the nearest line, taken whole. Strays 1. `72b814f`, `d1beec9`.

**3. [Rule 1] The prose drop applies to the bands of a box only** (the plan words it for boxes). Over a rule the line rules need the words, under a footnote rule its mark. Under a free rule longer than 200 pt, a text line nearly as wide as the rule (the next line of the page) is not read. `caff18a`, `0e63c0c`.

**4. [Rule 1] The margin rule.** "Left of the leftmost place" took rules starting inside the margin column (page 5: a line at the page's left edge); "30% of the picture's height" missed page 6's margin rule (292 px against 338: the page's short section). Now: left of the leftmost place box, at least 30% of the height the places span and a tenth of the picture. Pure cases unchanged and passing.

**5. [Rule 2 - prohibition on reading running text] Letter and margin zones.** The plan's zone (b) is every row at the left edge of the places; that is running text. A letter zone now needs clear paper above (0.6 of its height), an opening mark at most 1.4 times the line's height that ends in a full stop (a dot low in the line) or a closing bracket reaching below the capital, and only its first 70 pt are read. A margin block is read for its first 60 pt. Zones under 10 pt wide or 3 pt high are dropped. On page 2 the two zones are exactly its two lettered lines.

**6. [Rule 1] The letter head's mark** may come back as one or two capitals or digits (the engine read the capital B as "8." and "8B."). It decides only that the line is a heading; the kept text is never changed.
