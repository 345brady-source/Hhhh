## Changes, each a class (no page, position or document named)

**anchors.js**
- `textless()`: counts letter-like drawn shapes (filled, not a plain rectangle, at most 40 pt high) against `LETTER_SHAPES` (40); first test unchanged.
- `page()`: `scan` is set for any text-less page (picture or drawings), so both are counted in the offer and read only after it.
- Picture pages only (`raw.pictured`): a rule flagged `bracket` counts as set in brackets (the drafting-note refusal then applies); words at the start of a line whose label stands right before it are an answer (no field); the paper above a line ends at the page's top edge; a line printed inside a table cell fills the cell.

**textread.js**
- `joinRuns`: level strokes of one run (gap at most 0.8 pt, same height and thickness within 0.3 pt) are one rule, on pages of drawings.
- `inBrackets`: a short rule (at most 72 pt) with an upright stroke 5 to 15 pt high ending within 2 pt under it at each end, within 4 pt outside the end, is set in brackets (pictures and drawings).
- A found box full of print (`why: 'filled'`) is not a frame for the line rules; print a zone shares with a strip is kept once in the page's words (the zone's own text is unchanged).
- A `block` zone is read for its words only.

**picturepage.js** (band classes)
- `ruleRow`: beside a free rule whose own rows hold no whole line, the whole text line coming down to within 3 pt of it (a bold label with descenders).
- A label over a free rule that starts at the rule's left end is read whole past its right end (up to the rule's width again; words may be up to 16 pt apart, `REACH.spread`).
- `paragraph`: over a free rule with words before it and a line above running past both its ends, up to three lines of that paragraph, read whole.
- Block heading zone over a stack of three or more free rules (each within 30 pt of the one above, overlapping across).
- The first line under a long free rule is read (taken whole when the band's edge cuts it).
- The box a place stands inside does not cut that place's bands.

**esign.js** `readNote`: the picture sentence names the pages ("Pages 1 to 8 had no text, so the portal read them …") instead of counting them.

**score.js**: `--self-test`; gate `pictures`.
