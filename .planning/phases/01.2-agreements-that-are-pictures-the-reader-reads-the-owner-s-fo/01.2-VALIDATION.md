# Phase 01.2 — Validation Strategy

> Source: `01.2-RESEARCH.md` § Validation Architecture. The planner fills the per-task map.

## Test Infrastructure

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Framework** | Node scripts with Playwright; an independent scorer and proof runner kept outside the repository (they read documents that may not be committed) |
| **Config file** | none |
| **Quick run command** | the finder's no-browser check added by this phase (pure data, three ink levels) |
| **Full suite command** | scorer parts a to e (`score-notext.js`), proof runner steps 1,3,4,5,6 (`proof.js`), the nine check files |
| **Estimated runtime** | quick: seconds; the scorer on the owner's form: about two and a half minutes until plan 03, about one minute after; a plan's last verify with the earlier checks: 4 to 7 minutes more; the whole proof (`all.js`): about 20 to 30 minutes |

A check that needs a document folder and does not find it prints "NOT RUN" and exits non-zero. "NOT RUN" is never green.

## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** the quick command and the scorer part the task touches
- **After every plan wave:** scorer parts a to e and proof steps 1,3,6
- **In the last verify of every plan that edits `anchors.js`, `placement.js` or `esign.js` (plans 03, 04, 05, 06):** the proof run's steps 1 and 5 and the nine earlier check files (`earlier.js --proof 1,5 --nine`)
- **Before verification:** everything green, inside the sandboxed frame as well (`all.js`)
- **Feedback latency, as measured, not as wished.** The 60 seconds this document first named cannot be kept by an end-to-end measure of a document that takes a minute to read. Measured at HEAD b8709ce on the build machine: scorer parts a and e together 151 s (part a 149 s: whole-page recognition); parts b, c, d together 240 s; the proof run's steps 1 to 3 about 20 to 26 s and step 5 between 75 and 200 s; the nine earlier check files about 3 minutes together. So: checks with no browser and no document (`bands.js --mode pure`, the part `shapes`) answer in seconds; `finder.js --mode pixels` in under a minute; a scorer gate on the owner's form in about two and a half minutes in wave 1 and, once plan 03 reads by bands, in about one minute; a gate over parts a to e in three to five minutes; the earlier checks add 4 to 7 minutes and are therefore run once, in a plan's last verify; `all.js` takes 20 to 30 minutes and is run once, in plan 07. Every task has a check of its own that answers within about a minute except the tracer (plan 01 Task 1, about two and a half minutes) and the tasks whose verify is the scorer over several documents

## Per-Task Verification Map

`CH/` stands for `.planning/phases/01.2-agreements-that-are-pictures-the-reader-reads-the-owner-s-fo/checks/`. Every command is run from the root of the checkout under test; each check finds that checkout from its own file's place, takes the outside documents and the scorer by path, and prints "NOT RUN" with exit status 2 when one is missing. `score.js` runs the independent scorer (`score-notext.js`, outside the repository) against the checkout and applies a named gate to the scorer's own result files: `baseline` (not worse than HEAD: at least 235 of 241, at most 1 stray, 0 overlaps), `places` (241 of 241, 0, 0), `kinds` (and 0 kind differences), `owners` (and 0 owner differences, every field marked), `full` (and safe by default, the three signature sentences, no row FAIL), `rows` (no row FAIL in the parts run), `speed` (6 s a page, 2 s for a page with nothing on it), `pictures` (eight whole-page pictures).
