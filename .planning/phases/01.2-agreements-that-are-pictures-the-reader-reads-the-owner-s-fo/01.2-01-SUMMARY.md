## Decisions Made

- **Corner rule (class rule, in `find`).** A stroke that falls between two pixels leaves a band of half-dark pixels
  along its whole side, inside the box. That band is the edge of the stroke: the corner test starts past it (at most
  one pixel a side, and only when at least 85% of that row or column is not paper). Proved on the made-up page by a
  box drawn with such strokes.
- **Shared from patches.** Past each side, across the stroke, most of the side looks out on one patch. A box with
  its own four strokes sees the same patch on every side; a cell of a ruled table sees more than one. A sliver of
  paper between two frames one pixel apart is too small to count as a patch. This holds inside a page border
  (proved on the made-up page) and beside a cell full of words that is itself no box (the scanned example, page 13).
- **Turned rules.** Level strokes that continue each other one row down, each shifted the same way along, are one
  stroke; its tests follow the stroke column by column. A stroke that stays on its rows is tested exactly as before.
- **Geometry handed on.** `found` is the outer edge of the printed frame (it equals the answer sheet to a tenth of a
  point). `rects` and the frame strokes are written along the middle of the strokes, half a pixel inside, as
  `NS.anchors.walk` gives a drawn rectangle.
- **Image smoothing is left alone.** Measured: the canvas under the picture equals the picture's own pixels (mean
  difference 0.01 to 0.03 grey levels, at most 8 on about 19 pixels of 712,320, from the two JPEG decoders), and it
  is identical with smoothing on and off. The finder's print level differs between the two (for example 64 and 72 on
  one page) only because the canvas also holds the page's white margin.

## Known limits, said plainly

- **Three places are still missed through the dialog** (2 tick boxes on page 1, 1 blank on page 2). The finder finds
  all three as boxes. Today's placement grows a tick-box field by 1 pt and then drops one of two fields that overlap
  by more than 0.5 pt, which loses one of two tick boxes stacked 0.7 pt apart. `placement.js` is plan 04's file.
- **Until plan 04, today's `fromPicture` gives a field to every found box, including one whose `place` is false**
  (it reads `found` and does not look at `place`). On the owner's form every box is a place, so nothing changes
  there; on a scan with filled squares or table cells it would. Plan 04 states the rule ("every box of pg.found
  whose place is not false"); it must not be forgotten.
- **A wide box on a turned scan is not covered.** A box side must keep its distance to within one pixel over 85% of
  its length; at 0.4 degrees that holds for sides up to about 135 pt at 150 dpi. The scan set has only small squares
  on its turned pages. The rule joining does not change how box sides are tested.
- **Free rules that are no place are still reported** (section rules, a page border's lines: 1 to 4 a page on the
  form). The line rules decide them, as planned.
