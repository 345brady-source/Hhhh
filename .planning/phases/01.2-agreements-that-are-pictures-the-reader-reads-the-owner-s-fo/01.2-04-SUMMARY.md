## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] A third party in a box's own caption needs an act.** The plan's O1 counts OTHER_PARTY in any caption. On the owner's form three investor places carry a party's name ("... Account Number" for a custodian, a guardian of a minor beneficiary, a tick box saying the broker will pay), and the answer sheet gives them to the investor. Rule: in a caption a third party decides only with signature, sign, initials, stamp, seal or completed; in a heading the party word counts as it stands. Proved by shapes cases (trap "custodian account number" stays the investor's; "custodian signature" is the custodian's). Commit `78327dd`.

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] A bracketed remark in a caption names nobody.** A caption "(... cannot be same as Investor or Co-Investor)" made a column of investor boxes the co-investor's. Remarks in brackets are dropped from a caption before reading the owner (as `plain()` does for labels on text pages); a heading's remark ("To be completed by custodian") still counts. Shapes case added. `78327dd`.

**3. [Rule 2] A box beside a column over the same rows takes the column's heading (O2).** A stamp panel beside a column of custodian blanks under a column heading was otherwise the investor's. Rule: a box in no column whose top and bottom are within 6 pt of a column's, with no box between, is governed by that column's head. Changes no other place on the form (the local diff of owners before and after showed only that box). Shapes case added. `78327dd`.

**4. [Rule 1 - Bug] A free rule beginning in the margin column is no place.** The plan keeps every proposal on a free rule; the footnote rule on page 5 (inside the margin heading's column) kept the plan-03 stray. Shapes case added. `6aa9335`.

**5. [Rule 1] A sentence over a box is no caption; a one- or two-word party heading over a column is no caption for the name** (it still counts for the owner). Without this a box's name was a paragraph's last line, and the top box of each initials column was named "INVESTOR"/"CO-INVESTOR". `6aa9335`, `78327dd`.

**6. [Rule 1] The verb "initial" vs the adjective, and "signatory".** KIND_WORDS has "initial"; it counts only as a verb (end of caption, or followed by here, each, below, where, to, box, line, page), so "Initial investment amount" stays a typed blank. A read word spelt exactly "signatory", "advisory", "prepared" or "broken" is never a near match. Shapes traps added.

**7. coInvestor keeps the picture kind.** `coInvestor` turns a typed blank whose label holds "date" into a date field (a co-investor's date of birth would become a date). For picture fields the kind decided by K1-K6 is restored after `coInvestor`, and required follows signature/initials.

**8. The tick box's words are its name even when they are a sentence** (the prose rule applies to boxes only).

**9. `NS.pictureWords`** (read-only: PARTY_WORDS, KIND_WORDS, CO_INVESTOR, OTHER_PARTY) is a new key on the namespace so the check can hold the derived lists against the exact patterns without touching the `NS.placement` export line that 01.3-04 also edits.

Check structure.** `picture-fields.js` reads the form through `NS.esign.agreementDraft` and `NS.esignKit.rereadAgreement(env, { pictures: true })` (the offer's action), a second reading in the same workspace for `twice`, and a second browser for `refused` (so no engine is already loaded).
