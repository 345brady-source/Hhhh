## What the three parts share

1. **An owner of "somebody else"** (witness, notary, adviser, custodian, dealer). A, classes 1 to 4; C, classes 8 and 13. One mechanism in `placement.js`: optional, named for that party, never the investor's required field, never filled in from the investor's onboarding, never counted as the investor's or the firm's signature line. Phase 01.2 needs the same owner for its picture form; it is built here.
2. **How far a heading or caption reaches.** A: a heading must carry across a page break. C, class 7: a caption used as one place's own label must not also act as a heading for the places after it. These pull in opposite directions and must be planned and scored together (C's owner differences rise from 34 to 56 once its cells are read and before class 7 is corrected).
3. **The read summary counts printed places, not proposals** (A; B; C class 14): it must never report a signature line that is not printed, must say when the investor has none, must say when only the firm signs, and must account for every place left without a field.
4. **A document the investor does not sign** (B; and A, where no investor signature place is printed). Defaults the owner was shown and did not change: B, the firm signs once and investors do not sign it; A, the sponsor is told plainly that no investor signature place is printed and adds one or uploads the complete document. `agreementTodo` and `templateReady` in `esign.js` need a firm-only path.
5. **Marks only for real doubt (D-09).** A place the reader is sure is somebody else's is shown as theirs with no review mark; the two unclear places of C keep theirs. The scorers' "review allowed" lists are tightened to match before the build.
6. **Every reader change is measured on everything.** C's trial added wrong fields to a public document while the existing judge passed (an empty table cell counts as clean). Field counts per public document (today 38, 0, 0, 64, 58, 0, 147) and the first agreement's 47 are pinned before any reader change.

### What PASS means
Per document: every place of its answer sheet has a field; zero strays; zero overlaps; zero kind differences; zero owner differences; required only where the sheet says a person must complete it; no field filled in from the investor's onboarding on somebody else's place; a review mark only where the sheet says a place is unclear; the read summary true (the detail per document is in each part's own Validation Architecture). A check that cannot find its document prints "NOT RUN" and exits non-zero.
