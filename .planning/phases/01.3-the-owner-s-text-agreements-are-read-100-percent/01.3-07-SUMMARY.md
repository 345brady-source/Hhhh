### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The picture form was told some places had no field when every one had**
- **Found during:** the picture form's full gate after task 2.

The summary then said "Not every printed place got a field: check pages 1, 2 and 5", although 241 of 241 places had fields. The gate passed, but the sentence was false.

- **Files modified:** assets/js/placement.js
- **Commit:** 8809bf3

**2. [Scope] The column-line class was not built as a "place left"**
- The plan lists "a line in a column of captioned lines that has neither a caption nor a label" as a recognised place with no proposal. I built that rule and proved it on a made-up page (`leftSaidLine`, written for the trial and not committed).
- The page showed that the reader already gives such a line a field: optional, marked Review, "say what goes here and who fills it in, or remove it". So the place is not left silently.
- Turning that field into a bare "left" would change placement and fire on no document. I took the rule and the trial page out again.
- The prohibition still holds: no printed place is left without a field silently.
