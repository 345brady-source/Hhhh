<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### The owner's words (verbatim, binding)
- **D-01:** "i need you to get 100% correct on all of these"
- **D-02:** "train the text version and picture version as appropriate": the three text documents are development documents for the normal reader; the picture form for the picture reader (Phase 01.2).
- **D-03:** Earlier, still binding: "every box needs to be tickable, every field needs to be fillable, every signature needs to be signable"; "only add fields to corresponding fields and boxes on the agreement"; notice "where the signers signature is, and where the counter signature is, it may not have either"; "use the gsd planning".

### What 100% means, per document
- **D-04:** For each document an answer sheet is made independently, by looking at every page: every printed place a person completes, with its page, kind, who completes it, its label and its box; and what must not get a field. The answer sheets stay outside the repository.
- **D-05:** Through the real upload dialog, each document gets a field on every place of its answer sheet and no field anywhere else; no two fields overlap; none covers printed words.
- **D-06:** Every field is the right kind (signature, initials, date, amount, percentage, email, phone, tax number, address, text, tick box) and carries the document's own label.
- **D-07:** Every field is for the right person: the investor (and only the right kind of investor where the document separates individuals and entities), the co-investor where the document prints a second investor's places, the firm for its acceptance or countersignature, and third parties (witness, notary, spouse consent, adviser and the like) as nobody's required field: optional and marked.
- **D-08:** Whether the document has an investor signature block and a firm block is recognised correctly, and the read summary says so truthfully. A document that is not signed by an investor at all (for example an agreement signed only by the firm, or with signature pages for partners) is handled as what it is, and the sponsor is told plainly.
- **D-09:** Review marks only where there is real doubt. A document read 100% correctly should not ask the sponsor to confirm fields the reader is sure of.
- **D-10:** An individual investor, an entity investor (where the document provides for one) and the firm can each complete the document end to end, and the finished PDF shows every value on its own place.

### The Word file
- **D-11:** The portal's agreement upload takes PDFs. Document C is judged as the PDF a word processor makes from the Word file. When a sponsor tries to give the portal a Word file as an agreement, the portal says plainly to save it as a PDF and upload that; it never fails silently and never mis-reads it. (Reading Word files directly is not built in this phase.)

### Nothing else breaks
- **D-12:** The owner's first agreement stays at exactly 47 fields with none marked; the seven public documents stay at zero unclean; the proof run and the nine check files stay green; every rule is a convention class, never a rule for one document.
- **D-13:** Proof: an independent score per document against its answer sheet (found, missed, strays, kind and owner differences, overlaps, marked), one picture per page from the portal's own editor looked at, and the end-to-end walk. After the verifier passes, the preview is republished and the owner checks the documents in it. The phase is not complete before that.

### Claude's Discretion
- How each failing convention is fixed in the reader, and how the checks are organised.

</decisions>
