## Summary

Document A is a subscription agreement template written for another investor-portal product. That product adds the investor questionnaire, the investor's signature page and the firm's acceptance page itself, so the PDF prints none of them. The 15 pages of agreement text, the schedule and the blank last page have no place to complete. The only printed places are on two attachment pages, and every one of them is for somebody other than the investor and the firm: page 18 is a letter a professional writes to confirm the investor's status (6 places), page 20 is the details and signatures of the investor's intermediary firm, its representative and its principal, followed by details of a custodian (23 places). The answer sheet has 29 places: 3 signature lines, 3 dates beside them, 23 blanks; 0 tick boxes, 0 initials.

The reader finds 28 of the 29 places today, each on its printed line, with no stray field on any of the 21 pages, no overlap and no field over printed words. Where it fails is who and what: it gives all 28 fields to the investor, makes 20 of them required, treats the letter writer's signature on page 18 as "the investor's signature line", and sets three phone blanks and one address blank to be filled in from the investor's onboarding and locked. As read today, an investor would be required to sign a professional's letter as its author and to complete an intermediary's registration numbers before the agreement counts as signed. The read summary is untrue on the point D-08 cares about most.

The fixes are all convention classes in `placement.js` step 3 (labels above lines), step 5b (lines without a label) and step 6 (whose field it is), one line in the adapter's counting for the summary, and the summary sentences. One of them (the missed place) was tried on a scratch copy and brought the document to 29 of 29 found with no stray and no overlap.

**Primary recommendation:** Teach step 6 of `pageFields` that a block can belong to somebody else (heading on the page, heading carried from the page before, a label that names the other party, a letter signed under a closing word), make every field in such a block optional, marked with the "someone else" reason, never filled in from onboarding and never counted as the investor's signature line; then make the summary say plainly that the PDF prints no signature line for the investor and none for the firm.

## Differences by convention class (cause → fix)

Every line reference below was read in the source this session.

### Class 1 — A block that belongs to somebody else: places under a heading for another party, or whose own label names another party

**Seen on:** page 20, 18 of 22 fields (12 blanks for the intermediary firm, its representative and its contact, and the 6 custodian blanks), plus the missed place. The two dates are class 3.
**Cause.** `placement.js` knows another party in two places only. (a) A label: `classify()` returns `skip` at line 226, quoted verbatim:
```
226:    if (SKIP_HARD.test(t) || (SKIP_SOFT.test(t) && SIGNING_WORDS.test(t) && !/\bauthori[sz]ed\b/.test(t))) return is('skip');
```
so a label with a word of `SKIP_SOFT` (line 156: `custodian|broker|dealer|advis[eo]r|representative of|purchaser representative|registered representative|preparer|attorney|counsel|medallion`) counts only when it also has a signing word. That is why the two signature captions on page 20 were recognised and the 13 blanks whose labels name the same parties were not. (b) A heading: line 1069 pushes a `skip` heading only for `SKIP_HARD` words (witness, notary, spouse, second signer); a heading that names a custodian or an intermediary becomes at most a `blocker`. The words for other parties exist a second time as `OTHERS_RE` (line 1239) with the reason `WHY_OTHERS` (line 1240), but only `fromPicture` uses them, for pages read from a picture.
With no heading and no party, step 6 ends at lines 1160 and 1161: `role = 'investor';` and `sure = !m.block;` so every plain blank is a sure investor field, required by its kind in `KINDS` (lines 176 to 195).
**Fix (class rule).** In step 6, a heading that names another party (the `OTHERS_RE` list) and is set as a heading (capitals, or ends in a colon, or a title line; at most about 8 words; not itself followed by a blank on its line) is a `skip` heading, as a witness heading is today; an "if applicable" in it changes nothing more, the block is optional already. And a label that names another party makes its own blank somebody else's without needing a signing word, when it is a label (ends in a colon or stands directly before its blank), not a sentence. Fields so found get what `second()` (lines 525 to 531) and lines 1124 to 1127 give today (`second: true`, `required: false`, low confidence) with the `WHY_OTHERS` sentence rather than the witness sentence, and lose `prefill`.
**Not fitted to one document:** intermediary and custodian sections are standard attachments to subscription documents; the word list already exists in the reader for picture pages; the picture form of Phase 01.2 has the same sections.

### Class 2 — The heading for a block stands on the page before

**Seen on:** page 20. Its heading (a bold underlined heading in capitals ending in a colon) is on page 19, followed by running text only; the blanks start at the top of page 20.
**Cause.** `c.carry` (lines 1088 and 1089) carries a heading to the next page only when it names a side, a kind of investor or the firm's name, and it must have "nothing under it there" in the sense of `closing` (a block line after it, or the heading low on the page with no place after it). A heading for another party is never carried, and this one is near the top of its page with a page of prose under it.
**Fix (class rule).** The last `skip` heading of a page governs the next page from its top when no place and no other heading follows it on its own page, however much running text does; it stops at the first heading on the next page. Page 20 then needs no word list hit of its own for the 13 intermediary blanks; class 1 covers the custodian heading on page 20 itself.
**Not fitted:** an attachment whose explanation fills one page and whose form starts on the next is a layout class; the carry mechanism is the reader's own.

### Class 3 — The date, printed name and phone lines beside a signature follow that signature

**Seen on:** page 20, the two dates under the two third-party signatures (marked "Could not tell whether the investor or your firm fills this in.", both stored `required: true`); page 18, printed name, date and phone under the letter's signature.
**Cause.** In step 6 a block field (`name`, `date`, `title`) with no heading gets `role = 'investor'; sure = false` and is weakened with `WHY.who` at line 1184. Nothing looks at the signature line right above it. The picture path has this rule (line 1381: a field by a `second` signature becomes `second`); the text path does not.
**Fix (class rule).** A block field in the same column directly under a signature line (no heading and no other signature between, within the block) takes that signature's party: under a `second` signature it is `second`, optional, with the same reason; it is never asked "investor or firm".
**Not fitted:** this is the existing picture-path rule applied to text pages.

### Class 4 — A letter or certificate somebody else writes: a signature under a closing word

**Seen on:** page 18, all 6 fields. This is the worst consequence in the document.
**Cause.** Step 5b, lines 1027 to 1033, quoted verbatim:
```
1028:        const signs = !words && !!lead && /\b(in witness whereof|accepted( and agreed)? by|agreed( and accepted)? by|signed by|sincerely|very truly yours)\b/i.test(lead.s);
1032:        const m = propose(signs ? 'signature' : 'other', sl, null, { seg: name, how: 'after', label: signs ? '' : words.replace(/^[([]\s*|\s*[)\]]$/g, '').replace(/\s*[:：]\s*$/, '') || 'Text', required: signs || (clear && !OPTIONAL_RE.test(words.toLowerCase())) });
1033:        if (!clear || signs) Object.assign(m.p, { conf: 'low', why: WHY.unknown });
```
A bare line under a closing word becomes a signature that is `required: true` while its own reason (`WHY.unknown`, line 298) says "It is optional". Step 6 then makes it the investor's, and `read()` counts it at line 1432 (`if (p.type === 'signature' && !p.second && list.indexOf(p.page) < 0) list.push(p.page);`), which is where "The investor’s signature line was found on page 18" comes from. With a required investor signature in place, `agreementTodo` (esign.js lines 2981 to 2986) lets the sponsor save once the marks are confirmed.
**Fix (class rule), three parts.**
1. A signature the reader could not attribute (`WHY.unknown`) is optional, as its sentence says, and is not counted in `investorPages`. This alone makes the summary true for this document.
2. A signature under a letter closing is the letter writer's. On a page that speaks of the investor in the third person and of the writer in the first person singular, the writer is not the investor: the signature and the block under it (class 3) are somebody else's, as in class 1. Where a heading or the firm's name says whose letter it is, the existing heading rules decide.
3. Every other blank on such a page follows the letter's signature (the two blanks inside the sentence above the closing word), instead of `WHY.sentence` with `required: true`.
**Not fitted:** a verification letter from a professional is a standard attachment for offerings that must verify investor status; letters closed with a closing word and a bare line are a convention; part 1 is a consistency repair of an existing rule.

### Class 5 — A blank on its own line whose label, ending in a colon, stands above it, in a column that also has signature lines with captions under them

**Seen on:** page 20, the one missed place (a full-width underscore line, its label two lines above with an empty line between; two captioned signature lines follow in the same column).
**Cause, confirmed by experiment.** Two rules meet. (a) Step 3 reads a column of unlabelled lines together, "either every label is a caption, or every label is above" (comment at line 863); the column's two known captions decide for captions (line 905), this line has no caption, so line 911 `if (!info || !seg || seg.used) return;` leaves it. Also `over()` (lines 645 to 654) looks at most `Math.max(2.2 * g.h, 16)` pt up, which is 24.3 pt here; the label is 26.6 pt up. (b) Step 5b then drops it at line 1019 because the line is at least 85% of the text width and has no caption (`sl.x1 - sl.x0 >= 0.85 * (rightEdge - leftEdge)`), a rule meant for rules that set off text.
**Fix (class rule).** Per line, inside a caption column: a line with nothing under it, whose nearest text above ends in a colon, starts where the line starts (within 12 pt), is alone on its printed line and is not used, is labelled from above; and a label that ends in a colon may stand one empty line higher (about 3.3 line heights) when nothing lies between it and its line.
**Experiment.** Both changes on a scratch copy of `placement.js` (`A/exp1.diff`, 43 lines; the repository was not touched): the place is found, with the document's own label, kind email, no overlap with the signature field under it (1 pt apart), nothing over printed words; 29 of 29 found, 0 strays on the document. Not run against other documents.
**Not fitted:** "label and colon, then the answer line below" next to captioned signature lines is how most signature pages mix contact details and signatures.

### Class 6 — Filling in from onboarding on a place that is not the investor's

**Seen on:** page 18 phone, page 20 two phones and the first custodian address line: `prefill: 'phone'` and `prefill: 'address'` in the stored fields.
**Cause.** `propose()` copies `K.prefill` from `KINDS` for every field of a kind that has one (line 507); step 6 removes it only for the firm (line 1169: `if (p.prefill !== 'amount') delete p.prefill;`). A field that is somebody else's keeps it; `autoFields` stores it (esign.js line 2918), and the summary promises that such fields "arrive completed and locked".
**Fix (class rule).** A field that is `second` (anybody's but the investor's own) never carries `prefill`; do it in step 6 next to the firm's rule, after classes 1 to 4 have decided whose it is. A co-investor's field keeps what `coInvestor()` gives it today.
**Not fitted:** it is the rule "the investor's details go on the investor's places".

### Class 7 — What the summary says, and what the rest of the portal does with somebody else's field

**Cause.** (a) `readNote` (esign.js lines 2971 and 2972) has two sentences, investor and firm; it has none for other people's places, and none for "the agreement refers to a signature page that the PDF does not contain". (b) The flag is stored and never read: `if (p.second) f.second = true;` (esign.js line 2920) is the only use of `.second` outside `placement.js` in `assets/js` [VERIFIED: grep of `assets/js/*.js` this session]. A third party's field is assigned to the investor signer (`const sg = p.role === 'firm' ? cs : inv;`, line 2910) and shown in the editor as "for Investor (each subscriber)". (c) The counts in the first sentence count them with the rest.
**Fix.** A third sentence when such fields exist, naming the pages: places for other people were found, they are optional and nobody is asked to complete them. When no investor signature line is found and the text speaks of a signature page (or a signature block "below", "attached", "hereto"), say that the agreement refers to a signature page this PDF does not print. The investor sentence stays as it is for "none found". What the investor is shown at signing for a `second` field is for the planner to settle with D-07 ("nobody's required field"): today it is an ordinary optional field of the investor's.

## Common Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1: Calling the document done at "29 of 29 found"
**What goes wrong:** places are found today too (28 of 29); the failure is whose they are. **How to avoid:** PASS needs zero owner, required and prefill differences and a true summary, not only found and strays.

### Pitfall 2: Fixing page 20 by its words
**What goes wrong:** adding this document's label words to a list. **How to avoid:** the rule is the heading (class 1), the carried heading (class 2) and the signature's block (class 3); the word list is the one the reader already has.

### Pitfall 3: The save gate
**What goes wrong:** once the reader stops calling page 18 the investor's signature, `agreementTodo` (esign.js line 2984) blocks saving with "No signature line for the investor was found in this agreement. Add a signature field for the investor where they sign." That is correct, and it means the end-to-end walk of D-10 for this document needs the sponsor to place an investor signature by hand first. **How to avoid:** plan the walk that way (Open question 1).

### Pitfall 4: 29 marks to confirm one by one
**What goes wrong:** every third-party field is marked, and the editor asks the sponsor to confirm each before saving. **How to avoid:** decide with the owner (Open question 2) before building; D-09 and D-07 pull in different directions here.
