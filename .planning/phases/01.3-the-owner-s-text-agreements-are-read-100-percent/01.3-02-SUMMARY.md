## Decisions Made

- **Labels drop a trailing remark.** Rule 1 says "the document's own words"; the pinned pages already read those words without a remark in brackets or after ", if" (grid, the questionnaire), and the reader's `plain()` does the same. The pages follow that convention, so "Country of citizenship (required)" is labelled "Country of citizenship" and "Mailing address (if different)" is labelled "Mailing address". If the owner wants the remark kept, the pinned pages move too; that is a decision for the owner, not for a page.
- **Labels in capitals stay in capitals** ("EMPLOYER", "JOINT INVESTOR TAX ID NUMBER"): the reader has no case rule today and rule 1 says the document's own words.
- **Co-investor places named by a caption carry the caption's words** (rule 1), where today the reader writes "Co-investor ...". Plans 04 and 05 must apply rule 1 to co-investor places as well; the pinned check "Co-investor signature" for a plain signature line is untouched by this (rule 2, plain word).
- **Required, where the plan does not say:** a captioned cell is optional unless its caption prints the word for required (plan 06's precedence); a labelled underscore blank or a captioned rule keeps today's default (required); a blank opened by "If ..." is optional. The qualified labels on `qualifiedLabels` carry no `required` expectation: the plan fixes their label, kind and filling in only.
- **The seal box of `tallBox` is a text field, optional.** A seal cannot be typed honestly either; the owner may prefer no field or a signature there (plan 09).
