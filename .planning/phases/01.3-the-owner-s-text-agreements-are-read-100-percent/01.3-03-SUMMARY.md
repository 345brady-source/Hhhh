## Decisions Made

- The bound of a heading for another party: it governs a place only when no other governing heading (a named block or a blocker heading) stands between them; a line's own label does not bound it. A line of its own for a second signer, a witness or a notary under such a heading stays theirs, marked as today; one whose own label names a third party takes the heading's party.
- Label rule 1 is applied to such places of a kind the portal knows (phone, address, name, date, signature and the like) from the label's own words, a trailing remark in brackets or after ", if" and the colon removed, case kept (the convention plan 02 recorded). A place of the catch-all kind already carries the printed words.
- The places of somebody else lend no side to a blank on the same printed line: each finds its own heading.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The spoken name broke every reader of the editor**
- **Found during:** Task 1, first run of the measures (A label differences 8 to 11, C 12 to 15)
- **Issue:** The plan puts "for someone else (Party)" into `reviewSay`, after the page. With "for <signer>" dropped before the page, the proof's editor reader (`"<label> for <whom>, page N"`) could no longer read the label or the owner, so the scorers saw empty labels; keeping "for Investor" there instead would have told a screen-reader user two owners.
- **Fix:** the words stand where every field names its signer: "<label> for someone else (<Party>), page N, optional". `reviewSay` does not repeat them.
- **For phase 01.2:** its plan 04 says the words come "after the page, through reviewSay"; it checks only that they are in the spoken name, which holds. The scorer `score-notext.js` reads "someone else" anywhere and the class `is-else`; both hold for every such field.
- **Commit:** `53fb418`

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] An address's third line kept the investor**
- **Found during:** Task 1, first `--only otherHeading`
- **Issue:** On an address's continuation lines `m.second` means "the line after the second", not a second signer's line, so the rule skipped the last line.
- **Fix:** the rule reads `p.second` (the proposal's flag). The existing witness-heading branch has the same `!m.second` test (a third address line under a witness heading is not marked second); it is older than this plan, not changed, and reported below.
- **Commit:** `7dc8498`

**3. [Rule 3 - Blocking] The field's colour cannot be set by a class**
- **Issue:** the editor writes each field's `--sc` in its inline style, so `.fe .es-fld.is-else { --sc: ... }` alone cannot win.
- **Fix:** `editorFieldHTML` passes `var(--es-paper-ink-2)` as such a field's colour; the CSS keeps the declaration and gives the optional edge and wash, never over a review mark or the selection.

**4. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] The sponsor could not hand such a place to the investor**
- **Issue:** the place is already on the investor's signer, so the signer list offered no change, while `WHY.otherUnsure` tells the sponsor to "set it to the investor".
- **Fix:** the signer list shows "Someone else (Party)" selected for such a field; choosing any signer, the investor included, makes it that signer's ordinary field (`second` and `other` removed, mark settled). Proved in the browser: the field lost both flags, the rail went from 3 to 4 investor fields and from 6 to 5 places for someone else, and `fieldsOf` returned 4. The choices "Filled in from onboarding" and "Who gets this field" are not shown on such a field.

**5. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] A later reading must know such a field was changed**
- **Fix:** `untouched()` compares `other` with `placed.other`, so a field the sponsor gave to a signer is kept on "Read the PDF again".
