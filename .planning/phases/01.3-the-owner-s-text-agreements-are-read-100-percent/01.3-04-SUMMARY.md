## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The wider reach of `over` broke a guard page**
- **Found during:** Task 2. With the colon label's reach in `over()`, a heading ending in a colon over a limited partner's block was read as the label above its first line, and partnerPage (guard) got a mark.
- **Fix:** the taller reach lives in its own helper `overFar`, used only by the column rule of step 3 for a line with nothing under it; `over()` is as before. One empty line is measured on the page's spacing (3.3 font heights, or 2.25 steps to the line above), because the class page sets its paragraphs apart by a space.
- **Commit:** `8c60c36`

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] A paragraph's last line ending in a company's name was a heading**
- **Found during:** Task 1, document A. The last line of a paragraph on page 19 ends in a company suffix and was taken for a company heading; carried to page 20 it bounded the heading for another party. Also, two run-on lines of a list on page 17 were taken for headings for another party and carried to page 18.
- **Fix:** a run-on line (the line above close, with no stop, reaching the right edge) is never a company heading nor a heading for another party, and a heading for another party never opens with a small letter.
- **Commit:** `403a8b2`

**3. [Rule 3 - Blocking] The class page's heading has 10 words**
- **Fix:** a heading for another party set in capitals and ending in a colon may run to 12 words (8 otherwise, as plan 03 had it).

**4. [Rule 1 - Bug] Labels were cut at 60 characters, and 60 is pinned**
- **Issue:** a two-line caption is 71 and 89 characters on the pages measured; raising the cut for every label moved four pinned labels of the first agreement.
- **Fix:** only labels in the document's own words (rules 1 and 2) and two-line captions may run to 90 (`cleanFull`, a flag removed in `read()`); every other label stays at 60. The editor's label input takes 90.

**5. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] A label naming a custodian and the investor's side**
- **Found during:** proof5 (public document 1). "Name of trustee or custodian" was made a sure third party's place; the trustee can be the investor's own signatory.
- **Fix:** such a place is somebody else's but marked with the existing "may be for someone else" reason; a heading for the party settles it.

**6. [Rule 1 - Bug] The amount inside a sentence lost its standard word and filling in**
- **Found during:** proof7 (accuracy, labels, review red). `classify` reads words of money before a currency sign as an amount; rule 2 then took the sentence for a label.
- **Fix:** that branch marks its result `sentence`; it is never a label's own words. Nine check files green after.

**7. [Rule 1 - Bug] Labels that say only the investor were called qualified**
- **Found during:** proof5 diff (public document 4): "Signature of Prospective Investor", "My U.S. taxpayer identification ... number is".
- **Fix:** "prospective", "my", "our" are the investor's words; "is", "are" are filler; "U.S." is a word of a tax number. Also a page number printed on a label's line ("-13-") is no part of its words.

**8. [Rule 1 - Bug] A letter's subject line governed the letter's places**
- **Found during:** Task 1, letterClosing. A subject line naming the investor in the third person was a heading for the investor.
- **Fix:** on a letter page every place not given to the firm, a co-investor or a third party becomes the writer's.
