## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] A heading right over a grid row was taken for a caption**
- **Found during:** Task 1, page acceptanceOtherParty: the heading stands directly over the row's empty signature cell, so the first form of captionCell silenced it and the block lost its heading.
- **Fix:** a line that also stands over printed words in its cell's row heads a table, not a caption.
- **Commit:** `ac3df93`

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] A footnote mark line hid the first item of a list**
- **Found during:** Task 2, document C: superscript footnote marks on an item form a printed line of their own between the lead-in and its first item.
- **Fix:** a line of marks set smaller than the lead-in is passed over when looking for the list's first item.
- **Commit:** `830eb89`

**3. [Rule 1 - Bug] Short signature lines counted as section rules on a narrow page**
- **Found during:** Task 1, page otherHeadingCarried (red after the first form of the section rule): with only short lines of text on a page, a 3-inch signature line spans 85 percent of the text's width.
- **Fix:** a section rule spans at least 60 percent of the page as well, carries no field, and has no words on its line.
- **Commit:** `ac3df93`

**4. [Rule 1 - Bug] The firm-block prototype made labels.js checks 1 and 2 sure**
- **Found during:** Task 3: the prototype's `sure` held for a company name with no heading of its own under an investor heading two blocks up.
- **Fix:** sure only when the block's own heading (the nearest above the name) has no other signature line between it and the line (the plan's instruction: narrow the rule, never the check).
- **Commit:** `f004460`

### Other differences from the plan's text

- **captionCell reaches "about one line height" as 1.6 font heights plus 2 pt**, and walks down a caption of up to three lines (same left edge, same middle, or within the line above's width, size within 1.2 pt). The plan's class pages put their captions in the cell to the left, where the label rule already marks the line used; the rule over a cell is proved on document C and on the new page `captionOverCell`. On that page the cells get no field until plan 06 reads cells under captions: **plan 06 must give each of its two cells its expectation** (the comment on the page says so).
- **List mark without its bracket:** the plan says "the same left edge and the same width (within 2 pt)". On the plan's own page the misprinted mark is narrower than "(a)", so its blank starts 8 pt to the left of the others; the rule takes the same width and either the same blank edge (within 2 pt) or the same mark edge (within 4 pt). Document C has the same blank edge.
- **"Other than a natural person"** (and "not an individual", "non-individual") is read as entities by the lead-in rule; `headType` alone sees both words and says neither.
- **The section rule also stops every heading**, as the plan says ("in addition to today's limits"); no reading outside this plan's pages changed.
- **The two new class pages** are in `class-pages.js`, which is in the plan's files; `classes.js` needed no change.
