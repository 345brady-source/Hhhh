### What is put to the owner (defaults in force until the owner says otherwise)

1. Document A prints no place for the investor or the firm to sign: the portal says so, and the sponsor adds the investor's signature field by hand or uploads the complete document.
2. Document B is signed only by the firm: the firm signs once, investors do not sign it, and investors are not shown the signed copy unless the firm publishes it.
3. Document C, page 10: the acceptance block names another party: it is marked, optional and nobody's until the sponsor sets it to the firm.
4. A place the portal is sure is for someone else (a custodian, an adviser, a letter's writer) is shown as theirs, optional, with no review mark and no confirmation; nobody is asked to complete it.
5. Document C: captions that print "required" inside sections for only some investors are optional; the stamp box on page 3 is somebody else's place.
6. A line for a witness, a notary, a spouse or a second signer keeps the review mark it has today (existing checks pin it). Should those be unmarked too when the portal is sure?
7. The investor's own fields of known kinds (name, address, phone, tax number, date) keep the portal's words as labels; every other field carries the document's own label. Should the document's words be used on those too?
8. The Word file: judged as the PDF made from it here; the sentence shown for a Word file calls it a "Word file". A PDF of document C saved by Word itself would let that route be scored too.
9. Known gap, not changed: words printed in the paper's colour are read as visible words.
