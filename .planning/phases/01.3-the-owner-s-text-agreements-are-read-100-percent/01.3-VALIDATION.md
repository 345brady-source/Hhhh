# Phase 01.3 — Validation Strategy

> Source: `01.3-RESEARCH.md` § Validation Architecture and the same section of parts A, B and C. The planner fills the per-task map.

## Test Infrastructure

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Framework** | Node scripts with Playwright through the real upload dialog; one scorer per owner document, the proof runner, the nine check files. Scorers and documents are kept outside the repository |
| **Config file** | none |
| **Quick run command** | the scorer of the document a task touches (`node score.js --root <checkout>`), 10 to 40 seconds |
| **Full suite command** | the three scorers, document B's five made-up variants, proof steps 1,3,4,5,6 with pinned field counts, the nine check files |
| **Estimated runtime** | quick (one scorer): 10 to 40 seconds, measured; a task's whole verify: about 2 to 5 minutes; a plan's verification with proof steps 5 and 7: about 10 to 20 minutes (the last two are estimates; plan 01 records the measured time of each measure) |

A check that needs a document and does not find it prints "NOT RUN" and exits non-zero. "NOT RUN" is never green.

## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** the scorer of each document the task touches, and proof step 1
- **After every plan wave:** the three scorers, proof steps 1 and 5 with pinned counts, the nine check files
- **Before verification:** everything green, every compare picture looked at
- **Max feedback latency:** the 60-second target holds for the quick command only (scorer C about 10 s, B about 20 s, A about 40 s, measured in the research). A task's whole verify (the scorers it touches, proof step 1, its class pages) takes about 2 to 5 minutes; a plan's verification, with proof step 5 and the nine check files, about 10 to 20 minutes. These two figures are estimates until plan 01 records the measured time of each measure in its SUMMARY
