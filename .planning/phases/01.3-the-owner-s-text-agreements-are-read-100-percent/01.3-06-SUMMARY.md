## The guard as built

- **openPaper:** text whose middle lies inside a ruled cell that has words in it is table content, never a caption. anchors.js now reports those cells in `filled` (`{ x0, x1, y0, y1 }`).
- **labelLike:** a caption is at most 16 words and 90 characters. It has letters, no list bullet, no sentence stop at its end, and no figure, amount, percentage or range.
- Step 2b (a cell named from the cell to its left) uses labelLike as well. This covers the carry-forward case: an empty cell to the right of a figure no longer gets a field labelled with that figure.
- An empty cell that 2b rejects is only soft-skipped (`rowSkip`). A caption over it can still name it, and if none does it is skipped as before.
- The research's third condition ("every cell of the row empty") was not needed. No public document gained a field: totals stay 38, 0, 0, 64, 58, 0 and 147, and the 38-field memorandum is unchanged field for field.
