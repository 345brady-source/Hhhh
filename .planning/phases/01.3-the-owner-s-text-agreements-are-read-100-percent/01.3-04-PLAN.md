<assumption_delta_decision>
- Noun: who completes a printed place (the owner of a field). Until now every field had a signer who completes it (the investor, the co-investor or the firm).
- Decision: add-alongside. A place for somebody else stays attached to the investor's signer and carries `second` and `other`; `NS.placement.isOther` is the single test, and `fieldsOf` in esign.js is the single seam that keeps such a field out of every signer's work.
- Rationale: the portal has one signer per investor by design and D-07 asks for nobody's required field, not for a new signer; the picture reader already emits exactly this shape.
- Accepted debt: the owner is not a first-class property. What would force a promote: a later phase that lets a third party sign in the portal (v2 SGNR-01), which needs a real signer for them.
</assumption_delta_decision>
