**The rule behind each figure and each refusal.**
  Box 1 and box 4; every other box empty: Instructions for Forms 1099-INT and 1099-OID (Rev. January 2024); D-02.
  $10: the same instructions, for interest on notes in registered form or of a type offered to the public; any amount when backup withholding was taken. The sponsor confirms the notes are of that kind; otherwise the threshold is another one ($2,000 from tax year 2026, Publication 1099 "What's New") and the portal stops and names the CPA.
  Left out: the instructions' "Exempt recipients" (an IRA, a corporation the sponsor marks); a foreign payee gets Form 1042-S.
  Interest added to principal, or notes issued at a discount: that is original issue discount, Form 1099-OID, which this phase does not prepare: stop.
  The name and number on the form are the W-9's: Publication 1099 (2026) part J; the recipient's number may be truncated on the recipient's copy and never on what goes to the IRS; the payer's number is never truncated.
  Copy B: the notice on Form 1099-INT lets the black copies be downloaded and used for the recipient.
  The upload file: Publication 5717 (Rev. 9-2026) and the IRS's "1099-INT Template Formatting Guidelines" (March 6, 2025, in the file updated January 14, 2026).

**Planner's choices (the owner may override).** (1) The payer's own nine-digit number is typed each time a file or a copy is built and is not stored either; only its last four are kept, to catch a mistyped one. The owner may decide the fund's own number can be saved. (2) An amount is written with two decimals (assumption A3). (3) The 1099 forms have their own folder, map table and data file, so the phase 6 form checks keep their counts. (4) The account number box and every box other than 1 and 4 stay empty.
