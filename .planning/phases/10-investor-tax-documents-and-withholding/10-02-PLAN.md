The rule for a proposal (the one definition; among investors with a position in the offering, redeemed or not):
  N = investors whose last four on record (taxCert.tinLast4, else tinLast4) equal the package's last four, when it has one. M = investors whose name agrees: the words of the first line of item F, lower-cased, without punctuation and without llc, lp, inc, co, the, of, fbo, ttee, jr, sr, equal as a set to the words of the account title or of the contact name.
  Exactly one investor in both N and M: proposed, 'number and name'. Otherwise N holds exactly one and M is empty: proposed, 'number only'. Otherwise the package has no number and M holds exactly one: proposed, 'name only'. Anything else: no proposal.
A K-1 first page carries the code 651123, "Schedule K-1", "Form 1065" and "Information About the Partner". Item E is what lies between the label "Partner's SSN or TIN" and the label that starts "Name, address, city, state, and ZIP code for partner", item F between that label and "General partner or LLC", both in the left half of the page, from text items and field values together. The codes 661117 and 671124 (other K-1s) are not matched automatically.
