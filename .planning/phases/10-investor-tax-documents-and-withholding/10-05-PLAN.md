**The rule behind each thing the module holds.**
  Reporting type: the portal cannot tell how a vehicle is taxed, and income reported on a Schedule K-1 must not be repeated on a Form 1099 (Publication 1099 (2026), part B).
  Backup withholding, 24%: Publication 1099 (2026), part N: only on payments reportable on a 1099, on the listed conditions.
  Foreign, 30%: Publication 515 (2026): the withholding agent withholds on the part of a payment that is U.S.-source income of the kinds subject to it; which part that is, and any lower rate, is a determination the portal does not make.
  W-8 facts: Instructions for the Requester of Forms W-8 (Rev. June 2022): signed; valid to the last day of the third calendar year after signing; a foreign tax number or the box saying none is legally required.
  Deposits: Instructions for Form 945 (2026) and for Form 1042 (2025): the withholder deposits; the portal records what the sponsor says was deposited.
  IRS notice about a payee: Publication 1099 part N: the sponsor records the date it arrived.

**Planner's choices (the owner may override).** (1) An amount of tax is worked in whole numbers and half a cent rounds up; the sponsor sees and confirms the amount. (2) Research assumption A6 (no minimum payment before backup withholding on interest or dividends) is used and named in the summary as unconfirmed. (3) A foreign investor whose W-8 is unsigned or expired is not treated as documented: the first backup condition then applies to them, with the reason saying which. (4) The module states each deposit rule as one sentence with its source and computes no date (C-08).
