**Why each sample entry balances (the arithmetic the case checks, written once).** For a month k, the other entries move equity by contributions - distributions - general partner distributions - fees, and the Audit page's own check ('nav', audit.js line 971-973) already holds H.totalAssets - H.totalLiabilities = F.endCap + F.gpEnd for every sample fund. The engine's month adds income + gain - fee to net assets. So target assets - target liabilities - (journal net assets before the entry) = income + gain, which is exactly what the P&L lines credit: the entry sums to zero. If a sample fund's 'nav' check fails for some month, that month's entry cannot balance and is reported: the executor reports which fund and month and does not force it.
Example to verify by hand in the summary: the first month of kb-fund-ii, with every line of its sample entry and the sum 0, copied from the check's output.
