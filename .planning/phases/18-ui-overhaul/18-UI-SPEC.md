# Phase 18 — UI Design Contract: the regal look (combined direction, owner-approved)

> Visual and interaction contract for the whole portal: the sponsor console in both editions, the investor portal,
> onboarding and invitations, e-signature (sender, signer, field editor), the report builder, sign-in, the help assistant
> and every dialog. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker. Locked inputs: 18-CONTEXT.md D-01 to
> D-17. Where this contract and a locked decision disagree, the decision wins.

**What the owner approved (D-17, "ok this is good"):** the mockups in `.planning/phases/18-ui-overhaul/mockups/combined/`
as they stand: direction A's layout and fonts (D-13, D-14), direction B's colours exactly (D-13), every content section a
complete panel with an underlay in the manner of the portal's earlier Refract look (D-15), the investor's "Ask me
anything" in the tab row (D-16), and the 24 new sponsor marks (D-10). Rejected and kept only for the record:
`mockups/rejected-1/` (first direction, D-09), `mockups/rejected-2/` (direction A as it stood), `mockups/direction-b/`
(B's layout; its colours are the source of the palette below).

**The brief in one line.** A private bank's annual report on a navy frame: Brygada 1918 for what is said, Ysabeau for what
is done, ledger tables with italic sub-lines and a double rule under every total, each section a clear white panel lying
on a cool-white page with a soft steel-tinted shadow. Every word, step, rule, number and route stays as it is (D-02);
only the look and three placements change: the help entry (D-04, D-16), the Offerings/Offering intake merge (D-05) and
the console's top bar that holds the help entry.

## Typography

Four sizes. Two weights of the text face (400, 600); the display face and the label face are each used at one weight
(500), so the system has one regular, one medium (display/labels) and one semibold (text emphasis).

| Role | Size | Weight | Line height | Face | Used for |
|------|------|--------|-------------|------|----------|
| Label | 14px | 500 (Ysabeau SC) / 400 and 600 (Ysabeau Office) | 1.3 (labels) / 1.5 (text) | Ysabeau SC for column heads, group names, figure labels, phone statement labels; Ysabeau Office for hints, meta, buttons, the console's dense text | |
| Body | 16px | 400, 600 for emphasis and totals | 1.5 | Ysabeau Office; ledger row names, notice subjects and document titles at 16px in Brygada 500 | body text, every input/select/textarea (16px stops iOS zoom), menu items, investor tabs (Ysabeau SC 500) |
| Heading | 24px | 500 | 1.2 | Brygada 1918 | panel headings, dialog titles, offering names |
| Display | 40px | 500 | 1.1 | Brygada 1918 | page titles; headline figures scale between 20px and 40px to fit their cell (container query, never wrap, never touch) |

- Italic Brygada 400 at 14px or 16px for: the page sub-line ("3 offerings · 24 investors"), ledger sub-lines, "As of …",
  the Ask control's "Ask me anything", the "Total value …" line. Never one italic word inside a roman heading.
- Figures: `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums` on every amount, count, date and percentage in a table or
  figure row; numeric cells right-aligned and `nowrap`. Amounts keep today's text exactly (currency format, minus sign,
  "—" for empty, rounding).
- No tracking anywhere; no capitals-only styling (small caps come from the Ysabeau SC face, so `innerText` stays the
  words as written: checks read it).
- Headings `text-wrap: balance`, paragraphs `text-wrap: pretty`, display heads `overflow-wrap: break-word; min-width: 0`.
- Exceptions (documented, nowhere else): **12px** for menu and bell counts, the legal line, chart axis and value labels;
  **32px** page titles at 640px and under; the lockup's wordmark is part of the logo (20px in the console bar, 24px in
  the investor masthead), not the type scale.
