<objective>
Asking an investor to re-verify accreditation (D-04: 506(c) accreditation re-checks about every 90 days while an offering is open; orchestrator choice C-06 in 08-CONTEXT.md: checked at each new subscription with a 90-day validity, no calendar re-checks). The research found the rule needs verification at each sale, a third party's letter is good for three months, and the portal already expires a verification after the firm's validity days and blocks a new Rule 506(c) subscription; what is missing is a way to ask (research gap 11). Built like plan 06-26's tax-form request.

Purpose: when a verification lapses the sponsor today has no button to press.
Output: the button and dialog in the console, requestReverify, the single-step link in onboarding, the server's flag, email wording and tier, tests and the check reverify.js.
