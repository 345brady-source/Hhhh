<objective>
Regulation A Tier 2's 10 percent investment limit (D-01) with the tier split (C-04): the offering page separates Tier 1, Tier 2 and exchange-listed Tier 2; a non-accredited Tier 2 investor self-certifies income and net worth (or revenue and net assets) on the status step, sees their limit, and is warned on the subscribe step when the commitment is above it, without being blocked (C-01); the sponsor sees every over-limit subscription.

Purpose: a Reg A Tier 2 sponsor must reasonably rely on each non-accredited purchaser's representation that the purchase is within 10 percent of the greater of income or net worth; this plan collects that representation and shows the sponsor where it fails.
Output: tier values and the untiered rule, the Tier 2 branch of the status step, the subscribe-step limit line and warning, the console section and request flag, new check parts.
</objective>
