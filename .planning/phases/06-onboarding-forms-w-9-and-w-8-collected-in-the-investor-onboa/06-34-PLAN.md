The buildTax comment (lines 2290-2292) says a signed earlier form stays only "until the new one is finished", but no code does that; a "Request tax form" opens a new flow (fl.taxEnvelopeId empty), so buildTax never sees the earlier form, and voidUnsignedTax (2650-2658) skips it anyway because it is 'completed', not 'sent'.
3. Consequence also on the other path: an investor with a signed form on file who finishes a new form unsigned (an invitation with "Include tax form", D-10) has i.taxCert replaced by the unsigned one, so the record says "not signed" while a signed form exists.
4. The console's server-mode import (invitations.js merge, lines 1144-1153) lists each imported signed tax form beside any earlier one the same way.

**Decisions (the owner may override; recorded in the SUMMARY):**
- One tax form per investor in Documents and the record. A later form replaces the earlier one; a form finished without a signature never replaces a signed one on file (the unsigned new one is the one retired, and the record stays on the signed one). Reason: the walkthrough's N1 and the orchestrator's rule "the record should hold one signed W-9, the signed one"; a signed certification on file is legally stronger than an unsigned one.
- A tax form finished without the investor's signature is not published to Documents at all (it is not a signed document; server mode already never sends it, plan 06-22, and the console already offers no download for it). The record, the investor's Account page and the console keep saying "W-9 not signed" as today.
- Retiring an envelope = remove its ws.docs listing, delete its stored files (orig, work, signed: they hold the full taxpayer ID, research Pitfall 2), mark it voided with a reason the way resign() does (esign.js 3099-3101: status 'voided', voidedAt, voidReason, addEvent, logChange), and one audit line. Reasons: "Replaced by the Form W-9 finished on <date>" (or signed on), and for an unsigned form not replacing a signed one "Finished without a signature; the signed Form W-9 on file stays".
- An uploaded form (plan 06-21) is left as it is (its own rule in storeTaxUpload); not part of N1.
- Item 8 (failure 1 remainder): on the agreement step, when this browser did not keep the agreement's file, a primary "Continue and sign later" sits beside Back. It finishes the flow with the agreement unsigned (nothing is signed without the pages being shown), logs an audit line and notifies the firm; the done screen says what happens next. When the firm saves the agreement again from the offering page, every unsigned subscription agreement of that offering whose file this browser no longer has is re-issued from the saved agreement (prefilled as before), the old envelope voided with the reason. The smallest change that leaves nobody stuck: no new storage layer (06-32: no storage layer was added), no change to the honest messages.
