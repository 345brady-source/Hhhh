<objective>
State notice filings (D-03): the states picker, the order with its amount due, the package staff file from by hand, per-state results and the state and renewal reminders (D-04), as one section of the Filings tab in its own files.

The state fee table cannot be filled in this plan: the official fee matrix could not be reached from this session, and a fee written from memory would be wrong somewhere and cost a sponsor money. So every row starts unconfirmed and shows no amount; the screen says fees are confirmed when the order is reviewed. Plan 09-10 fills the rows from the official matrix once the owner supplies it.

Purpose: a sponsor can order their state notice filings and be reminded of renewals now; staff get everything they need to file by hand.
Output: assets/js/bluesky-data.js, assets/js/bluesky.js, checks/bluesky-math.js (6 cases), checks/bluesky-flow.js (4 parts).
</objective>
