**What D-02 asked for and what the SEC allows.** D-02 says "pre-filled Form ID ready to notarize". The SEC accepts Form ID only when completed online on its dashboard, and the notarized document must be the printout of that online form (research, "What could not be established" item 4, from the SEC's Form ID guide). The owner's provisional choice, which this plan builds: the sponsor completes Form ID on the SEC dashboard from the portal's answer sheet. No power-of-attorney wording is part of this phase.

**Planner's choices (the owner may override).** (1) The sheet does not collect the issuer's tax ID or anyone's Login.gov details; it tells the sponsor what to have ready. (2) The filing agent's CIK is read from window.CVPortalConfig.filingAgent.cik where the portal is published; Carrow & Vale has none until the SEC grants its own application, and until one is configured the delegation step says the number is sent when the order is reviewed. (3) The order of parts and the cautions come from the SEC's Form ID guide; the field-by-field instructions PDF on the SEC's filer management site could not be reached, so the order of fields inside each part is proven against the guide only, and the summary says so first.
