## Rules shipped (all class rules, designed on train documents only)

- **Fallback glyphs:**
  - A lone U+00A8 in any font is a box.
  - A lone q, o or U+00A8 in a symbol font is a box, and so is an unmapped control code before an option's words.
  - A lone o, p, q, r, c, i or apostrophe in a text font is a box only in a choice position. That means another such option stands at the same left edge (past indented run-on lines), or is set apart on the same line by 12 pt or two spaces.
  - Not a box: a row of lone letters (a comb), a fixed-pitch font, a sub-list under a bulleted line, or a sentence of more than 12 words (its run-on lines included). The last one still counts as a box if the lines over the list ask for a choice or its own words say "check this box".
- **Typed `|_|`** is a tick box.
- **Short-rule choices:** a rule under 15 pt that opens an option's line, in a group of two or more at one left edge, is a tick place, sure, whatever the words say.
- **Captions on one drawn rule:** captions standing on the rule, or small captions up to 6 pt over it, cut the rule into one blank per free stretch of 18 pt or more after a caption. This never applies to a sentence, a numbered or capital heading, words larger than the body, or text that runs on from before the rule.
- **Hyphen runs:** 6 or more hyphens or en or em dashes, 24 pt or longer, are a blank. Not a blank:
  - a separator across the column;
  - a heading's underline;
  - a banner framing a capital heading;
  - a table's column rule over figures;
  - a run that touches + or |;
  - a leader to a page number.
- **A line right under a question** (ending "?" or opening "Describe/Explain/List…") is a place however wide it is.
