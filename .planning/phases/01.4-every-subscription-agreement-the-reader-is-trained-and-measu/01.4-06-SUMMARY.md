## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The shared rate lock lost its timestamp under concurrent writers**
- Found during: Task 1
- Issue: the lock file's timestamp was written buffered and the lock was released before the write reached disk, so two processes could read an empty or merged stamp. The set-b sec.py has the same flaw. In the first minutes this produced up to 6 requests in a second, in 16 one-second windows. That is still under SEC's 10-a-second limit, but over the plan's 3 a second.
- Fix: flush before unlock; an unreadable stamp now means waiting the full gap. Request start times are logged since then. After the fix: at most 3 requests in any second (10,186 requests measured).

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Module path order would have loaded the set-b client**
- Found during: Task 2 (build and appendix scripts)
- Fix: the set-b helper folder is appended after our own scripts folder, so our sec.py, cache and log are used. Caught before any document was built with it.

**3. [Rule 2 - Held-out hygiene] Stopped reading set-a/set-b document folders**
- Issue: an early trial of pick_c.py hashed set-b source files to deduplicate. It ran before the 00:58 seal, and the guard still passes. Reading those folders goes against the executor note added during the run.
- Fix: issuers and hashes now come from set-a/index.json and set-b/index.json only (setc-work/known.json). Every set-a/set-b issuer is excluded, so no issuer-and-sha pair can repeat.

**4. [Rule 2] Ten broker-dealer forms made of page pictures were printed by the owner's method**
- These are picture forms. They are now printed in landscape with background graphics and recorded as `picture-print` (collected_as stays `bd`). This raised the scan/picture count from 45 to 55. It was done before B was chosen.

**5. A filer's contact address removed from a candidate record**
- One filer's contact address had come in with a document's title text in `dl_pic.jsonl`. It was removed, so setc-work logs and records now hold only the generic placeholder address. The downloaded filings themselves still carry issuers' public contact details.

**Total deviations:** 5 (2 bugs, 2 correctness/hygiene, 1 cleanup). **Impact:** none on the frozen sets. The scan shortfall is not a deviation fix. It is an unmet minimum, reported above.
