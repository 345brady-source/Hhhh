<objective>
Label set-e automatically where independent readers agree, prove on hand-sheeted train documents how far those labels can be trusted, and size the one review tranche.

Purpose: hand sheets cost about 50k tokens a document and cannot scale to 2,000; agreement plus a measured trust rule and a small review sample can. Expected cost per review tranche (300k cap): at a disagreement share of 10 percent about 8k tokens a document (about 37 documents), at 20 percent about 13k (about 23), at 30 percent about 18k (about 16); reviewing all 2,000 at 20 percent would be about 87 tranches (about 26M tokens), so one sampled tranche is planned and any more is the owner's choice with these measured numbers.
Output: set-e labels, quality-e.md, trust.json, tranches.json (ET01), spot.py.
</objective>
