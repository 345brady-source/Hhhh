What each document needed:
- **c040**: every pre-fill candidate (179) was a false hit: "o"/"e" letters and underlined headings in the pictured text. The real places were found by detecting strokes in the page pictures and mapped through each PDF page's drawing matrix. The page clip was checked on a rendered page break. The places are:
  - the investor signature page;
  - two non-U.S. holder tax certificates (exhibits signed before closing);
  - the issuer acceptance blocks, which are already signed and so are not places.
- **c011**: 239 pre-fill boxes on scanned letters were deleted. The places are on the signature pages:
  - two signature lines whose captions are printed on the next page;
  - a joint-subscriber column;
  - the company acceptance block;
  - one telephone place with no printed line.
- **c089**: 47 tick boxes drawn as font glyphs. 30 initials places were added on the acknowledgement page: each initials line is split at the printed bar into investor and co-investor. Row cells were split at their captions.
- **c062**: a dotted-line text form. 9 places were added: names, title, dates, the advisor's signature and phone, and the segmented account number (one place).
- **c181**: these were added:
  - 15 tick boxes printed as pictures (8 accreditation, 7 on the questionnaire);
  - 7 alternative-distribution rows with no printed line;
  - Name and Title places after each By line.

  Three boxes printed already checked were left out.
