Joins the reader's fields with the measure's counted candidates by GROW 3 / SHARE 0.4: a pair whose kinds fall in the same family (tick; blank, name and date; signature and initials) is accepted as a place with the reader's kind, the candidate's snapped box and `auto: true`; a reader field with no candidate, a candidate with no field, a kind clash, and a reader label that differs by nl() from the printed caption words (pdfplumber words before or under the place) each become one item in `adjudicate/<id>/items.json` with a crop PNG (the page at 100 dpi, 120 pt around the item, the item outlined). Places are grouped into signature blocks (a block runs from a heading or signature caption to the next heading or a 24 pt gap); each block becomes one person item (a crop of its heading and places) instead of one item per place. adjudicate.py shows the next item (crop path and the question), records answers, applies a block's person to all its places, and `done` writes `truth/<id>/truth.json` with `source: bootstrap`, runs check_sheet.py and records its hash. batches.json: BT01 to BT04 = the set-a/set-b train ids outside the tranche in four batches of about 36; AS01, AS02 = manifest-c `as` batches (25 each). PROCEDURE-adjudicate.md: the steps and the rule "decide from the crop and, if unclear, the page; never from what the reader did".
