# Phase 01.4 Plan 08: The tracer place model Summary

**GATE RED FIRST.** The held-out A exact gate fails on two rate columns, not on counts:
`EXACT GATE A: FAIL - kindRightPct best 89.36 now 89.29; markedPct best 65.83 now 65.93`.
The model added 7 fields on held-out A documents. All 7 are on answer-sheet places (found 1786 to 1793, strays 517 to 517, fixes 3693 to 3693). 2 of the 7 have the wrong kind (kindDiff 190 to 192). Every new field is marked for review, like every picture field. These fields pull the two rates down. Any new picture field moves markedPct up while picture fields are always marked, so this gate can only stay green if the model adds no field. An owner or orchestrator decision is needed (see "Decision needed").

The other failures:

- **The must-have "on a006 and b042 the measure's missed places fall" is not met.**
  - a006 stays at 128 missed and b042 at 106. Their fields are identical with and without the model.
  - I looked at the pages. About 120 of a006's 128 missed "tick boxes" are the insides of letter O's in the scanned text: the measure's pixel finder takes them for boxes. Most of b042's misses are 0.6 pt glyph boxes of the hidden text layer, or edges of grey panels.
  - Covering those would put wrong fields on the page. The model does not do that, and its snapping would drop such places anyway.
- **The first whole-set run failed worse.** Before the writing-place fix (commit f1b3911), held-out strays went from 517 to 544. The owner's form went from 241 to 248 fields (7 strays: lines snapped on text and on the footer separator). After the fix the owner's form reads exactly as before.
- **proof5 is red as before.** It fails on 11 passed, 4 failed rows: 287 and 159 fields on the two public documents. pins.json was not edited, and this plan does not change those fields: both documents give identical fields with and without the model.
- **The first runtime-frame run failed on "upload requests".** It counted the portal's own PDF reader script files (fetched on the first PDF and answered from local copies in the frame) as outside requests. The check now names those two files. The model's own requests were always only the portal's files.

**What works, measured.**

- **The whole learned-place path runs in the real upload.** Training happens in scratch on train documents only. The model is exported and delivered as one of the portal's own scripts. It starts in the module worker during the automatic picture reading. Its heat maps are decoded into places, every place is snapped to printed evidence or dropped, and snapped places reach fields through the existing picture rules.
- **First gain on a document the model never saw.** c038 is a train document of set C: 17 picture pages, never used in training. It went from 23 to 42 fields. Its answer sheet confirms all 19 new fields: found 22 to 41, 0 new strays. They are small tick boxes, about 5 pt, that the rules did not find.

   - Training ran 15 min, not up to 30.
