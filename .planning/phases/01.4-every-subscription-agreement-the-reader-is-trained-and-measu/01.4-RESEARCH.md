## Summary

**Today's reader, scored exactly on the 39 scoreable held-out answer sheets, gets 0 of 39 documents ready to send.** It finds 1,786 of 3,711 places (48.1%). It places 517 stray fields (22.4% of its 2,303 fields). Of the places it finds, 190 have the wrong kind, 473 the wrong person and 755 of 1,616 labelled places the wrong label, and 1,516 fields (65.8%) carry a review mark. Only **371 of 3,711 places (10.0%) are right first time**: found, right kind, person and label, and unmarked. The sponsor would have 3,857 fixes to make. `[VERIFIED: SCRATCH/edgar/measure/exact.py run this session; output in SCRATCH/edgar/measure/exact-baseline.md and exact-baseline.json]`

**The no-answer-sheet measure is far too kind.** It reports 72.6% coverage, but its candidate finder sees only **62.7% of the real places** on the answer sheets (2,326 of 3,711), and 20.2% of the places it counts are not real places. It is blind to whole classes: tick boxes printed as fallback glyphs (`¨`, `o`, `q`), letter-box rows split into one field per letter, dates in several parts, and lines shared by several captions. Example: b091 scores 97.8% "coverage" on the measure while all 327 of its `¨` tick boxes lack a field. **The exact score on answer sheets is the judge. The measure is upgraded in Wave 0, and more answer sheets are written.** `[VERIFIED: computed this session against truth/*.json and cache/*-978d303e.json]`

The failures sort into about twenty layout classes (table in "Failure classes"). By documents affected:
- **Meaning errors are the widest class.** Review marks appear in 208 of 221 documents. Person errors appear in 33 of 39 answer-sheet documents. The commonest are a co-investor's place given to the investor (123 places, 21 documents), a third party's (custodian, adviser, broker) given to the investor (117, 14), and an entity-only place given to every investor (98, 11).
- **Geometry misses come next.** Lines shared by several captions miss places in 80 documents. Single lines are missed in 96. Dates in several parts are missed in 48, and signature lines in 41 (with 32 documents getting no signature field at all). Fallback tick-box glyphs go unread in at least 34 documents (1,022 boxes, none read). Hyphen blanks in plain-text exhibits are missed in 35 documents (633 places), and empty ruled cells in 26 (298).
- **Picture pages are next.** In 26 documents, picture pages or picture regions are never read or only read on request. Tinted boxes, round buttons, coloured-edge boxes and letter-box rows go unread on pictures.

**Primary recommendation.** Keep everything in the portal's page (D-18). Build in three layers:
1. **Geometry rules first.** Rules fix most geometry classes deterministically, and every picture page and picture region is read automatically, with no button.
2. **Two small models trained here from scratch, both in the page.**
   - **(a) A place model:** a fully convolutional network of about 0.3 to 1 M parameters, run with ONNX Runtime Web (MIT). It maps every rendered picture page, and every vector page where rules are unsure, to "tick / writing place / signature / letter-box / illustration" maps. Rule proposals are confirmed against these maps, and places that rules miss are found from them, always snapped to pixel or vector evidence.
   - **(b) A meaning model:** gradient-boosted trees trained with scikit-learn (BSD-3) and run in plain JavaScript. They predict kind, fine kind, person (per signature block, then per place) and which printed words are the label, from the words and layout around each place.
3. **The existing rules stay the final guard.** No field over printed words, exact fit, never invent a field.

A review mark is placed only when the model and the rules disagree, the model's calibrated probability is low, and no block-consistency rule decides. The measure counts every mark as a failure.

The models train on the 179 non-held-out documents, more EDGAR documents (D-21: about 280 more, mostly scans, picture prints and broker-dealer-style forms) and generated variants. Labels are answer sheets written to the held-out brief, bootstrapped from today's reader and the measure and then corrected. A probe this session ran a 257k-parameter network of this shape in the page runtime (WebAssembly, one thread) at **0.18 to 0.22 s per letter page at 96 dpi; the model is 1.0 MB**. `[VERIFIED: probe this session]`

**What the environment needs.** No pretrained weights are needed: both models can be trained from scratch with packages from PyPI and npm, which are reachable. huggingface.co, arxiv.org, download.pytorch.org, cdn.jsdelivr.net and GitHub repositories are blocked. Allowing download.pytorch.org (the CPU-only torch wheel and ImageNet backbones) and huggingface.co (CommonForms data and weights, after a licence check) would be accelerators, not requirements. A server or large model is used only if the held-out exact score shows the small models cannot reach the target, and then only after the owner decides (D-19).

## Today's exact score on the held-out answer sheets

`exact.py` is new this session (scratch, `SCRATCH/edgar/measure/exact.py`). It ports the owner's scorer rules line by line from `owner-set/C/score.js` and `owner-set/lib-score.js` `[VERIFIED: read this session]`:
- **Matching:** `const GROW = 3, SHARE = 0.4` (score.js:81). A field matches a place when its centre lies in the place grown by 3 pt, or when the boxes overlap by 40% of the smaller one. Pairs are matched one to one: nearest first, then augmenting paths.
- **Kind:** `KIND_MAP = { tickbox: ['checkbox'], initials: ['initials'], signature: ['signature'], date: ['date'], blank: ['text', 'name'] }` (score.js:80), plus the place's `kind_also_ok`.
- **Owner:** taken from the measure's field `owner`: `someone else` gives third-party, then `co-investor`, `firm`, and otherwise investor narrowed by `forType` `'person' | 'entity'`. The owner must equal the sheet's owner or one in `owner_also_ok`.
- **Label:** `labelOk` from lib-score.js:52-58, with the same `STANDARD` and `PLAIN` lists.
- **Marks:** a mark counts as right only on a place the sheet calls `unclear`.

**Fixes** count every place the sponsor would touch, once each (missed; or found with a kind, person or label difference; or marked), plus every stray. **Exact-ready** means fixes = 0. Required-flag differences are reported but not counted (the sheet's `must` is a judgement).

## Architecture Patterns

### System architecture (all in the page after upload)

```
Sponsor uploads PDF ──► pdf.js parse (3.11.174)
        │
        ├─► vector pages: anchors.js geometry ──► RULE PROPOSALS (lines, runs, cells, glyph boxes incl. fallback ¨/o/q,
        │                                          hyphen runs, typed |_|, caption-split segments, multi-part dates)
        │
        ├─► picture pages AND picture regions inside text pages (automatic, no button)
        │       stitch pictures cut by a page break ─► render ~96-150 dpi ─► picturepage.js pixel rules
        │       (edges, tint boxes, colour-edge boxes, rings, combs, strokes) ─► RULE PROPOSALS
        │       └─► text engine on caption strips ─► words
        │
        ├─► PLACE MODEL (FCN, ORT Web, worker) on every picture page/region and on vector pages with unsure proposals
        │       heat maps: tick | writing place | signature | letter box | illustration
        │       ├─ confirms or rejects each rule proposal (mean probability inside its box)
        │       └─ proposes places rules missed ─► SNAP to pixel/vector evidence (a rule, tint edge, box edge) or drop
        │
        ▼
CANDIDATE PLACES (each with source and geometry confidence)
        ▼
BLOCK SEGMENTATION (rules: headings, signature blocks, sections, investor-type branches)
        ▼
MEANING MODEL (trees, plain JS): per block → person; per place → kind, fine kind, label span; calibrated probabilities
        ▼
GUARD RULES (placement.js): no field over words, exact fit, one field per place, block consistency,
        never invent; disagreement + low probability ─► review mark (counted failure, rare)
        ▼
fields {kind, fine, owner/second/other, forType, label, required, parts?, comb?} ─► field editor (unchanged)
```

### Pattern 1: Rules propose, the place model confirms and completes
**What:** every candidate place has a geometric source. A rule proposal stays if the place model agrees, or if it is a certain vector shape (an underscore run or a box glyph with free space). A model-only place becomes a field only after it snaps to pixel or vector evidence.
**When:** every page; the model runs on picture pages and regions always, and on vector pages only where a proposal is unsure (to save time).

### Pattern 2: Person per block, not per place
**What:** rules segment signature and information blocks (heading, then lines up to the next heading). The meaning model predicts the block's person from the heading and the block's words: "joint", "co-", "spouse", "custodian", "trustee of", "authorized signatory", "accepted", "registered representative", "branch manager", "for entities". Each place inherits the block's person unless a place-level signal overrides it, for example "Accepted by" inside a firm block. This attacks class 2 (473 places) structurally.

### Pattern 3: Marks are a calibrated last resort (D-14 with D-07)
**What:** each field's kind and person probabilities are calibrated (isotonic calibration on dev folds of the training documents). A mark is set only when (a) calibrated probability < threshold **and** (b) the rules give no deciding signal **and** (c) the second-best answer would change who signs or whether the field exists. Thresholds are set on dev folds so that **a wrong unmarked field is rarer than a mark**, never on held-out. All picture fields stop being marked by default; they are marked only by this rule.

### Pattern 4: Picture pages read automatically within a stated time
**What:** the first reading after upload runs with pictures on. Today pictures are read only on request (`esign.js:2939-2944`: "opts: { pictures: true to read such pages from their picture (the sponsor asked for it) … `const PICTURE_LIMIT_MS = 180000;`"). The button and its dialog text go away. Mixed pages (a picture region on a text page, as on b083, b001 and b108) count as picture regions.
**Time budget (target):** a typical laptop shows progress in the existing reading dialog. Each picture page takes at most 3 s at the median, the whole document at most 60 s at the 95th percentile, and `PICTURE_LIMIT_MS` stays as the hard stop.
**Today, measured in headless Chromium on this 4-CPU machine:** picture reading takes 0.55 to 7.3 s a page (b042: 41.4 s for 19 picture pages; owner form: 38.6 s for 8). The place model adds about 0.2 s a page (probe). `[VERIFIED: summary rows this session]`

### Pattern 5: Model bytes delivered as scripts
**What:** "A hosted preview refuses every request a page makes for data, so nothing here asks the network for anything: the engine's script is loaded by a script element, its worker is started from one of the portal's own files … and its language data reaches that worker as a script it imports" (`textengine.js:4-7`). The place model and the runtime's wasm follow the same rule: a script defines the bytes (base64), and `ort.env.wasm.wasmBinary` plus `InferenceSession.create(bytes)` take them. The probe below used exactly `wasmBinary` + bytes.

### Pattern 6: Training data from answer sheets, not from the measure
**What:** the measure sees 62.7% of real places, so its candidates are not labels. Training labels are **answer sheets written to `SCRATCH/edgar/truth/BRIEF.md`** for the 179 train documents (and every added document outside the held-out slices). They are pre-filled from today's reader plus the measure's candidates and then corrected by agents who look at overlays. **Generated variants** of each born-digital train document carry the labels through the transform (no new labelling):
- re-print with background graphics on (tinted boxes), landscape with split pictures, A4 and 80/120% scale, Wingdings unavailable (fallback glyphs);
- rasterise to picture PDFs at 100 to 300 dpi with JPEG, skew, blur and speckle (simulated scans);
- recolour box edges (red, blue, grey).

### Anti-patterns to avoid
- **Fixing one document:** every change is a rule for a class, proved on all train documents (D-03).
- **Tuning on held-out:** the held-out sheets are only scored. A held-out document whose failures are inspected to design a fix is **burned**: it moves to train and is replaced from the fresh slice.
- **Model boxes as fields without snapping:** that is how strays happen.
- **Marking as a safety valve:** a design that marks when unsure fails D-14 by construction.
- **Trusting the measure's coverage:** it overstates (62.7% recall).
- **Letter-box rows as one field per letter:** the investor types one value. Use one field per row with a cell count.

## Common Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1: The measure looks good while the product is wrong
**What goes wrong:** coverage rises on the measure while answer-sheet places stay missed (b091: 97.8% versus 327 boxes missed).
**How to avoid:** exact.py on the held-out sheets every wave; upgrade measure.py in Wave 0; write answer sheets for every added document.

### Pitfall 2: Held-out leakage
**How to avoid:** manifests list train ids; the training scripts refuse any id in `held-out.json` or the fresh slice; burned documents are recorded.

### Pitfall 3: The runtime cannot start under the hosted preview's rules
**What goes wrong:** ORT Web looks up its `.wasm` and a `.mjs` glue file by URL, which a page that may not request data cannot do.
**How to avoid:** set `env.wasm.wasmBinary` from a script, and prove in Wave 0, inside the sandboxed preview frame (`01.2/checks/reader-frame.js` exists), that the session starts. If the glue `.mjs` dynamic import is refused, use the bundle build (`ort.wasm.bundle.min.mjs`, 73,054 B) `[VERIFIED: file present in npm pack]` and prove it again.

### Pitfall 4: Threads
Multithreading needs `crossOriginIsolated`; otherwise ORT uses one thread `[CITED: onnxruntime.ai/docs/tutorials/web/env-flags-and-session-options.html]`. Set `numThreads = 1` explicitly. The timing target assumes one thread (probe: 0.18 to 0.22 s a page).

### Pitfall 5: Non-deterministic fields
`stable:A|B|C` requires the same fields twice. **How to avoid:** one thread, fixed rendering size, thresholds away from ties, and a `stable` run on several EDGAR documents too.

### Pitfall 6: Answer-sheet errors
**What goes wrong:** a wrong sheet trains or judges wrongly.
**How to avoid:** where the reader and a sheet disagree after a build, a second agent re-checks the sheet against the overlay. Any sheet change is logged and hashed (as `owner-set/hashes.json` does), and **never** made to fit the reader.

### Pitfall 7: Multi-part dates and letter-box rows need the field model
**What goes wrong:** one field with parts, or one comb field, is what the sheets expect and what the investor needs, but the editor and the finished PDF must render it.
**How to avoid:** add `parts` (sub-boxes) and `comb` (cell count) to the field model, render them in the signing step and the finished PDF, and walk them end to end.

### Pitfall 8: Model size and first-page delay
**How to avoid:** load the runtime and model only when a page needs them (picture pages, unsure vector pages), in a worker, while pdf.js parses. About 3.7 MB of runtime (gzipped) plus a 1 to 4 MB model.

### Pitfall 9: The owner's pins break silently
**How to avoid:** the owner's measure (`owner-set/measure.js`) runs at every wave (see Validation Architecture). Every changed pin needs a listed reason shown to the owner.

### What PASS means
- **Task:** the quick run shows no class worse than before on the train documents of that class, **and** exact.py on d1 is no worse, **and** the owner scorer of any touched area is still at its recorded level, **and** the unit checks pass.
- **Wave:** the full suite meets three bars:
  - exact.py on held-out A is no worse on any column than the best recorded (ratchet), and better in the classes the wave targets;
  - the measure on the whole set has no class worse;
  - every owner measure prints PASS, with B held at PLACES: PASS and TOLD unchanged until the owner answers.
- **Phase:** the done targets above, held-out B scored once at the end (and only numbers and class names inspected), then the owner's check.

`NOT RUN` is never green. Any answer sheet or scorer change is detected by hash and reported.
