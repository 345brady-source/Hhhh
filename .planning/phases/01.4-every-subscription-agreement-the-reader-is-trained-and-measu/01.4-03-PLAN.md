  <action>
prefill_sheet.py writes `SCRATCH/edgar/truth/<id>/truth.json` as a draft (`draft: true`, `source: hand`): mode train joins the reader's latest fields (kind, owner from owner and forType, label, printed caption) with the measure's counted candidates (a candidate with no field becomes a place with `doubt: "candidate only"`; disagreements carry `doubt: "check"`); mode B uses candidates only, each with `doubt: "check"`, and refuses unless the id is in held-out-b.json. A held-out A id is refused in every mode. Boxes snap to vector geometry (pdfplumber underscore runs with 14 pt writing room, drawn rules, rects, glyph boxes) or, on picture pages, to pixel edges through the page's drawing matrix, as BRIEF.md says.
sheet_edit.py: `list --page N`, `del <placeId>`, `add <page> <x0> <y0> <x1> <y1> <kind> <owner> [--what] [--label] [--printed] [--must]`, `set <placeId> key=value`, `snap <placeId>` (nearest printed evidence within 6 pt), `done` (removes draft, recomputes counts, runs check_sheet.py, draws overlays, records sha256; a later change to a done sheet needs `--why`, appended to sheet-reasons.txt). overlay.py draws `truth-overlay-pNN.png` at 100 dpi (boxes coloured by kind, owner initials, place id) and with `--contact` one contact sheet of all pages. check_sheet.py validates keys, kind and owner vocabularies, `what` for blanks, boxes inside the page, no two places overlapping by more than half the smaller, consistent counts, and no email-shaped string; prints `SHEET <id>: PASS|FAIL - <reasons>`. Every tool that reads a held-out B sheet restores its read seal (access time = modification time minus 1 s).
PROCEDURE.md: the steps per document (pre-fill, contact sheet, every page with places at full size, correct, executed copies flagged, no personal data, done), and what to do when unsure (`owner: unclear` with a doubt, never a guess).
manifest.json: `train` = every set-a/set-b id not in held-out.json (sorted); `folds` 1 to 5 by document, stratified by pdf_made and offering_kind, deterministic (sorted by sha256 of the id); `tranche` = 36 train ids chosen deterministically to cover every pdf_made and offering_kind value and at least one document of each failure class 4 to 23 that has a train document (classes.json), spread over the five folds; `batches` TR01 to TR06 of 6 tranche ids each.
