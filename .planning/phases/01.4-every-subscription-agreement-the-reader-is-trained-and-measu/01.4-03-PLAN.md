  <action>
prefill_sheet.py writes `SCRATCH/edgar/truth/<id>/truth.json` as a draft (key `draft: true`): with `--from reader,candidates` every field of the reader's latest result.json (out-set-a, out-set-b, later runs given by `--runs`) becomes a place with its kind, owner (from the field's owner and forType), label and printed label from the caption, and every counted candidate with no field becomes a place with `doubt: "candidate only"`; places where the reader and the candidates disagree carry `doubt: "check"`. With `--from candidates` (held-out B) only candidates are used and every place carries `doubt: "check"`. Boxes are snapped to vector geometry (pdfplumber: underscore run with 14 pt writing room, drawn rule, rect, glyph box) or, on picture pages, to pixel edges at the picture's own resolution through the page's drawing matrix, as BRIEF.md says.
sheet_edit.py edits a sheet by id: `list --page N`, `del <placeId>`, `add <page> <x0> <y0> <x1> <y1> <kind> <owner> [--what W] [--label L] [--printed P] [--must true|false]`, `set <placeId> key=value`, `snap <placeId>` (to the nearest printed evidence within 6 pt), `done` (removes `draft`, recomputes counts per page, owner and kind, runs check_sheet.py, draws overlays, records sha256 in sheet-hashes.json; a later change to a done sheet requires `--why <text>` which is appended to sheet-reasons.txt).
overlay.py draws `truth-overlay-pNN.png` at 100 dpi for every page with places (boxes coloured by kind, owner initials and place id beside each) and with `--contact` one contact sheet of all pages (so pages with no place are seen too).
check_sheet.py validates a sheet against BRIEF.md: keys, kind and owner vocabularies, `what` for blanks, boxes inside the page, no two places overlapping by more than half the smaller, counts consistent, and no string anywhere matching an email address pattern; prints `SHEET <id>: PASS` or `SHEET <id>: FAIL - <reasons>`, and `--batch <name>` checks a whole batch.
PROCEDURE.md: the steps an agent follows per document (pre-fill; contact sheet; every page with places at full size; correct with sheet_edit.py; executed copies flagged `doubt: "executed copy"`; never copy personal data; `done`), the time each step took on the two proof documents, and what to do when unsure (`owner: unclear` with a doubt, never a guess).
manifest.json: `train` = every id of set-a and set-b not in held-out.json (sorted); `folds` 1 to 5 by document, stratified by meta.json how-made and offering kind, deterministic (sorted by sha256 of the id); `batches` T01 to T26 of 7 ids each (the last shorter), interleaved across folds and how-made so each batch mixes kinds.
