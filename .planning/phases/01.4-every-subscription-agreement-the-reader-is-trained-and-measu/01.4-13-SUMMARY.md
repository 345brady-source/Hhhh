What the corrections were, by class (no document text):
- **b110 (pictures):** no reader field. A third of the places were added by hand: the investor and co-investor rows, city/state/zip rows, phone split into day and evening, e-mail, title line 1, the SSN row, two tiny S-Corp/C-Corp boxes, custodian rows, alternate-payee rows, percentage lines, direct-deposit rows, broker-dealer rows and each Date cell beside a signature. Every "tickbox" pre-fill on pages 3-5 is an Initials box (Owner / Co-Owner captions). Letters in an all-caps paragraph read as boxes and were deleted. Box geometry was measured from the page's own pixels.
- **b029:** Wingdings box glyphs read as blanks, so they were set to tick boxes. Labels were shifted by one line on the entity-type grid. Individual and entity sections were split. Footnote rules and bracketed issuer text were deleted. One qualified-client letter blank with no printed line was added.
- **a046:** 79 deletions. The certificate-of-designation and warrant exhibits are already signed (/s/), and their conversion, exercise and assignment forms are used only on a later event. The filled list-of-subscribers schedule was deleted with every name in it. The legal-opinion schedule belongs to counsel. Three cells on the subscriber signature page have no printed line (purchase price, preferred shares, warrants) and were added with a doubt.
- **a049:** the year-figure blanks were added. A US questionnaire had two missed lines for entity-owner details. The warrant and notice-of-exercise pages were deleted under the same class rule as a046. Witness places were set to third party.
- **a088:** dashed heading rules were deleted. The acceptance date runs were re-measured. The "circle one" ID-type words have no box and were recorded in not_fields.
- **a109:** the "Your initials" column cells of the individual risk form were added, as were the salesperson section rows, the face-page cells (residential address, phone, e-mail, disclosed principal) and the account-reference cell. The checklist boxes on page 1 are initials ("Please initial each box").
