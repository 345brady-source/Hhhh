A model that fails to start or run leaves the page exactly as the rules read it, logs one neutral console line and sets nothing visible. Count model places into the page result so esign.js records `read.modelPlaces`.
placemodel-unit.js (Node, no browser): decodes made-up heat maps (two blobs, a blob touching another, an empty map) into the expected boxes, snaps a box to a drawn square on a made-up page picture and drops one with no edge near; prints one line per case and `PLACEMODEL UNIT: PASS` or `FAIL`.
