# Phase 01.4 Plan 72: Picture pages inside text agreements, unnamed box glyphs, and the owner's tax, grid and caption rules Summary

Two defects from the second blind subscription test are fixed and proved with fake readers on public agreements, and the owner's four new rules are in. **Nothing was spent and nothing was measured on a blind or owner document.** SCRATCH/blind/ was never opened, listed or used. None of this is ready to call working until a real reading is measured and the owner has checked it.

## Failures first

No gate is red. Things the orchestrator must know first:

1. **I could not confirm the blind page's own cause.** I was not allowed to open the blind document. On the base commit, a public agreement with one page replaced by a **bare** picture already worked: the page was read from its picture, numbered, sent and its picks became fields. What failed on the base commit, with no field at all on the page (the blind symptom), were two other kinds of scanned page:
   - a picture with a stamp drawn over it in the margins (a copy line, "Page i of N"), as an agreement put together from scans carries;
   - a picture stored as several band images.

   Both are fixed. The orchestrator can tell which kind the blind tax page is without reading its content: `pdfimages -list -f 32 -l 32` shows whether it has one image or several, and `pdftotext -f 32 -l 32` shows whether any text is drawn over it. If it is neither kind, the cause is still open.
2. **The real tax page will now be skipped (owner decision), not filled.** That works only if the page is recognised. On a picture page it is recognised from the words read off the picture, so it depends on how well those are read. If they are not recognised, only the reader's SYSTEM rule keeps fields off the page.
3. **The in-page fallback still places fields on tax form pages.** D-33 says not to work on the fallback, so the tax rule applies on the reading-server path only.
4. **The box rule is narrower than the brief's wording.** The brief said any symbol-font or private-use glyph is a box. What I built:
   - A private-use or control-code glyph that is about square and stands before an option's words counts as a box on its own.
   - A symbol-font or geometric-shape glyph counts only when it starts a list of choices or the words around it ask for one.

   Without that limit, the bullets, arrows and ticks in symbol fonts would become stray boxes. I also did not cover a box glyph that the reader reports as an ordinary letter other than o, p, q, r, c or i (for example "n") in a font with no recognisable name.
5. **More pages are now read from their pictures.** I compared all 180 train documents with the base commit:
   - 33 pages in 3 documents changed from text pages to picture pages: 30 pages of a059, page 39 of a046, and pages 33 and 34 of b046. Every one is a browser-printed scan whose only visible text is a header or footer stamp. Before, they got nothing.
   - On such a document, reading the words off the pictures takes longer. PICTURE_LIMIT_MS (180 s) still caps it.
6. TDD order: the code came before the check. RED was then shown by running the new check against the base commit (below).

## What the check proves (mixed-boxes.js, fake readers, public train documents)

- **mixed.** b005 with page 6 made a picture three ways: bare, stamped, and in 4 bands. Each was uploaded through the portal. In all three:
  - page 6 was read from its picture;
  - its 8 numbered places were sent as one numbered picture carrying exactly those numbers;
  - the 8 picks became 8 fields, each on its own place.
- **symbol, rewritten boxes.** I rewrote b005's 116 box characters as the reader would give them, in two ways: private-use code U+F0A8 in a font named "F7", and control code U+0000. For both:
  - 116 boxes are found, at the same places as with the original characters;
  - each of the 116 glyphs carries exactly one numbered place on its own extent;
  - fake picks give 116 fields, each on its glyph (largest centre offset 0.7 pt).
- **symbol, a014.** This public agreement prints its tick boxes as unnamed control codes (risk levels, Yes/No). Its 6 boxes, which got no place on the base commit, are now found, numbered, and the picks land on them.
- **tax.** b005 with two made-up tax pages added (a W-9 style form and an instruction page):
  - The two pages are recognised.
  - They get no numbered place, no picture and no field. A missing place named on them is skipped.
  - The same two pages with a non-tax title keep their 10 places.
  - a031, whose own pages 25, 29 and 30 mention Form W-9 and W-8, has no tax page and keeps its places.
  - Across all 180 train documents, 0 pages are recognised as tax forms.
- **grid.** A made-up choice grid (3 methods by 3 purposes). Each of the 9 cells was reported in missing as "column / row". Each landed inside its own cell, both when the cells are numbered and when there is no numbered place at all.
- **undercap.** a015 page 8 prints its captions under the lines. A missing place named by such a caption lands on the line directly above it, whether the answer says below, on or above. On the base commit, "below" put it elsewhere. When that line is already a field, nothing is added.
- **beside (spaceBeside).** A missing tick box named by a b005 choice's words lands on its printed box, whether the answer says left, right or below. On the base commit, "right" missed the box. When the box is already a field, two such entries add nothing.
