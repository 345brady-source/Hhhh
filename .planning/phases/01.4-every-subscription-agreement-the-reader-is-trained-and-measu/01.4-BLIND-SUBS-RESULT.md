# Phase 01.4: the API reader on four blind subscription agreements, before and after D-46

Each agreement was read once as text through the portal's real upload. The first agreement was also read once as pictures. Each reading is scored against its own answer sheet, which was frozen by hash before the reading. The answer sheets were written by agents who built none of the reader code. This report gives two sets of numbers:

- **Original:** every place on the sheet.
- **D-46:** the owner's new convention. Places and fields on attached tax-form pages (W-9, the W-8 series and their instruction pages) are left out of scoring entirely: they count as neither misses nor strays. The frozen sheets are unchanged; the exclusion is applied when scoring.

This report gives counts, page numbers and kinds only, and quotes nothing from any document. Nothing here makes the reader ready or working. The owner checks it first.

Documents: **A** = the first blind agreement (plan 65), **B** = the second (41 pages), **C** = the third (32 pages), **D** = the fourth (27 pages). They are named here by letter only.

## Method

- **Sheets.** Each sheet was written from its PDF's own geometry with the sheet tools in path mode (pre-fill from printed candidates only), following BRIEF.md's conventions and D-30. Each was frozen by hash in an empty commit before its reading: A under plan 65, B 757da15, C 5008ab1, D 742a756. The frozen sheets are not edited for D-46.
- **D-46 scoring.** A small wrapper drops the sheet places, and the reader fields, on the excluded pages. It then scores the rest with exact.py's single-document scoring unchanged: person on two classes (D-30); D-28 score = right / (places + strays), passing at 90 percent. Re-scoring A's original readings reproduces plan 65's published numbers exactly.
- **Readings.** Each was made once by the orchestrator with the key, from the main checkout, through run.js with the server reader: model claude-fable-5-2, max_tokens 48000, chosen by the zero-spend count. Caps were $3.00 for A, and $4.25 for B, C and D, the orchestrator having raised B's cap before its reading. Each run.json shows health reader true, outcome read (counted as ok) and used true. Costs were recomputed from each read log at $8 / $40 per million.
