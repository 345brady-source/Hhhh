# Phase 01.4 Plan 71: One PPM upload with the subscription agreement inside does both (D-44) Summary (up to the measurement checkpoint)

When the reading server says an uploaded PDF contains the subscription agreement, the review screen now shows the pages it found. The sponsor can change those pages or untick the box. On apply, the offering's terms are saved first. Then, with no question, those pages are cut into their own PDF, stored with the offering's documents, read by the subscription reader and saved as the offering's agreement, all in the background.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 1] Page inputs do not re-render the screen on each keystroke.** The plan said the binder re-renders. A re-render on every keystroke drops focus and the next keystroke, and can swallow the click on Apply. Instead:
   - the value is stored, kept within 1 to the file's pages;
   - when the field is left, the first-after-last fix runs and the other input's value is updated in place;
   - ik-apply applies the same fix before it applies.
2. **[Rule 1] The review hint says "kept" for a target offering with an agreement draft as well as a template.** prepareAgreement keeps both.
3. **[Rule 2] ikDone (not pinned) leaves out "Next: add the subscription agreement" while the found agreement is being read.** Otherwise it would contradict the reading state on the panel.
4. **[Rule 3] combined-cmd.sh has a `REPO` override** (default /home/user/Hhhh), so the dry run can be pointed at a worktree.
5. **Typographic apostrophe** in "this offering’s subscription agreement" and in the hint, matching the rest of the intake screens.
6. **Environment:** server/node_modules was symlinked from the main checkout for the test runs and never staged. Remove the link before merging if the worktree is kept. The `.git` ledger was not used. The plan base is recorded above and in out-71/plan-head-before.txt.
