## Method

- **Answer sheet.** An agent who built none of plans 64, 66, 68 and 69 wrote it from the PDF's own geometry, before any reader output existed. It used the sheet tools in path mode, with the pre-fill drawing on printed candidates only. Box-character squares were measured from the page pixels at 300 dpi, the contact matrix from its drawn cell edges, and the tax form from its pixels. It follows BRIEF.md's conventions and D-30: the firm owns the general partner's two cover blanks and the acceptance block, and everyone else is the investor. Every page was drawn as an overlay and looked at, and check_sheet.py passed. The sheet has 225 places (123 tick boxes, 96 blanks, 5 signatures, 1 date) and is frozen at sha256 639a26ef2f4f269aae23ef2914808c3342016b0c88bceb39de06739677180af2 (commit 757da15).
- **Reading.** The orchestrator ran it from the main checkout at commit 3492441 with no uncommitted reader files, as recorded by the script, through the portal's real upload (run.js). The server's zero-spend probe counted 163957 tokens in 1 request (no page ranges). The max-tokens rule picked 48000, with a worst case of $4.04 at the dearest prices ($10 / $50 per million) against the $4.25 cap. Model claude-fable-5-2, priced at $8 / $40 per million in server/lib/model.mjs. The reading's run.json shows health reader true, outcome read (counted as ok), used true, and 1 read-log line.
- **Scoring.** exact.py's single-document scoring (`--truth --run`), with person on two classes (D-30). D-28 score = right / (places + strays), passing at 90 percent or more. The cost was recomputed from the read log: 165063 input tokens x $8 / 1e6 + 16852 output tokens x $40 / 1e6 = $1.9946.
