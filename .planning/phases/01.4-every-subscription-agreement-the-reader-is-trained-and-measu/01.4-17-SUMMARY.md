### What each document needed
- **c025**: the pre-fill found 95 candidates, mostly logo marks, heading letters and footer rules. Every table cell (investor, joint owner, trust, entity, bank, brokerage) was added from the picture's own pixel rules. Six ownership boxes the measure missed were measured from their sides. The joint owner's name row and citizenship row are cut by page breaks and recorded on both pages ("split by page break"). The page clip was measured at y 576 / 35.8.
- **c038**: the grey tick boxes are lighter than the measure's ink threshold. Six missed boxes were found at a lighter threshold. The signature, ownership choices, counterpart page and SAFE signature page have no printed lines or boxes: places carry doubts naming the missing line or box.
- **c096**: the address and advisor cells were rebuilt from the vector rules (the pre-fill had mis-split them). The tax, trustee and signature-table cells, the associated-person names and the benefit-plan list box were added.
- **c172**: the dashed row rules wrap onto the next line. Every wrapped rule piece was deleted, and every wrapped blank or box was kept as two pieces ("split by line wrap"). Cells under captions have no lines, which is why it carries 92 doubts.
- **c243**: mostly kind fixes (round "o" glyph boxes were pre-filled as blanks). Issuer offering blanks were deleted. Three purchase-price boxes inside a picture and the note date were added.

## Conventions applied (BRIEF, both sections, including the update received mid-run)
- Issuer blanks in recitals and offering terms are listed under not_fields, not places.
- Wrapped lines and page breaks: one place per piece, with the doubt.
- No-box choices are tick-box places with a doubt. No-line places carry a doubt naming the missing line.
- Custodian and broker-dealer blocks are third-party.
- Exhibits signed at the same closing (c038 SAFE and counterpart page, c243 promissory note and operating agreement joinder) carry places.
- No box was printed already checked in this batch. No row is captioned only "Broker/Dealer" or "Account Executive". No separate tax certificate is in this batch.
