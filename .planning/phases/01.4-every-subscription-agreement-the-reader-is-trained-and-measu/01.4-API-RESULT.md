## Method

**The answer sheet (D-34).**
- Written by the plan 65 executor, who built none of plans 64, 66, 68 or 69. It was written before any reader output or picture version of the document existed.
- Tools: the existing sheet tools, given a path option. The pre-fill used measure.py's printed candidates only, never a reader's fields. Every page with places was then decided by eye at 100 dpi and in crops.
- Boxes come from the PDF's own geometry: drawn rules, rectangles, form cells and digit boxes.
- It follows BRIEF.md's rules and settled conventions. People follow D-30: sponsor = the two company acceptance blocks, plus the share and bonus-share counts the cover page leaves to the issuer. Everything else is the investor, including custodian, registered representative, broker-dealer and purchaser-representative places.
- 173 places on 12 pages: 31 tick boxes, 118 blanks, 14 signatures, 10 dates. 11 are the sponsor's and 162 the investor's.
- Frozen by sha256 before any reading: b2c21acdf926bddab12cf97012b3921512e75364102804aa8538fba82ede256c (commit 4311bbf). It still matches.

**The picture version (D-42).**
- Made after the freeze with plan 69's checks/picture-pdf.js: 25 pages, every page a picture, the same page sizes, no text layer.
- It is scored against the same sheet.

**The readings.**
- Each ran once, alone on its own server (server/index.mjs from the portal checkout), through the portal's real upload as a sponsor (run.js with the reading server saved in the workspace).
- Each kind had its own $3.00 cap (CARROWVALE_SPEND_CAP_USD, plan 66's createBudget) with its own read log.

**The zero-spend probe before each reading.**
- The server was started with a cap of 0.000001, so it counted the real request and refused it with spend_cap before any generation.
- The request the browser sent was counted with the server's own count function (server/lib/read.mjs createReader().count). The spend_cap log line of /v1/read carries no count.
- Text: counted 72,745 tokens, 1 range (not split), scaled tokens 72,745. The rule chose max_tokens 32000; worst case at the dearest prices $2.33 of $3.00.
- Pictures: counted 144,761 tokens, 1 range (not split), scaled tokens 144,761. The rule chose max_tokens 24000; worst case $2.65 of $3.00.

**Server settings.**
- Model claude-fable-5-2, thinking adaptive, effort high (the default), server-side fallback "default".
- Server call limit 300 s; the browser waits 330 s.
- Prices come from server/lib/model.mjs: $8 / $40 per million for claude-fable-5-2. The cap's worst case uses the dearest rate, $10 / $50 (D-39).

**Scoring.**
- exact.py score_doc, unchanged, through a new one-document command (--truth, --run). Person is judged on two classes (D-30).
- right = places with a field of the right kind and the right person. D-28 score = right / (places + strays), with a bar of 90 percent.
- A reading counted as a measurement only because its run.json shows healthReader true, used true and the server's success outcome, and its read log holds that one line. The server writes "read" for a successful reading, and the orchestrator decided that this is the plan's "ok".
