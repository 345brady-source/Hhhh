What the pre-fill got wrong, by kind of document:
- **b082** (landscape print of a REIT appendix, forms in table cells): every reader field on the investor tables was one row off, because the caption sits at the top of each band. The initials table had become 59 text candidates; it was replaced by 24 drawn initials boxes in three columns. The signature table was offset; it was replaced by the 3 drawn signature boxes and 3 date boxes. Underlined words, the page header bands and the footnote rules were deleted.
- **b051** (plain-text negotiated agreement): the deal-term blanks were deleted as places the issuer's lawyer completes before execution. The two signature lines were kept, and the company signer's empty name and title captions were added.
- **a099**: the issuer's escrow details, the bracketed notice line and the effective date were deleted. Phones and tax numbers were merged into one place each, and subscriber 2 was made the co-investor.
- **b022** (1998 plain text): the drawn-with-characters amount and account boxes were fixed. The circled account types were added. The second-column signature, title and date lines were made the joint subscriber's.
