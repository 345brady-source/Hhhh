What the pre-fill got wrong, by class:
- **Box glyphs and letter-o boxes missed or mis-kinded.** b104 had 33 Wingdings tick boxes with no place. In b090, a007 and a098, every lone letter "o" before an option is a tick box; the measure read them as 10 pt "text" candidates, and the reader made no field for them.
- **Joint and second-owner columns read as the investor**, and custodian, broker-dealer and adviser rows mixed between investor and third party (b104 owner changed 64, a051 25, b063 37).
- **Decorative rules read as blanks.** These were full-width table rules and the double rule under a title (a007 p3-4, b090 p6).
- **Caption-only rows had no place at all** (a007 p3: 39 places added from the room after each caption).
- **Text print (b063).** The pre-fill cut each digit comb into one place per cell; these were merged into one place per number on each line (72 deleted). Lines cut by the print's line wrap kept both parts with a doubt. The issuer's minimum-amount blank was deleted.
- **Signature rows.** b090 and b063 put signature and date on one rule, so they were split at the Date caption. In b104 the print-name and signature boxes were swapped in kind.

Doubts left on purpose (all in the sheets' truth.md):
- b090: owner of the four header tracking fields (account, date, IC name/code, track) is not stated.
- a007: the three eligibility options whose boxes did not print.
- b063: 26 places split by line wrap; the wrapped other-title blank; the zip and the section 9 occupation rows, which have no printed line.
- a098: the two signature-row dates and the two acceptance dates, whose day and month fall on two lines (split by line wrap), and the five identification types chosen by circling words.
