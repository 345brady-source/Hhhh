<objective>
D-38: every upload is read and placed with the same bar, text or picture. The flow never stutters or asks, and long documents are read whole. This plan adds three things on top of plan 64's subscription API reader and plan 68's intake: picture-only and broken-text pages go to the model as page images with the picture reader's pixel candidates; long agreements are split into page ranges and merged; transient failures are retried silently. It also removes the last "allow" wording from the reading flow, guarantees a firm signature place after every reading so saving never stalls, and brings the owner gate's walk rows back to green (walkA's expectations to two people under D-30). Nothing is spent here, and SCRATCH/blind/ is never opened.

Purpose: the owner's bar is a product that reads any agreement it is given, every time, with no questions.
Output: the picture path, ranges and retries in the readers; the picture-pdf tool for measurement; checks in fake mode; the README.
</objective>
