## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The gate compares rates for kind, person, label and required, not raw counts**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** "every column worse fails" on raw counts would fail any plan that finds more places, because a newly found place can only add kind, person or label differences (missed falls, ownerDiff rises, fixes unchanged).
- **Fix:** counts where finding more cannot hurt (scored and places must be equal; found, exact-ready, right first time not lower; missed, strays, fixes not higher), and rates of the found places for kind, person and label right and required differences, marks as a share of fields. Recorded in scorer-reasons.txt.

**2. [Rule 2 - Critical] Held-out results of the research runs moved into sealed heldout/ folders**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** out-set-a, out-set-b, the calibration run and the research picture run held 43 held-out result folders in the open with 97 editor screenshots and compare pictures, held-out rows in summary.json/summary.md, and per-document lines in the logs; exact-baseline.md/.json carried per-document held-out numbers. Builders could read all of it (D-22).
- **Fix:** each moved to `<run>/heldout/<id>/` with every picture removed; held-out rows, summary lines and log lines moved into `<run>/heldout/`; summary.json totals recomputed over train rows with a `heldout` entry of counts only; exact-baseline.* moved to `measure/heldout/`; the original summaries kept in `measure/heldout/pre-migration/`. All sealed. exact.py finds results in `<run>/<id>`, `<run>/heldout/<id>` and `<run>/../heldout/<id>`, so the numbers are unchanged.

**3. [Rule 1 - Bug] A short held-out id in code files**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (guard flagged a layer name in another plan's check file)
- **Fix:** in .js/.mjs/.py files an id shorter than four characters counts only as a path segment (`/id/`, `/id.pdf`); in notes and data files it is still a whole token.

**4. [Acceptance wording] A totals differ from RESEARCH by the page-break rule the plan itself adds**
- The criterion "A totals equal RESEARCH's baseline" and the action "a split pair counts as one place" cannot both hold. Without the rule the totals equal the baseline exactly; with it, 14 pairs make places 3,697 and fixes 3,843. The gate's record uses the rule; both numbers are in scorer-reasons.txt and exact-best.json's note.

**5. [Scope] measure.py was not changed**
- The plan's artifact line says run.js and measure.py restore the read seal. measure.py belongs to plan 01, and plan 01 was editing and running it while this plan ran. run.js restores the seal after measure.py reads a held-out PDF or cache file inside a run; measure.py run directly does not (see the open items).
