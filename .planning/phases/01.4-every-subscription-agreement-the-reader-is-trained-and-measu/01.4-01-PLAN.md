  <action>
Extend `measure.py candidates` with these candidate rules, each designed and checked by eye on train documents only (never an id of held-out.json; per D-03):
- `glyphbox`: a word that is a lone U+00A8 or a lone `q` (the fallback of a symbol-font box) is a tick box, conf high, fill true; a lone `o`, `c`, `i` or apostrophe is a tick box (conf medium, fill true) only in a choice position: two or more such glyphs whose left edges align within 4 pt, each followed on its own line by option words. The box is the glyph's box made square on its height. Example document: b091 (all of its printed fallback boxes).
- `typedcomb`: a run of three or more typed `|_|` or `|__|` cells on one line is ONE place of kind comb carrying `cells` (the count); drawn comb rows stay one place each.
- `dateparts`: underscore runs on one line interleaved with date words (day, of, a month name, 19 or 20 printed before a run) form ONE date place carrying `parts` (the boxes of each run); the separate runs are no longer counted alone.
- `captionsplit`: a rule or underscore run with two or more captions directly under it or on it (each caption a separate group of words with a gap of 12 pt or more) is split at the midpoints between the captions into one place per caption; the old rule that refused a line with no single 30 percent free stretch no longer drops `City ____ State ____ Zip ____`: each free stretch of 18 pt or more after a caption is a place.
- `illustration`: a picture region whose words (text layer or none) include a sample or example marker, or whose boxes are filled with print in more than half of them, is flagged illustration and none of its boxes or strokes is counted (d1 pages 8 and 9 are the known case; design on a train document of the same kind found through classes.json class 22, and never by opening d1, which is held out). A grid of ruled cells where every cell of a row holds print is a table frame and is not counted.
Bump the finder version string so `run.js` re-computes every cached candidate file. Rewrite the README's "The candidate places" and "Known weaknesses" for the new rules (no document text; ids only).
