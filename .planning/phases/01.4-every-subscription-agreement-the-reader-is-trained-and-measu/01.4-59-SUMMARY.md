## What the re-pin holds, stated plainly

The pin freezes the picture state as it stands. It does not make that state clean:
- Binder: the oracle finds 161 of its 288 fields not on a printed place, over ink, in brackets or overlapping. All 288 are marked for review.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] measure.js hash re-record.** measure.js hashes pins.json's pinned values and refuses a change without a matching reasons.txt line in the form `date | file key | first 12 of the hash | why`. The D-28 line was therefore written with the hashed key `pins.json (pinned values, without the explained list)` and the new hash 069aeb5a06ce, then `measure.js --rehash` was run once. Scratch only. The prior hashes.json is kept at `SCRATCH/p59/hashes.before-59.json`.

**2. [Rule 2] Notice on the "Most of this PDF could not be read" return too,** when that path has a pictured page. Without this, a partly pictured document could skip the notice. Text documents are unaffected (proven by the check's text part and the five text documents in proof5).
