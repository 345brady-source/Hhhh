<objective>
Teach the portal the other kinds of field (initials, blanks to fill in, tick boxes) and give the sponsor a plain way to
see what the portal was unsure about and settle it before the agreement can be used.

Purpose: the owner asked that the portal tell apart what the client signs, fills in and initials, that blanks become
fillable on their own, and that an unsure placement is flagged and must be confirmed or moved. Editor polish and smooth
dragging belong to Phase 2: this plan adds only what is needed to show a mark and clear it.

Output: `placement.js` covering all field kinds; review marks, confirm action and gates in `esign.js`; a small set of
styles; a browser check.
</objective>
