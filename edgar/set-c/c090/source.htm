(1)          Overview. The Partnership is offering to sell 1,428,572 Class A Common Units (each, a “Class A Common Unit”) of the Partnership in an aggregate amount of $20,000,000 (the “Offering”). This   Offering will commence immediately upon the Offering becoming effective, and will be a “best-efforts” offering by the Partnership without underwriters. However, the Partnership may, in its discretion, enter into agreements with one or more online platform providers, placement agents or finders to assist in sales of our Class A Common Units. In consideration for such services, the Partnership may pay to such persons customary fees, which the Partnership expects to be no greater than 8% of the Offering Price for any Class A Common Units sold. The Partnership is offering an aggregate of 1,428,572 Class A Common Units (the “Units”) at $14.00 per Unit, for gross proceeds of approximately $20,000,000. Each Investor purchasing Units will also receive a warrant to purchase additional Class A Common Units in an amount equal to 20% of the Units purchased by the Investor, at an exercise price of $12.00 per Class A Common Unit (the “Warrants,” and together with the Units, the “Securities”). Until such time as all of the Securities are sold by the Partnership, all proceeds of sales of the Securities shall be held by The Nottingham Company, as escrow agent (the “Escrow Agent”). If the Partnership does not sell all of the Securities prior to January 25, 2017, or the Partnership’s General Partner, AWA Management, LLC determines to terminate the Offering prior to such date, (i) the Offering will terminate, (ii) all proceeds will be reimbursed in full, without interest, to all Investors, and (iii) the Partnership will be under no obligation to issue any Warrant to any Investor. If the Partnership sells all of the Securities within the Offering period, the Partnership shall issue the Warrants in the form included in the Offering Materials and the proceeds will be released by the Escrow Agent to the Partnership. The Warrant will become exercisable upon the two-year anniversary of the date of issuance, and expire on the five-year anniversary of the issuance date.
