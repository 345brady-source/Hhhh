1.1 Description of Convertible Notes.

 

Principal Amount; Interest. The Company has authorized the issuance and sale of Senior Secured Convertible Notes in the aggregate original principal amount of up to One Million Seven Hundred and Seventy-Five Thousand Dollars ($1,775,000 USD) in substantially the form attached hereto, and with the terms set forth in, Exhibit A (the “Convertible Notes” and each a “Convertible Note”), subject to the receipt by the Company or Persons as directed by the Company of the Consideration no later than the Closing Date. Each Convertible Note will have a principal balance equal to the Consideration paid by such Purchaser for such Units, as set forth opposite such Purchaser’s name on Schedule A, and shall accrue interest on its unpaid principal balance at an aggregate rate of ten percent (10%) per annum (with a minimum of one year of interest) from the Closing Date, which interest shall accrue from the Closing Date and be paid to such Purchaser upon the earlier of maturity or conversion of the applicable Convertible Note (the “Interest”). The Convertible Notes shall be secured pursuant to the terms of a security agreement in substantially the form attached hereto as Exhibit B (the “Security Agreement”).
