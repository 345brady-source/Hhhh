Scorer reasons: every change to exact.py's scoring, with exact.py's sha256 after it. Append only.

2026-11-15  plan 01.4-02  exact.py sha256 09bde1909dd78bbb5e57fe05ffbae1804767192e5a9c65652122a17b36064f73
  - Matching, kind, owner, label and mark rules unchanged (GROW 3 pt, SHARE 0.4, KIND_MAP, label_ok).
  - Fixed order: fields and places of a page are matched in id order (then original position for a repeated id), so
    equal distances always resolve the same way, whatever order the result file lists them in. On today's runs this
    changes nothing: without the page-break rule below, held-out A totals equal the research baseline exactly
    (3711 places, 1786 found, 517 strays, 3857 fixes, 371 right first time, 0 of 39 exact-ready).
  - Sheet convention (BRIEF.md): a place cut by a page break is recorded on both PDF pages with doubt
    "split by page break". The two pieces count as ONE place, met by a field on either piece. A field on each piece:
    the second is a stray (the sponsor would delete it). Pairing is structural: both pieces carry the doubt, are on
    consecutive pages, have the same kind, overlap across by at least half of the narrower piece, and the first sits
    lower on its page than the second on the next; widest overlap first, then by id; each piece pairs once.
    Effect on today's held-out A: 14 pairs; places 3711 -> 3697, missed 1925 -> 1911, fixes 3857 -> 3843; found,
    strays, kind, owner, label, marks and right first time unchanged. `--no-split` scores without the rule.
  - New column "right first time": a place found with the right kind, person and label, and unmarked.
  - The gate compares counts where finding more places cannot make them worse (scored and places must be equal;
    found, exact-ready and right first time may not fall; missed, strays and fixes may not rise) and rates of the
    found places for kind, person and label right and required differences, and marks as a share of fields: so a
    newly found place with a wrong person is not read as a regression of the person rule.
