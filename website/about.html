Who We Are

Built around the people who invest with you.

Carrow & Vale Shareholder Advisors works with real estate sponsors and private fund managers. We take on the work that sits between a sponsor and its investors: raising capital, keeping investors informed, and handling every question, call and distribution along the way.

Our securities professionals hold FINRA Series 7 and Series 63 registrations. They present offerings and answer investment questions directly, so your investors deal with qualified people from first conversation to final distribution.

Series 7
General Securities Representative registration, held by the professionals who present your offerings.
