Who We Are

Built around the people who invest with you.

Carrow & Vale Shareholder Advisors works with private placement sponsors and fund managers. We take on the work that sits between a sponsor and its investors: raising capital, keeping investors informed, and handling every question, call and distribution along the way.

What We Bring

Experience across the whole of a private offering.

Our team has raised capital, structured offerings and served investors across the channels and products private placement sponsors depend on.

I

The full life of a transaction

II

Our Founder

Brady Smith

Brady Smith founded Carrow & Vale. His career spans capital raising, investment banking and distribution, and the full life of a transaction, from deal origination and due diligence through structuring, investor positioning and placement.

Brady has led capital markets activity for a real estate investment trust and Delaware Statutory Trust sponsor, consulting on private placement memorandums, building broker-dealer selling groups and managing relationships with financial advisors, broker-dealers and registered investment advisers. He has overseen the structuring and distribution of private securities offerings and has distributed a broad range of alternative investments, including Regulation D and Regulation A offerings, REITs, preferred equity, private equity and oil and gas programs, across the RIA and independent broker-dealer channels.

Earlier in his career, Brady held distribution roles at Franklin Templeton and Amundi Asset Management, working with financial advisors across the wirehouse, independent and RIA channels on mutual funds, separately managed accounts, ETFs and alternative strategies. He also brings experience in alternative finance.

Brady holds the Certified ETF Advisor (CETF®) designation from The ETF Institute and FINRA Series 7 and 63 licenses.
