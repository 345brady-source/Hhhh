Brady Smith

Founder

Brady Smith founded Carrow & Vale after five years raising capital and serving investors across private placements and public funds, on both sides of the distribution desk.

He began at Franklin Templeton Investments, supporting financial advisors from every channel in the country on alternatives, ETFs, mutual funds and separately managed accounts, and raising $6.3 million directly from the inbound advisor line. At Amundi Asset Management he covered the Southern California wirehouse and RIA channels across a territory of $780 million in mutual fund and SMA assets.

He went on to lead capital markets for a REIT and Delaware Statutory Trust sponsor: building its broker-dealer selling group, managing every RIA and broker-dealer relationship, consulting on PPMs and private securities creation, and personally handling investor relations at 100 to 130 inbound investor calls a week. As Vice President for the Northeast at UWC Private Equity Group, he leads external distribution of Rule 506(c), Regulation A and Regulation D offerings, REITs, preferred equity, private equity and oil and gas programs through the RIA channel.

Today he is Managing Director of Capital Markets at Block Equity Group, overseeing the structuring, creation and distribution of private securities offerings from origination through close. Carrow & Vale brings that same work, and the people who do it, to every sponsor it serves.
