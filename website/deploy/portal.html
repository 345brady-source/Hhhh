What Is Included

Fund administration and investor relations, end to end.

Fifteen working areas, in the order an offering runs, and an investor app for iPhone and Android.

I

Offering Intake

Upload the offering documents. The portal reads them and sets up the offering for you to review.

  • Reads your documents. PPMs, operating and partnership agreements, term sheets and subscription documents, in PDF, Word or text.
  • Pulls out the terms. Offering name and type, minimum investment, target raise, first close, share and unit classes, management fee and its basis, fee step-downs, carried interest, preferred return, other fees, and distribution frequency, type and target.
  • Builds the waterfall. Return of capital, preferred return, catch-up and profit splits, tier by tier, in order.
  • Shows its sources. Every term comes with the words it was read from and the page number, so you check it in seconds.
  • Writes the investor summary. Six to twelve key terms in plain words for the investment page.
  • Finds the subscription agreement among the documents and hands it straight to e-signature.
  • Nothing applies until you approve it. Review, correct and apply in one step.
  • One upload for the PPM and the subscription agreement together
II

Offerings & Fund Structures

  • Every kind of offering. Funds, single-asset syndications and multi-strategy firms under one account.
  • Exemptions. Rule 506(b), Rule 506(c) and Regulation A set per offering, and the onboarding questions follow it.
  • Complex structures. Waterfalls, multiple share classes, currencies, master-feeder, side pockets and equalization.
  • Commitments. Committed, called and funded amounts per investor and per offering.
  • Investment pages. Photographs, terms and documents for each offering, in your brand.
  • Investor requests. Existing investors ask to subscribe more or redeem from their portal.
III

Investor Onboarding

  • Invite in seconds. A first name, last name and email, one at a time, added by hand or uploaded from a CSV or Excel file.
  • One link, every step. Profile, investor status, tax form, verification, signing and bank details, on any phone or computer, picking up where the investor left off.
  • Official IRS W-9 and W-8, filled and e-signed inside onboarding and delivered to you.
  • Investor-status questions matched to the exemption, with the Rule 506(b) non-accredited count tracked against 35, Regulation A Tier 2 investment-limit flags, and the ERISA 25 percent benefit-plan test per class.
  • Joint holders and co-investors on the same investment.
  • Existing investors brought in without starting over.
  • You are notified when each investor finishes, and the investor is fully added with nothing left to key in.
IV

E-Signature

  • Your own agreement. Upload the subscription agreement you already use; no template to rebuild.
  • Fields placed for you. The portal finds every printed blank, tick box and signature line and puts the right field on each, for the right person: investor, joint holder or your firm.
  • Any format. Typed documents, filed PDFs, browser prints, scans and picture-only pages.
  • Honest about what it read. It tells you what it found and flags anything for a second look.
  • A drag-and-drop field editor for any change you want to make.
  • Real-looking signatures: typed in a choice of script styles, drawn, or uploaded, adopted once.
  • Signed inside onboarding, then countersigned by your firm in the console.
  • A signed record that stands alone: the final PDF with a certificate page, a full audit trail and tamper evidence.
  • No envelope fees, however many investors sign.
V

Verification & Compliance

  • The Carrow & Vale verification package. Identity, sanctions screening and accreditation, added to any invitation with one tick.
  • Investors never pay. They complete their checks inside onboarding; the package is charged to your card on file.
  • Ongoing screening. Package investors are screened continuously; every other investor is re-screened against the U.S. Treasury lists when due.
  • 506(c) accreditation kept current, with a one-click request for an investor to re-verify.
  • An AML program on record, exportable for an examiner, plus a complete verification record per investor.
VI

Capital Calls & Distributions

  • Capital calls allocated pro rata to commitments, with notices, due dates and payment tracking.
  • Distributions with the general partner's share and tax withheld, storing gross, withheld and net for every investor.
  • Redemptions requested by investors and processed in the console.
  • NACHA payment files for your own bank, with bank holidays for any year, same-day limit warnings, one file per payment run and a record of every file released.
  • Pre-notes to test new bank accounts before money moves.
  • Bank returns and change notices read from your bank's file or report, and posted back to the books.
  • Your money stays in your bank. No payment provider, no per-payment fee; Carrow & Vale never holds funds.
  • Returns matched to the investor and applied in one step
  • Pre-note and payment-file screens showing who is in each file and who is held back, and why
  • Foreign investors paid by wire, with their own wire details
  • Redemption payment files and a wires list
VII

Fund Accounting

  • A double-entry journal per fund, in whole cents, posted once from every call payment, subscription, distribution, redemption and fee. Nothing is entered twice.
  • Bank reconciliation from your statement file, with a decision for each unmatched line.
  • Capital accounts for every investor, tied to the journal every month.
  • Fair values entered per investment at a date, and opening balances for an existing fund.
  • Trial balance, general ledger, cash flows and financial highlights.
  • Month-end close with the trial balance fingerprinted.
  • Audit schedules, automated checks and a full change log, downloadable as one audit package.
  • The CPA year-end package: one click gives your accountant a workbook and a PDF holding everything for the return.
VIII

Report Builder

  • Templates for every report: investor statements, capital account statements, distribution notices, quarterly and annual letters, fund performance and holdings summaries, and tax-season summaries.
  • Built from your figures. Capital accounts, journal, distributions, calls, holdings and tax documents flow in; nothing is retyped.
  • Yours to shape: turn sections on or off, reorder them, add your own wording, set the date range, report per fund or per investor.
  • Preview on screen, then export as PDF and spreadsheet in your brand.
  • Delivered to each investor's documents, with a record of what went to whom.
IX

Tax Documents & Withholding

  • K-1s. Import the file your CPA prepares; the portal splits it by investor, proposes each match, and delivers only what you confirm.
  • Electronic delivery under the IRS consent, notice and access rules, with opens tracked and a list of investors who want paper.
  • Forms 1042-S and 8805 delivered the same way, with a worksheet for your CPA.
  • Forms 1099-INT and 1099-DIV filled from stored payments, delivered as the official Copy B, and written to a file you upload to the IRS IRIS site.
  • Withholding: 24 percent backup withholding and 30 percent for foreign investors proposed where the IRS rule is mechanical, and confirmed line by line.
  • Full taxpayer numbers are never stored.
X

Filings

  • A Filings tab with every date: the first Form D within 15 days of the first sale, the annual amendment, and state notices and renewals, each reminder silenceable.
  • Form D prepared from what the offering already holds, reviewed and signed off in the portal.
  • Step-by-step EDGAR access.
  • Filing by our team. Order the Form D filing or a state notice filing; our staff file it and the tab shows the accession number and the next annual date.
XI

Investor Communications

  • Every email in your brand, sent from your own domain, with guidance for SPF, DKIM and DMARC so it lands in the inbox.
  • Clear messages: the investor's name, one action per email, plain-text and HTML, built for phones.
  • A preview of every message in the console and a sent log per investor.
  • Notification preferences and unsubscribe where the law requires them.
  • No taxpayer, password or bank numbers ever sent by email.
  • Secure messaging inside the portal: threads per investor and offering, attachments, read status and broadcasts to all investors
  • A data room for prospects: NDA-gated documents, watermarking, view tracking and soft commitments
XII

Ask Me Anything

  • A help assistant on every page for your team and your investors, in the search field at the top of the screen.
  • Short, correct answers drawn from the portal's own guide for the page the person is on.
  • Stays in its lane: no investment, legal or tax advice, no personal data repeated.
  • Says so when it cannot answer, and shows your support contact.
XIII

Your Brand & the Investor's Portal

  • Your firm on every screen: logo, colors and typeface, with your web address and email domain.
  • Your photographs of each project on the investor's home page and every investment page.
  • The investor's portal: home, investments, distributions, documents, updates and account, with capital calls due, statements and tax documents in one place.
  • Works on any phone, tablet or computer, with native iPhone and Android apps for investors.
  • A library of 24 original marks for your firm, in the interface shown on this page
XIV

CRM: HubSpot

  • Connect your own HubSpot account from the console's Integrations page.
  • Investors and co-investors become contacts; commitments become deals.
  • Onboarding progress flows through: invited, signed, accredited and funded.
  • Invite a HubSpot lead to an offering from the console.
  • No taxpayer or bank number ever leaves for the CRM, and a CSV export covers any other CRM.
XV

Your Team, Security & Integrations

  • Self-serve sign-up with a setup checklist that walks you from account to first invitation.
  • Team roles for administrators, team members and accountants; team members see masked bank accounts and cannot download payment files.
  • Two-factor sign-in for your team, session protection and a working password reset.

  • Taxpayer and bank numbers encrypted at rest, backed up, and kept only as long as needed.
  • An integration server to connect the other systems you use.
