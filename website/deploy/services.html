Services

Investor relations and securities distribution, from A to Z.

Everything a private placement sponsor needs, from creating the offering to answering the investor years after the close. Take a single service or hand us all of it.

What We Provide

Ten services, one firm, one standard.

Each is carried out in your firm's name, documented, and reported back to your team. They work on their own or together, and every one runs on our fund portal.

I

Product Creation & Structuring

We help you build an offering that investors, advisors and broker-dealers can say yes to.

  • Product creation: shaping the vehicle, the share or unit classes, minimums, fees, preferred return and waterfall around your strategy and the investors you want to reach.
  • Exemption strategy: Rule 506(b), Rule 506(c) or Regulation A, chosen for how and to whom you intend to raise.
  • Private placement memorandum consultation: working alongside your counsel so the PPM, subscription agreement and operating documents read the way investors and due diligence teams expect.
  • Due diligence readiness: the materials, data and answers a broker-dealer or RIA due diligence review will ask for, prepared before they ask.
II

Distribution Consultation

A plan for who will sell the offering, to whom, and on what terms.

  • Channel strategy: your own investors, registered investment advisers, independent broker-dealers, or a combination.
  • Selling group design: selling agreements, dealer compensation and due diligence fees structured so the offering is attractive to sell.
  • Positioning: how the offering is described, priced and compared against what advisors already have on their shelf.
  • Raise planning: targets, timelines, closings and the reporting cadence that will follow.
III

Licensed Representatives

FINRA Series 7 and Series 63 registered representatives working your raise.

  • Solicitation of your clients and prospects: our representatives present your offerings to your existing investors and to the prospects you introduce.
  • Investor advice: investors speak with qualified, registered people about the offering and whether it fits them.
  • Follow-on raises: new offerings and additional closings presented to the investors you already have.
  • Through the close: questions answered, subscriptions completed and commitments funded.
IV

Broker-Dealer Introductions

Access to the firms that bring private placements to their advisors.

  • Introductions to independent broker-dealers and RIAs suited to your offering.
  • Selling group building: onboarding firms into your selling group and keeping each relationship active.
  • Due diligence support: coordinating reviews, data requests and site visits.
  • Advisor engagement: offering presentations, updates and answers for the advisors who sell your offering.
V

Marketing Creation

Investor-ready materials produced in your brand.

  • Offering materials: pitch decks, one-pagers, offering summaries and fact sheets.
  • Ongoing communications: fund updates, investor letters, notices and email campaigns.
  • Events: annual meetings, webinars and investor events, planned and run.
  • Press: announcements and media relations when your firm has news worth sharing.
VI

KYC, AML, OFAC & Accredited Verification

Every investor verified, screened and documented before the first dollar moves.

  • Know-your-customer: identity verification for every investor and every person behind an entity investor.
  • AML and OFAC: sanctions and watch-list screening at onboarding, then ongoing re-screening.
  • Accredited investor verification: the reasonable steps Rule 506(c) requires, documented and kept current.
  • A complete record: an AML program and a verification file per investor, ready for an examiner.
VII

Form D & State Filings

Your filings prepared, filed and tracked, so no deadline slips.

  • Form D prepared from your offering and filed on EDGAR within 15 days of the first sale.
  • Annual amendments filed on time, every year the offering continues.
  • State blue sky notices and renewals in every state where you raise.
  • EDGAR access set up for your firm, with every filing and accession number on record.
VIII

Investor Relations Support & Management

Your investors looked after by people who know them by name, in your firm's name.

  • Dedicated phone line answered in your firm's name.
  • Investor relations email managed and answered promptly.
  • Onboarding help: investors guided through activation, signing, tax forms and bank details.
  • Capital calls and distributions: notices, follow-up and payment processing.
  • Transfers and redemptions processed and documented.
  • Tax season: K-1 delivery and the questions that come with it.
  • Every interaction logged and reported back to your team.
IX

Quarterly & Annual Reporting

Reports your investors read and trust, delivered on schedule.

  • Quarterly reports and investor letters.
  • Annual reports and year-end summaries.
  • Account and capital account statements for every investor.
  • Distribution notices and tax documents, delivered to each investor's portal.
X

  • Included with full service: your investors get their own portal and app; your team sees everything we do.
  • Or run it yourself: the complete do-it-yourself fund administration and investor relations portal, $225 per month.
