FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How we work with private placement sponsors, and how the Fund Portal works for the sponsors who run it themselves.

The Firm

What does Carrow & Vale do?

Carrow & Vale Shareholder Advisors is a financial services firm for private placement sponsors and fund managers. We handle investor relations and securities distribution from start to finish: product creation and structuring, distribution consultation, licensed representatives, broker-dealer introductions, marketing, KYC, AML and accredited investor verification, Form D and state filings, investor support, and quarterly and annual reporting.

Who do you work with?

Sponsors and managers raising capital through private placements, including real estate syndications, REITs, Delaware Statutory Trusts, private equity, private credit, preferred equity and oil and gas programs, offered under Rule 506(b), Rule 506(c) or Regulation A.

Can we hire you for a single service?

Yes. Engage us for one service, such as Form D filings or quarterly reporting, or hand us the whole investor relations and distribution function. Each service works on its own or together with the others.

Do you work under our firm's name?

Yes. Investor support, emails, reports and the investor portal all carry your firm's name and branding. Your investors deal with your firm; we work behind it and report everything back to your team.

Do your representatives hold securities licenses?

Yes. Our representatives hold FINRA Series 7 and Series 63 registrations. They present offerings to your investors and prospects and answer their investment questions directly.

Can you introduce us to broker-dealers and RIAs?

Yes. We introduce sponsors to independent broker-dealers and registered investment advisers suited to their offering, help build and onboard the selling group, and coordinate due diligence reviews.

Do you prepare and file Form D?

Yes. We prepare Form D from your offering details and file it on EDGAR within 15 days of the first sale, file the annual amendments, and track and file state blue sky notices and renewals in every state where you raise.

How do you handle KYC, AML, OFAC and accredited investor verification?

Every investor is identity-verified and screened against sanctions and watch lists, including OFAC, at onboarding and re-screened over time. For Rule 506(c) offerings we take and document the reasonable steps required to verify accredited status, and we keep a complete verification record for each investor.

What reports do you prepare for investors?

Quarterly reports and investor letters, annual reports, account and capital account statements, distribution notices and tax documents such as K-1s and 1099s, each delivered to the investor's own portal.

The Fund Portal

What is the Carrow & Vale Fund Portal?

A complete do-it-yourself fund administration and investor relations portal. It covers offering intake, investor onboarding, e-signature, verification, capital calls and distributions, fund accounting, reporting, tax documents and filings, with a branded investor portal and an iPhone and Android app.

How much does the Fund Portal cost?

The Fund Portal is $225 per month. The optional identity, sanctions and accreditation verification package is charged per investor to your card on file; your investors never pay for it.

Will our investors see Carrow & Vale?

No. The investor portal, the app and every email carry your firm's logo, colors and web address. Your investors see your firm.

Is there a mobile app for investors?

Yes. Investors get a native app for iPhone and Android that opens straight into your firm's account, with their investments, distributions, documents and account details, protected by a six-digit sign-in code and Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint unlock.

Does Carrow & Vale hold investor funds?

No. Capital calls and distributions run through your own bank. The portal prepares NACHA payment files that you upload to your bank, so there is no payment provider and no per-payment fee.

Which offerings and fund structures does the portal support?

Funds, single-asset syndications and multi-strategy firms under Rule 506(b), Rule 506(c) or Regulation A, including multiple share classes, waterfalls with preferred returns and catch-ups, currencies, master-feeder structures, side pockets and equalization.

Can we move from the portal to full service later?

Yes. The portal is the same platform our team uses for full-service clients. When you want help, our professionals take over inside the same portal, with the same investors and records, so there is nothing to move or rebuild.

How do we get started?

Contact us through the contact page or call (917) 837-7427. We will learn about your firm and your offering and recommend the services, or the portal, that fit.
