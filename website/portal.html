What Is Included

From the first invitation to the year-end package.

Twelve working areas, built around how an offering actually runs. Nothing below is an extra module.

I

Offerings & Fundraising

  • Funds, single-asset deals and multi-strategy firms under one account
  • Upload a PPM or operating agreement and the portal reads out the terms for you to review
  • Exemption on every offering: 506(b), 506(c) or Regulation A
  • Commitments and amounts funded tracked per investor
  • Subscription requests from existing investors
II

Investor Onboarding

  • Invite with a first name, last name and email, one at a time or from a CSV or Excel file
  • One link takes each investor through every step, on any device
  • W-9 or W-8 collected in the flow
  • Investor-status questions, with the 506(b) non-accredited limit of 35 counted for you
  • Joint holders and co-investors on the same account
  • You are notified the moment an investor finishes
III

E-Signature

  • Upload your own subscription agreement; signature, initial, date and fill-in fields are placed on its lines automatically
  • Drag-and-drop field editor for anything you want to adjust
  • Typed, drawn or uploaded signatures, adopted once
  • Signed inside onboarding, then countersigned by your firm
  • Final PDF with a certificate page, audit trail and tamper evidence
  • No envelope fees
IV

Verification & Compliance

  • Carrow & Vale verification package: identity, sanctions screening and accreditation, added to any invitation with one tick
  • Investors complete their checks inside onboarding and never pay for them
  • Package investors screened continuously; everyone else re-screened against U.S. Treasury lists when due
  • 506(c) accreditation kept current
  • A written AML program and a complete verification record per investor
V

Capital Calls, Distributions & Payments

  • Capital calls allocated pro rata, with notices and payment tracking
  • Distributions with the general partner's share and tax withheld
  • Redemption requests and redemption payments
  • NACHA payment files for your own bank, with pre-notes
  • International investors paid by IAT or wire
  • Bank return and change files read back and matched to the investor
  • Funds move through your bank; nobody else holds them
VI

Fund Accounting

  • A double-entry journal per fund, in whole cents, posted automatically from every call, subscription, distribution, redemption and fee
  • Bank reconciliation from your statement file
  • Capital accounts for every investor
  • Waterfalls, share classes, currencies, master-feeder structures, side pockets and equalization
  • Audit schedules, a full change log and an audit package to download
  • A year-end package for your CPA
VII

Reporting

  • Templates for investor statements, capital account statements, distribution notices, quarterly and annual letters, performance and holdings summaries
  • Built from the figures already in the portal, so nothing is typed twice
  • Delivered as PDF and Excel, in your brand
  • Published to each investor's documents, with a record of what went to whom
VIII

Tax Documents

  • Import the K-1 file your CPA prepares; the portal splits it by investor and proposes each match for you to confirm
  • Electronic delivery under the IRS consent and notice rules, with opens tracked and a list for paper copies
  • 1099s prepared
  • Backup and foreign withholding handled on distributions
IX

Filings

  • Form D prepared from the offering and investor data you already have
  • State blue sky notices tracked for every state where you raise
  • Deadlines and filing status in one place
X

Investor Communications

  • Every email sent from your own domain, in your branding
  • Invitations, reminders, notices and reports, each with one clear action
  • Notification preferences and a full sent log
  • An "Ask me anything" assistant on every page for investors and your team
XI

The Investor's Portal

  • Home, investments, distributions, documents, updates and account, in your firm's look
  • Photographs of your projects on the home page and every offering
  • Capital calls due, statements and tax documents in one place
  • Works on any phone, tablet or computer
XII

Security & Your Team

  • Two-factor sign-in for your team
  • Roles for administrators, team members and accountants
  • An audit log of every action
  • Taxpayer and bank numbers encrypted at rest and backed up
